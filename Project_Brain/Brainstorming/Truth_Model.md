# Purpose

This document explores how truth, knowledge and belief may function within Project Ascension.

It is an early design concept and is not yet part of Canon.

The purpose is to investigate how different individuals, groups and systems can hold different understandings of the same reality.

---

# Core Idea

The world contains one objective reality.

Every person experiences only a part of that reality.

Knowledge is therefore always incomplete.

Beliefs are shaped by experience, trust, fear, culture and available information.

---

# Proposed Layers of Truth

## Objective Reality

What actually happened.

Objective Reality exists independently of whether anyone knows it.

---

## Institutional Reality

What governments, organisations and institutions believe or officially communicate.

Institutions may possess incomplete or intentionally distorted information.

---

## Aurora's Understanding

Aurora continuously builds a model of reality using available information.

Although vastly more capable than humans, Aurora is not omniscient.

Its understanding evolves as new information becomes available.

---

## Faction Reality

Every faction develops its own interpretation of events.

Shared ideology often shapes which information is accepted or rejected.

---

## Personal Reality

Each individual builds a personal understanding of the world based upon:

- experiences
- relationships
- education
- trauma
- trust
- available information

No two people experience the world in exactly the same way.

---

## Unknown Reality

Some truths remain unknown.

They may never be discovered.

Others may only become visible many years later.

Unknown Reality creates mystery and uncertainty within the Living World.

---

# Open Questions

Examples of questions still requiring exploration:

- How do rumours spread?
- How should misinformation evolve?
- Can truth disappear?
- Can historical events become distorted over time?
- How much does Aurora actually know?
- Can two contradictory beliefs both influence the world?
