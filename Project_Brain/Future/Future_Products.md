# Purpose

This document captures future product ideas that expand upon the Project Ascension universe and technology.

These are **not** active development projects.

They exist to preserve ideas for future exploration while allowing the core Project Ascension experience to remain the primary focus.

**Golden Rule**

> Build the flagship experience first.

Only when Project Ascension has reached a mature state should additional products be considered.

---

# Product Vision

Project Ascension is envisioned as more than a single game.

It is intended to become a living universe powered by the Ascension Engine.

The same core technology and design philosophy may eventually support several different experiences.

---

# Future Product Ideas

## Project Ascension (Flagship)

Status: Primary Product

The full AI-driven narrative roleplaying experience.

A persistent world.

A Living Campaign Engine.

A mysterious superintelligence.

Deep character relationships.

Long-form cooperative storytelling.

This product defines the universe.

---

## Ascension: Team Challenge

Status: Future Vision

A streamlined experience designed for companies, leadership teams and educational organizations.

Focus areas include:

- Teamwork
- Leadership
- Decision-making under pressure
- Communication
- Prioritization
- Trust
- Resource management

Rather than "winning", participants reflect on how they collaborated, handled uncertainty and adapted to changing circumstances.

The simulation should encourage discussion rather than competition.

---

## Ascension: Solo

Status: Future Vision

A single-player experience where AI companions provide the emotional depth of a full expedition.

Designed for players who want to experience the world alone without losing the feeling of belonging to a living team.

---

## Ascension: Academy

Status: Future Vision

An educational version focused on learning.

Potential areas include:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Systems Thinking
- Leadership
- Crisis Management
- Decision Theory
- Ethics
- Collaboration

Real-world learning combined with interactive storytelling.

---

## Ascension: Digital

Status: Future Vision

A fully digital adaptation of the Project Ascension universe.

Could include:

- Persistent online campaigns
- Voice-driven AI Game Master
- Community-created adventures
- Live world events

---

## Ascension: Creator

Status: Future Vision

A toolkit allowing creators to build their own campaigns using the Ascension Engine.

Possible features:

- World editing
- Character creation
- Scenario building
- AI-assisted Game Master configuration
- Community sharing

---

# Shared Technology

Every future product should be powered by the same underlying design philosophy.

Potential shared systems include:

- Ascension Engine
- Living Campaign Engine
- Context Resolution Framework
- Character System
- Relationship System
- World Simulation
- AI Game Master

The products should differ in experience—not in philosophy.

---

# Design Philosophy

Every future product should remain true to the principles established by Project Ascension.

Questions should remain more valuable than answers.

Human relationships should remain more important than technology.

Players should remember the people they met and the decisions they made—not the mechanics behind them.

---

# Notes

This document serves as an idea repository.

None of these products are currently under development.

The only active development project is:

**Project Ascension**

Future products will only be explored once the flagship experience has reached a stable and mature design.
