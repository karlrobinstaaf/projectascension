# 1. Purpose

Republic already contains:

```text
REAL GEOGRAPHY

+

OPENING WORLD STATE

+

PEOPLE

+

PRESSURES.
```

The first Seeds now introduce:

```text
POSSIBILITY.
```

They do not introduce a story the players are required to follow.

> **Seeds create possibility. Actions create history.**

---

# 2. Seed Philosophy

The first Seed set should deliberately produce different kinds of uncertainty.

The players should not learn:

```text
STRANGE
=
IMPORTANT

OR

STRANGE
=
DANGEROUS.
```

Instead:

```text
STRANGE
MAY BE

NOTHING

COINCIDENCE

MISUNDERSTANDING

INFRASTRUCTURE

HUMAN BEHAVIOR

DECEPTION

CRIME

OR

A SMALL PART
OF SOMETHING
MUCH LARGER.
```

---

# 3. Uncanny Principle

Project Ascension may present events that initially feel supernatural.

This does not require supernatural World Truth.

> **The Game Master may use supernatural framing, but should not rely on supernatural causality.**

The purpose is not to trick players cheaply.

The purpose is to reproduce a very human experience:

```text
INCOMPLETE
INFORMATION

+

FEAR

+

PATTERN
RECOGNITION

=

EXPLANATION.
```

Humans explain uncertainty.

Sometimes incorrectly.

---

# 4. The Rational Horror Principle

Some uncanny events should eventually receive mundane explanations.

Others may reveal something considerably darker.

> **Sometimes the rational explanation should make the players wish it had been a ghost.**

This is an important mystery principle for Project Ascension.

---

# 5. World Truth Must Exist

For every authored Seed, the Game Master must know its underlying World Truth.

The GM cannot wait for player theories and then select the most dramatic explanation.

```text
WORLD TRUTH

EXISTS

BEFORE

PLAYER
INTERPRETATION.
```

Players may never discover it.

That does not make it undefined.

---

# 7. SEED 001 — The Silent Repeater

## Classification

```text
ORIGIN:
World / Infrastructure

STATE:
Stirring

VISIBILITY:
Low

GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE:
Republic Region
```

---

## Core Possibility

A communication repeater serving part of the surrounding region has stopped behaving normally.

The immediate assumption is that it failed during the broader communication disruption.

That assumption is only partly correct.

---

## World Truth

The repeater experienced a power interruption.

Backup power activated.

It should therefore still be capable of operating.

However, a secondary technical fault occurred during repeated power cycling.

The equipment remains physically present.

No sabotage has occurred.

No mysterious Actor caused the failure.

---

## Why It Matters

The failure contributes to Republic's informational isolation.

If investigated and repaired, limited communication capability may potentially be restored.

If ignored, it remains unavailable.

---

## Player Visibility

Players are not initially told:

```text
GO FIX
THE REPEATER.
```

They may encounter the Seed through:

- comments about lost radio coverage
- law-enforcement discussion
- a technician reference
- inconsistent communication range
- Character Expertise

---

## Expertise Interaction

A Character with relevant technical Expertise may recognize:

> The reported failure pattern does not sound like complete infrastructure destruction.

This does not reveal the fault.

It creates a better question.

---

## If Ignored

The repeater remains non-functional.

Other people may eventually investigate it.

Or they may not.

---

# 8. SEED 002 — The Voice in the Trees

## Classification

```text
ORIGIN:
Local Environment

TYPE:
Uncanny / Benign

STATE:
Dormant

VISIBILITY:
Very Low

GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE:
Forested area outside Republic
```

---

## Surface

Several locals have independently heard what sounds like:

```text
A HUMAN
VOICE

CALLING

FROM THE
FOREST.
```

Descriptions differ.

Some say it sounded like a woman.

Another says a child.

Someone claims it said a name.

---

## Local Interpretation

Possible rumors include:

```text
LOST PERSON

INJURED
HIKER

SOMEONE
PLAYING
A JOKE

ANIMAL

OR

SOMETHING
STRANGER.
```

Walt Givens dismisses some versions of the story.

That does not mean he knows the answer.

---

## World Truth

The sound is produced by a combination of:

- terrain
- wind
- distant human activity
- distorted sound propagation through the valley and forest

At least one source is an ordinary human voice from considerably farther away than listeners assume.

There is no person secretly calling from the nearby trees.

There is no crime.

There is no supernatural cause.

---

## Why This Seed Exists

This Seed teaches the players:

> **Not every mystery contains a hidden conspiracy.**

If investigated thoroughly, the answer may be disappointing.

That is intentional.

---

## Important Rule

Do not reward investigation by secretly adding something sinister.

If the World Truth is mundane:

```text
LET IT
BE MUNDANE.
```

---

## If Ignored

Nothing happens.

The sound may occur again under similar conditions.

Local stories may become increasingly exaggerated.

---

# 9. SEED 003 — The House With Power

## Classification

```text
ORIGIN:
Human

TYPE:
Uncanny / Human Darkness

STATE:
Stirring

VISIBILITY:
Low

GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE:
Republic outskirts
```

---

## Surface

During a period when nearby properties are dark, an isolated building outside the denser part of Republic appears to have power.

Players may observe:

```text
A LIGHT

THEN

DARKNESS

THEN

LIGHT
AGAIN.
```

No obvious vehicle is visible from the road.

The building appears quiet.

---

## Initial Interpretations

Possible player theories:

```text
GENERATOR

ELECTRICAL
ANOMALY

ABANDONED
HOUSE

SOMEONE
HIDING

EMERGENCY
FACILITY

SIGNAL

OR

SOMETHING
SUPERNATURAL.
```

The GM confirms none of them.

---

## World Truth

The building has an independent generator.

The generator itself is completely ordinary.

What is happening inside is not.

The property is being used by a small number of people involved in an existing criminal activity that predates the communications crisis.

The outage did not create them.

It created opportunity.

---

## Nature of the Activity

At VS-001 resolution, the exact criminal activity should remain narrow and plausible.

Recommended direction:

```text
STOLEN
PROPERTY

+

VEHICLE /
EQUIPMENT
STRIPPING

+

STORAGE OF
ITEMS THAT
SHOULD NOT
BE THERE.
```

The group has realized that degraded communications and reduced police visibility make detection less likely.

---

## The Darker Discovery

The unsettling element is not the generator.

It is evidence that:

```text
THESE PEOPLE
WERE OPERATING

BEFORE

THE CURRENT
CRISIS.
```

The players may initially believe they discovered something caused by the collapse.

Instead they discover something that ordinary society had already failed to see.

---

## Escalation Potential

If players investigate aggressively:

- occupants may notice them
- evidence may be moved
- a vehicle may leave
- law enforcement may become involved
- players may be mistaken for witnesses or threats

None is guaranteed.

---

## Important Boundary

The occupants are criminals.

They are not automatically murderers.

Escalation follows circumstances.

Do not increase their evil merely because players investigate.

---

## If Ignored

The activity continues.

The perpetrators may exploit worsening conditions.

Or law enforcement may eventually discover them independently.

---

# 10. SEED 004 — The Spokane Broadcast

## Classification

```text
ORIGIN:
Information / Wider World

STATE:
Active

VISIBILITY:
Medium

GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE:
Regional / Unknown
```

---

## Surface

An intermittent radio broadcast appears to originate from the Spokane direction.

The transmission contains fragments of useful information.

Then it repeats.

Later another fragment appears to contradict part of the first.

---

## Example Information Pattern

```text
"...residents are advised
to remain..."

STATIC

"...no indication at this
time that..."

STATIC

"...transportation corridors..."

SIGNAL LOST.
```

The exact wording should vary during play.

---

## World Truth

Multiple transmissions are being received under changing conditions.

Some are:

- current
- delayed
- repeated automatically
- partially recorded
- rebroadcast

The players are not always hearing information in chronological order.

---

## Why It Feels Wrong

Human listeners naturally assume:

```text
WHAT I HEAR
NOW

WAS SAID
NOW.
```

That assumption is false.

---

## Gameplay Function

The Seed introduces:

```text
INFORMATION
PROVENANCE

+

TIMESTAMP
UNCERTAINTY

+

SOURCE
RELIABILITY.
```

Players may begin recording:

- time heard
- source
- wording
- apparent timestamp

This rewards careful information management.

---

## Wider Potential

One fragment may eventually provide the first credible evidence that the disruption extends far beyond Ferry County.

It should not explain the cause.

---

## If Ignored

The broadcasts continue intermittently.

Other Characters interpret them instead.

Rumors develop from incomplete fragments.

---

# 11. SEED 005 — Locals First

## Classification

```text
ORIGIN:
Human / Social

STATE:
Stirring

VISIBILITY:
Medium

PRIMARY ACTOR:
Dean Mercer

GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE:
Fuel / Republic community
```

---

## Surface

Fuel demand is increasing.

Dean Mercer begins considering purchase limits.

A question emerges:

```text
SHOULD
LOCAL RESIDENTS

RECEIVE
PRIORITY?
```

---

## World Truth

There is no secret fuel shortage at the opening.

There is limited local inventory and uncertainty regarding resupply.

Dean does not know when the next delivery will arrive.

His concern is rational.

His possible response may not be fair.

---

## Social Pressure

Several competing arguments exist:

```text
LOCALS
DEPEND ON
THIS FUEL.


TRAVELERS
MAY NEED
FUEL TO
GET HOME.


EMERGENCY
SERVICES
NEED
RESERVE.


EVERYONE
SHOULD BE
TREATED
EQUALLY.


FIRST COME,
FIRST SERVED.
```

There is no mechanically correct moral answer.

---

## Escalation

If conditions worsen:

```text
PURCHASE
LIMIT

↓

LOCAL
PRIORITY

↓

ARGUMENT

↓

SOCIAL
DIVISION

↓

POSSIBLE
AUTHORITY
INVOLVEMENT.
```

But escalation is not guaranteed.

---

## Player Role

Players may:

- support Dean
- oppose him
- ignore the issue
- negotiate
- leave
- become affected by the restriction
- create a different solution

The game does not judge their choice through morality points.

---

## If Ignored

Dean eventually makes his own decision.

Republic reacts.

# 12. SEED 006 — The Character Echo

## Classification

```text
ORIGIN:
Character Creation

STATE:
UNINITIALIZED

VISIBILITY:
Unknown

GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE:
Unknown
```

---

## Purpose

One Seed slot remains deliberately empty until player Characters exist.

This is essential.

Republic should not contain every meaningful possibility before the players are created.

---

## Generation Sources

Character Creation may expose:

- a person
- surname
- profession
- former employer
- institution
- memory
- fear
- unresolved relationship
- object
- place
- regret
- promise

One may naturally intersect with the opening world.

---

## Example

A Character previously worked with communications infrastructure.

They notice a company identifier associated with equipment in Republic.

That does **not** mean:

```text
THE COMPANY
CAUSED
THE CRISIS.
```

It means the Character possesses a connection to something in the world.

---

## Rule

> **Character Seeds create relevance, not destiny.**

---

# 13. Seed Independence

At opening:

```text
SEED 001

IS NOT
SECRETLY
CONNECTED TO

SEED 003.


SEED 002

DOES NOT
FORESHADOW

SEED 004.


SEED 005

IS NOT
CAUSED BY

SEED 001.
```

Do not connect everything.

---

# 14. Seed Collision

Independence does not prevent later collision.

Example:

```text
COMMUNICATION
REMAINS DOWN

↓

FUEL ANXIETY
INCREASES

↓

LAW ENFORCEMENT
BECOMES BUSIER

↓

CRIMINAL ACTIVITY
HAS MORE SPACE

↓

SEEDS 001,
003 AND 005

NOW INTERACT.
```

This connection emerges causally.

It was not secretly scripted.

---

# 15. Uncanny Presentation

Uncanny Seeds should be presented with restraint.

Avoid:

```text
HORROR
STING

CAMERA
ZOOM

DARK
MUSIC

"YOU FEEL
WATCHED."
```

Instead:

```text
"You hear something
through the trees."

"There is a light
in the building."

"The sound stops."

"The window is dark again."
```

Then stop.

Let the players create the fear.

---

# 16. Music Rule

Do not use music to confirm that an Uncanny Seed matters.

The same ambient music language may accompany:

```text
A REAL
THREAT

A FALSE
ALARM

OR

NOTHING.
```

Otherwise players learn to read the soundtrack instead of the world.

---

# 17. Visual Rule

Generated imagery must not reveal hidden World Truth.

If players see the House With Power from the road:

show:

```text
HOUSE

DARK
SURROUNDINGS

ONE LIGHT.
```

Do not show:

- ominous silhouette
- hidden criminal
- blood
- suspicious vehicle highlighted by composition

unless Characters can actually observe those things.

---

# 18. Pattern Exploitation

The Game Master should understand that players naturally build patterns.

After discovering one dark explanation, they may assume every strange event is dark.

After discovering one mundane explanation, they may become dismissive.

Both create future opportunities.

But the GM must never change World Truth merely to punish the pattern.

---

# 19. False Connections

Players may connect Seeds that are unrelated.

Example:

```text
VOICE
IN FOREST

+

HOUSE
WITH POWER

+

RADIO
FAILURE

=

"THEY'RE
CONNECTED."
```

The Game Master should not correct them.

The world simply continues according to actual causality.

---

# 20. Player Theories

The Field Menu may record:

```text
PLAYER THEORY:

"The house may be connected
to the strange voices."
```

This remains:

```text
PLAYER
THEORY

NOT

WORLD TRUTH.
```

---

# 21. Investigation Does Not Guarantee Importance

If players spend two hours investigating the Voice in the Trees, the GM must not invent a major payoff because of their investment.

They may discover:

```text
SOUND
PROPAGATION

+

ORDINARY
PEOPLE

+

NOTHING
MORE.
```

That is valid.

---

# 22. But Investigation Still Creates History

Even investigating a benign Seed can create consequences.

Players may:

- lose daylight
- consume fuel
- meet someone
- leave equipment behind
- become known locally
- miss another event
- discover geography

Therefore:

```text
SEED
WAS BENIGN

DOES NOT MEAN

PLAYER ACTION
WAS CONSEQUENCE-FREE.
```

---

# 23. Human Horror

Project Ascension should generally prefer:

```text
HUMAN
CAPABILITY

SYSTEM
FAILURE

MISUNDERSTANDING

INCENTIVES

FEAR

AND

OPPORTUNITY
```

over unexplained supernatural causality.

The world is frightening because it is plausible.

---

# 24. Do Not Overuse Darkness

The Rational Horror Principle becomes ineffective if every mystery ends badly.

Desired distribution over time:

```text
SOME:
MUNDANE

SOME:
FUNNY

SOME:
SAD

SOME:
MISUNDERSTOOD

SOME:
DANGEROUS

VERY FEW:
DEEPLY
DISTURBING.
```

Contrast creates surprise.

---

# 25. Humor Is Allowed

A strange Seed may even resolve humorously.

Project Ascension should retain ordinary humanity.

Fear becomes stronger when the world also contains:

- embarrassment
- absurdity
- kindness
- boredom
- humor

---

Seeds become playable when causality and player proximity permit.

```text

NEEDS
CONTENT.
```

If players spend the session talking to locals and planning:

that is content.

# 30. Seed Failure

A Seed can disappear without resolution.

Example:

The criminal group at the House With Power may decide conditions are becoming dangerous and leave.

Players later investigate.

They find:

```text
AN EMPTY
BUILDING

AND

TRACES OF
SOMETHING
THAT HAPPENED.
```

The world did not wait.

---

# 31. Missed Truth

Players are not entitled to every answer.

Some World Truth may never become Character Knowledge.

This is intentional.

---

# 32. Long-Term Echo

A Seed ignored in Republic may later reappear indirectly.

Example:

```text
A NAME

A VEHICLE

A RADIO
REPORT

A PERSON

A CONSEQUENCE.
```

But only if causality supports it.

Do not resurrect Seeds merely for dramatic callback.

# 33. Seed Density

```text

HAS A SECRET.

EVERY PERSON
IS LYING.

EVERY ROAD
HAS A MYSTERY.
```

That ordinary background makes the unusual powerful.

# 38. North Star

The players should not fear Republic because the Game Master tells them it is frightening.

They should fear it because they cannot tell which details matter.

A voice in the forest may be nothing.

A light in a dark house may be crime.

A sheriff may be unpleasant and correct.

A friendly person may be confidently wrong.

A broken radio may be nothing more than broken equipment.

A repeated broadcast may be the first evidence that the world outside Republic is changing.

And sometimes the strangest thing the players encounter will have the simplest explanation.

Sometimes the ordinary thing will hide the worst one.

> **Project Ascension does not ask the players to believe in monsters. It asks them to decide what they believe when they cannot see the whole world.**
