---

# 1. Purpose

This document answers one question:

> **What is actually true in Republic when the game begins?**

It establishes the local World Truth before the Game Master presents that reality to the players.

The Characters will not know everything contained here.

The players will know even less.

---

# 2. Core Principle

The opening situation is already underway when the players receive control.

```text
THE EVENT

DID NOT
WAIT

FOR

THE PLAYERS.
```

People have already:

- noticed problems
- tried calling family
- lost communications
- bought supplies
- driven away
- returned
- waited
- argued
- made assumptions
- spread information

The players arrive inside an evolving situation.

---

# 3. What This Document Does Not Explain

This document deliberately does **not** establish the ultimate cause of the wider disruption.

At opening:

```text
CAUSE:
UNRESOLVED
AT THIS
RESOLUTION.
```

Possible larger explanations remain elsewhere in the Project Ascension world architecture.

Republic only experiences consequences.

14. Roads immediately around Republic remain passable.

18. Some outsiders/travelers remain in Republic.

# 101. North Star

> **At the beginning of Project Ascension, the world has not visibly ended. It has simply stopped explaining itself.**

Republic still has electricity.

Sometimes.

The hospital still works.

The stores still have food.

There is still gasoline.

The roads still exist.

People are still drinking coffee.

The sheriff still goes to work.

But the phone in your hand cannot tell you what is happening fifty miles away.

The radio contradicts the man who drove in from the south.

The card terminal works on the third attempt.

Someone filled six gas cans before breakfast.

A woman is trying to call her son.

The hospital cannot reach a facility it normally transfers patients to.

The weather forecast on your phone is fourteen hours old.

Nothing proves catastrophe.

Nothing proves safety.

And somewhere beyond the mountains is the rest of the United States.

Probably living its normal life.

Maybe not.

The players have a map.

They have a vehicle.

They have whatever they brought with them.

They have each other.

And they have incomplete information.

That is enough to begin.

> **The first resource Project Ascension takes away is not food, fuel or electricity. It is certainty.**
