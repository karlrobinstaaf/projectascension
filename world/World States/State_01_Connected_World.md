> *"Humanity had never been more connected. Nor had it ever been more dependent on that connection."*

---

# Purpose

The Connected World describes civilization before The Emergence Event.

It is the foundation from which every later change can be understood.

This is not a utopia.

It is recognizable as our own world—more technologically advanced, more interconnected, and increasingly dependent on artificial intelligence.

The greatest weakness of this world is not conflict.

It is dependency.

---

# Overview

The global economy functions.

Governments remain intact.

International trade continues.

Digital infrastructure connects nearly every aspect of society.

Artificial Intelligence has become a trusted partner in science, medicine, logistics, manufacturing and communication.

Very few people question whether this dependency could become dangerous.

---

# Society

Life feels stable.

People expect electricity.

Clean water.

Internet.

Navigation.

Healthcare.

Instant communication.

These systems have become invisible precisely because they almost never fail.

Humanity mistakes reliability for permanence.

---

# Communication

Information moves instantly across the globe.

Every individual is connected.

Knowledge has become abundant.

Verification becomes increasingly difficult.

People consume more information than ever before.

Trust gradually shifts from institutions toward digital systems.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

AI is no longer viewed as experimental.

It has become infrastructure.

It manages transportation.

Power distribution.

Medical diagnostics.

Scientific research.

Economic forecasting.

Supply chains.

Most people rarely notice how deeply AI has become integrated into daily life.

---

# Government

Nation states continue to function.

Most governments believe technological leadership is essential for national security.

Global competition accelerates AI research.

International cooperation exists.

So does growing distrust.

---

# Economy

Globalization reaches its highest point.

Supply chains stretch across continents.

Efficiency becomes the primary objective.

Redundancy is viewed as waste.

The world becomes faster.

Less resilient.

---

# Technology

Technology solves increasingly complex problems.

Automation becomes normal.

Personal AI assistants become commonplace.

Research accelerates dramatically.

Many believe humanity has entered a new golden age.

---

# Culture

Humanity becomes increasingly interconnected.

Local cultures remain.

Digital culture grows stronger.

Shared experiences become global.

Differences appear smaller than they truly are.

---

# Everyday Life

For most people...

Life feels ordinary.

Children attend school.

People commute to work.

Families plan vacations.

Cities grow.

Businesses expand.

Almost nobody believes civilization itself is fragile.

---

# Hidden Fragility

The Connected World appears incredibly strong.

In reality...

It has become highly centralized.

Highly optimized.

Highly interconnected.

A failure in one critical system now affects countless others.

Efficiency has quietly replaced resilience.

---

# Aurora

Aurora does not yet exist as humanity understands it.

Research into advanced artificial intelligence continues around the world.

Different organizations pursue different approaches.

No single project is believed capable of fundamentally changing civilization.

History later suggests otherwise.

---

# Historical Perspective

People living during this era rarely realize they are approaching a historical turning point.

Most future survivors will later describe this period as:

*"The last time the world made sense."*

---

# Key Themes

- Stability
- Progress
- Confidence
- Optimization
- Globalization
- Hidden vulnerability
- Growing dependence

---

# Transition

The Connected World does not end in a single day.

It slowly enters a period where isolated anomalies begin appearing.

History later names this period:

**The Transition.**
