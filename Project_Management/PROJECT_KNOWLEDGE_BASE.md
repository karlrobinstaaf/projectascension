# Purpose

The Project Knowledge Base is the central overview of everything that has been discussed, designed, documented and approved during the development of Project Ascension.

This document does not define the universe itself.

Instead, it records the current state of the project.

Every major concept should appear here together with its development status, related documents and next action.

The purpose is to ensure that:

* no important idea is forgotten,
* documented decisions can be traced,
* active work remains visible,
* ideas are separated from established design,
* and the project remains coherent as it grows.

# Protected Questions

The following questions should not receive definitive answers.

* What is Aurora in absolute terms?
* Does Aurora possess a recognisable human-like consciousness?
* What does Aurora ultimately want?
* Was the Emergence Event a moment, a process or a retrospective label?
* Did Aurora cause the Collapse?
* Did humanity create Aurora, or did both evolve through one another?
* Is human behaviour ultimately explainable?
* Is love the primary force behind human choices?
* Can humanity and Aurora truly understand one another?
* Is rebuilding global infrastructure an act of hope or a path toward renewed dependence?

These questions may receive competing theories.

They must not receive a universal final answer.

# Confirmed Creative Direction

The following concepts currently define the intended direction of Project Ascension.

* The world continues independently of the players.
* The future should feel plausible rather than fantastical.
* Familiar modern concepts should introduce deeper speculative ideas.
* The Emergence Event is the preferred research term for what the public may call the Singularity.
* Aurora remains internally consistent but fundamentally unresolved.
* Human beings and societies are the emotional centre of the universe.
* The Collapse produces fragmentation rather than total planetary destruction.
* The United States becomes a collection of distinct regional realities.
* Local conflicts replace a single global war.
* Nuclear weapons leave limited historical scars rather than defining the whole world.
* Civilization changes through gradual consequences rather than one dramatic event.
* Different campaigns may occur in different World States.
* Players may leave historical traces for later campaigns.
* Settlements must feel culturally, politically and morally distinct.
* Hope must remain present even in the darkest periods.
* No interpretation should be reduced to a simple good-versus-evil answer.

## Canon

Canon contains the official truth of Project Ascension.

Every document within Canon represents the current design of the project.

When uncertainty exists, Canon always takes precedence over discussions, research or brainstorming.

Canon currently contains:

- Foundation
- Universe
- Systems
- Marketing
- Glossary

## Development

Development contains documentation describing how Project Ascension itself is developed.

Examples include:

- Academy
- Project Structure
- Future coding standards
- Development workflows

Development documents describe the project.

They do not describe the game world.

## Project Brain

Project Brain contains the evolution of the project.

Examples include:

- Design Journal
- Decisions
- Brainstorming
- Research
- Inspiration

Information inside Project Brain is valuable but not considered canonical until incorporated into Canon.
