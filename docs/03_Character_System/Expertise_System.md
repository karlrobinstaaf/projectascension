# Expertise System

## Levels

- 0 — No Knowledge
- 1 — Basic Knowledge
- 2 — Trained
- 3 — Experienced
- 4 — Expert
- 5 — World-Class

Expertise improves information, available actions and risk awareness. It never guarantees success.

## Categories

Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Engineering, Medicine, Science, Communication, Human Behavior, Military and Security, Survival, Transportation, Investigation and Logistics.

## Starting Distribution

- One profession
- One expertise at Level 4
- One expertise at Level 3
- Two expertise areas at Level 2
- One expertise at Level 1
- One rare specialization
- One weakness at Level 0
