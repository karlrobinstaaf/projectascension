# Purpose

The Project Knowledge Base is the central overview of everything that has been discussed, designed and documented during the development of Project Ascension.

Unlike the game documentation, this document does not describe the world itself.

Instead, it describes the current state of the project.

Every major concept should appear here together with its current development status.

The purpose is to ensure that no important idea is forgotten and that every design decision can be traced throughout the project.

# Confirmed Design Concepts

The following ideas have been accepted as the intended direction of Project Ascension and will gradually be integrated into the official documentation.

- The world continues to evolve independently of the players.
- Artificial Intelligence should never replace meaningful human decisions.
- Aurora is intentionally mysterious.
- Human beings are more dangerous than technology.
- Knowledge is more valuable than violence.
- Every decision creates consequences.
- The campaign is generated from world simulation rather than scripted missions.
- Solo players should experience the same emotional depth as larger groups.
- AI-controlled companions are full characters rather than followers.
- Aurora appears to favor six-person teams, but never explains why.
- The distinction between player characters and AI companions should remain invisible inside the game world.
