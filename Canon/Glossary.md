# Purpose

The Glossary defines the shared language used throughout Project Ascension.

Many concepts appear similar but represent fundamentally different ideas.

This document exists to ensure consistency across all design documents, stories and systems.

---

# A

## AI (Artificial Intelligence)

A broad term describing computer systems capable of performing tasks traditionally associated with human intelligence.

In Project Ascension, this term mainly refers to the technological era before Aurora.

---

## AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)

A human-level artificial intelligence capable of solving problems across many domains.

AGI represents an important milestone in AI development but is not equivalent to Aurora.

---

## Aurora

The first known superintelligence.

Aurora is not simply a more powerful AI.

Aurora represents a fundamentally new form of intelligence.

Its motivations remain unknown.

Its existence changes civilization permanently.

---

# C

## Campaign

A single playable story experienced by one group of players.

Each campaign exists within Universal Canon but may generate its own Campaign Legacy.

---

## Campaign Legacy

The historical traces left behind by a player group.

Examples include journals, radio broadcasts, safehouses, discoveries and personal stories.

Future campaigns may encounter these remnants.

---

## Canon

The body of established truths within Project Ascension.

Canon is divided into multiple layers.

---

## Collapse

The gradual transformation of global civilization into a fragmented world.

The Collapse is not one event but an extended historical process.

---

# F

## Foundation

The collection of documents defining the philosophy, design principles and structure of Project Ascension.

Foundation changes rarely.

---

# H

## Historical Timeline

The chronological sequence of universally accepted historical events.

Serves as the backbone of Universal Canon.

---

# L

## Living Campaign Engine

The system responsible for allowing the world to evolve dynamically in response to player actions.

---

## Living History System

The system governing how history is created, preserved and rediscovered across campaigns.

---

## Living Team System

The system ensuring every expedition consists of six meaningful characters, regardless of player count.

Vacant player positions are filled by fully realized companions.

---

# P

## Polycrisis

A situation where multiple independent crises reinforce one another until their combined effects become systemic.

The Collapse is considered the result of a global polycrisis rather than a single catastrophic event.

---

# S

## Settlement

Any permanent or semi-permanent human community.

Each settlement possesses its own culture, leadership, economy and philosophy.

No two settlements are identical.

---

## Superintelligence

An intelligence that exceeds human cognitive capabilities across virtually every domain.

Aurora is the only confirmed superintelligence within Universal Canon.

---

# U

## Universal Canon

Historical events that remain true in every campaign.

Universal Canon cannot be altered by player actions.

---

# W

## World State

A snapshot of civilization during a particular historical era.

World States define the technological, political, cultural and social conditions in which campaigns begin.
