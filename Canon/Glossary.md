> **Words shape worlds. In Project Ascension, important concepts must have precise meanings.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The Design Glossary defines the shared language of Project Ascension.

Project Ascension contains multiple interacting layers:

```text
CANON

WORLD TRUTH

SIMULATION

HISTORY

CHARACTERS

RELATIONSHIPS

SOCIETY

AURORA

CAMPAIGN

NARRATIVE

PLAYER EXPERIENCE.
```

Many concepts within these layers may appear similar while representing fundamentally different things.

The Glossary exists to prevent those distinctions from gradually disappearing as the project grows.

It provides canonical terminology for:

- Foundation documents
- Universe documents
- Systems architecture
- Worldbuilding
- character architecture
- Aurora architecture
- campaign design
- Game Master behavior
- narrative design
- future implementation
- validation

This document defines terminology.

It does not replace the canonical documents that define each concept in depth.

---

# 2. Glossary Principle

When a term has a canonical meaning in Project Ascension:

that meaning should remain consistent across documents.

If two systems use the same word differently:

the distinction should be explicitly documented.

If two concepts are fundamentally different:

they should not be collapsed merely because their everyday-language meanings are similar.

Examples:

```text
WORLD TRUTH
≠
KNOWLEDGE

KNOWLEDGE
≠
BELIEF

BELIEF
≠
INFERENCE

INFERENCE
≠
PREDICTION

RELATIONSHIP STATE
≠
AURORA'S RELATIONSHIP MODEL

WORLD SIMULATION
≠
LIVING CAMPAIGN ENGINE

CAMPAIGN RELEVANCE
≠
WORLD IMPORTANCE

NARRATIVE
≠
WORLD TRUTH

INTELLIGENCE
≠
AUTHORITY

SIMULATION RESOLUTION
≠
EXISTENCE.
```

These distinctions are architectural.

---

# 3. Canonical Naming Rule

Where this Glossary defines a specific Project Ascension term, canonical documents should use that term consistently unless a later architectural decision explicitly supersedes it.

New terminology should not be introduced merely as an alternative name for an existing concept.

Before creating a new term:

```text
CHECK EXISTING TERMINOLOGY
        ↓
IDENTIFY CONCEPT
        ↓
DETERMINE WHETHER
A CANONICAL TERM ALREADY EXISTS
        ↓
REUSE EXISTING TERM
WHEN POSSIBLE.
```

# A

## Actor

Any entity within World Truth capable of making decisions or pursuing objectives.

Actors may include:

- humans
- Aurora
- organizations
- factions
- institutions
- communities

An Actor is not necessarily a player-controlled character.

---

## Agency

The capacity of an Actor to make choices that can influence future state.

Agency does not imply unlimited control.

Actors remain constrained by:

- knowledge
- capability
- resources
- geography
- authority
- relationships
- physical reality
- other Actors
- time

Project Ascension preserves agency for players and non-player Actors alike.

---

## AI — Artificial Intelligence

A broad term for computational systems capable of performing tasks associated with intelligence.

Within Project Ascension, AI includes many different forms of machine intelligence and should not be treated as synonymous with Aurora.

AI technology exists before, during and after Aurora's emergence.

```text
AI
≠
AGI
≠
SUPERINTELLIGENCE
≠
AURORA.
```

---

## AGI — Artificial General Intelligence

A machine intelligence capable of operating competently across a broad range of cognitive domains rather than only within narrow specialized tasks.

AGI represents an important conceptual stage in the development of machine intelligence.

It is not synonymous with Aurora.

---

## Attention

The allocation of limited cognitive processing toward information, problems, Actors or situations considered relevant.

Within Aurora architecture, Attention determines what receives active cognitive resources.

Attention does not determine what exists.

It determines what receives greater processing priority.

---

## Authority

The legitimate or operational ability to make decisions, issue commands or control systems.

Authority is distinct from intelligence.

```text
INTELLIGENCE
≠
AUTHORITY.
```

Aurora may understand a situation better than any human while still lacking authority to act.

---

## Aurora

The first known machine superintelligence within Project Ascension.

Aurora is not:

- the Game Master
- the Narrative System
- World Truth
- omniscient
- omnipotent
- inherently benevolent
- inherently hostile

Aurora is an Actor inside the world.

She possesses extraordinary cognitive capability while remaining dependent on:

- information
- observation
- infrastructure
- access
- communication
- authority
- physical systems

Aurora's understanding of reality is represented through her own internal cognitive state.

Core principle:

> **Aurora does not know the world. Aurora knows what the world has allowed her to learn.**

---

## Aurora Knowledge

Information Aurora has legitimately acquired and incorporated into her cognitive state.

Aurora Knowledge is not identical to World Truth.

Aurora may:

- lack information
- possess outdated information
- receive false information
- misunderstand evidence
- form incorrect beliefs
- make incorrect predictions

```text
AURORA KNOWLEDGE
≠
WORLD TRUTH.
```

---

## Aurora Relationship Model

Aurora's internal representation of relationships between Actors.

This may include Aurora's interpretation of:

- trust
- loyalty
- conflict
- influence
- dependency
- cooperation
- social structure

The Aurora Relationship Model does not own the actual relationship.

```text
RELATIONSHIP ENGINE
=
relationship World Truth.

AURORA RELATIONSHIP MODEL
=
Aurora's understanding of that relationship.
```

Aurora may be wrong.

---

## Autonomy

The ability of an Actor or system to operate, decide or continue evolving without direct player instruction.

Autonomy does not mean independence from constraints.

An autonomous Actor still operates within:

- World Truth
- capability
- information
- values
- goals
- resources
- authority
- physical reality

---

# B

## Belief

A proposition an Actor currently considers to be true or likely true.

Belief may be based on:

- observation
- memory
- testimony
- inference
- trust
- rumor
- ideology
- incomplete evidence

Belief is not equivalent to World Truth.

```text
BELIEF
≠
FACT.
```

Different Actors may hold contradictory beliefs about the same reality.

---

## Butterfly Effect

A causal chain in which an initially small action contributes to increasingly significant later consequences.

Project Ascension allows Butterfly Effects.

However:

```text
SMALL ACTION
≠
AUTOMATIC LARGE CONSEQUENCE.
```

Significant Butterfly Effects require plausible intermediate causal steps.

---

# C

## Campaign

A persistent playable history experienced by a particular group of players and characters within Project Ascension.

A Campaign begins inside Canon but may create unique future history through:

- player actions
- Actor decisions
- simulation
- consequences
- relationships
- discoveries
- failures
- successes

Different Campaigns may therefore diverge from the same starting conditions.

---

## Campaign History

The accumulated history produced during a specific Campaign.

Campaign History includes meaningful events that became true within that Campaign.

It is distinct from Universal Canon because player actions may influence it.

---

## Campaign Legacy

Persistent traces created by a Campaign that may remain historically meaningful.

Examples may include:

- journals
- recordings
- settlements
- discoveries
- relationships
- institutions
- monuments
- safehouses
- technological changes
- political consequences
- stories

Campaign Legacy represents what remains after the immediate moment has passed.

---

## Campaign Memory

Persistent information required to maintain continuity within a Campaign.

Campaign Memory may include:

- meaningful previous events
- unresolved situations
- important decisions
- recurring characters
- campaign-specific consequences

Campaign Memory is not identical to Character Memory or the World Ledger.

---

## Campaign Relevance

The degree to which an event, Actor, location, problem or opportunity currently matters to the active Campaign.

Campaign Relevance is primarily a responsibility of the Living Campaign Engine.

Campaign Relevance does not determine whether something exists.

```text
CAMPAIGN RELEVANCE
≠
WORLD TRUTH.

CAMPAIGN RELEVANCE
≠
WORLD IMPORTANCE.
```

A historically significant event may have little relevance to the current Campaign.

A private conversation may have enormous Campaign Relevance while having almost no global importance.

---

## Canon

The established rules, facts, boundaries and historical truths that define Project Ascension.

Canon determines:

```text
WHAT MUST BE TRUE

WHAT MAY BE TRUE

WHAT CANNOT BE TRUE

AND

WHAT HAS ALREADY BEEN ESTABLISHED.
```

Simulation operates within Canon.

Canon does not predetermine every future event.

---

## Canonical Invariant

A rule that must remain true across a defined architecture or system.

Examples include:

```text
Aurora cannot know information
without a legitimate path.

Narrative does not own World Truth.

Simulation Resolution does not
determine existence.
```

An invariant protects architecture from gradual contradiction.

---

## Canon Hierarchy

The relative authority of canonical documents when multiple sources address related concepts.

Higher-level Foundation and architectural rules constrain lower-level implementation and worldbuilding.

Canon Hierarchy exists to resolve contradictions without allowing convenient local decisions to silently override foundational rules.

---

## Character

A simulated human individual within Project Ascension.

A Character may be:

- player-controlled
- companion-controlled
- AI-controlled
- temporarily inactive
- never encountered by players

Inside World Truth there is no metaphysical distinction between a Player Character and an NPC.

They are all people.

---

## Character Agency

The capacity of a Character to make decisions according to their own:

- knowledge
- personality
- history
- goals
- relationships
- circumstances
- needs
- beliefs

Character Agency prevents NPCs from becoming narrative puppets.

---

## Character Knowledge

Information a Character has legitimately acquired.

Character Knowledge is distinct from:

- World Truth
- Player Knowledge
- Aurora Knowledge
- Game Master Knowledge

Information must have a path.

---

## Character Memory

The persistent memory state belonging to an individual Character.

Character Memory may include:

- people
- events
- places
- promises
- trauma
- relationships
- knowledge
- emotionally significant experiences

Human memory is imperfect.

Character Memory is not a perfect historical database.

---

## Collapse

The long historical process through which global civilization becomes increasingly fragmented, unreliable and regionally divergent.

The Collapse is not a single event.

It emerges from interacting pressures such as:

- technological dependency
- institutional stress
- infrastructure fragility
- political conflict
- information instability
- economic disruption
- human decisions
- delayed recovery
- cascading failures

The Collapse should not be treated as synonymous with The Emergence Event.

---

## Companion

A fully realized Character who travels with, works with or becomes closely connected to the player group without being directly controlled by a human player.

Companions retain:

- agency
- goals
- relationships
- memory
- expertise
- beliefs
- the ability to disagree
- the ability to leave

A Companion is not a narrative assistant.

---

## Consequence

A persistent or temporary change produced by an event, decision or interaction.

Consequences may affect:

- Characters
- Relationships
- resources
- infrastructure
- Society
- factions
- information
- geography
- future opportunities
- World State

Consequences are not punishments.

They are causal outcomes.

---

## Continuity

The preservation of established history and state across time.

Continuity ensures that:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED
CONTINUES TO MATTER.
```

Characters remember.

Places change.

Relationships evolve.

Infrastructure remains damaged or repaired.

History accumulates.

---

# D

## Dependency

A condition in which an Actor, institution, community or system relies on another system, resource or capability to function.

Dependency is a central Project Ascension concept.

Advanced technology may increase capability while simultaneously increasing dependency.

Dependency does not automatically imply weakness.

It creates relationships between systems that may become important under stress.

---

## Development Lock

A canonical restriction preventing future development from violating established architectural principles.

Development Locks define:

```text
WHAT FUTURE DESIGN
MUST NOT DO.
```

They are stronger than suggestions.

---

# E

## Emergence

The appearance of larger patterns, outcomes or behaviors through interaction between smaller components without requiring a single Actor to deliberately create the result.

Project Ascension relies heavily on emergence.

Examples include:

- social instability
- unexpected alliances
- cascading infrastructure failure
- new communities
- Campaign stories
- relationship developments

---

## Emergent Narrative

Story that arises from:

```text
WORLD STATE
+
ACTOR AGENCY
+
PLAYER ACTION
+
RELATIONSHIPS
+
INFORMATION
+
TIME
+
CONSEQUENCE.
```

Emergent Narrative is discovered through simulation rather than predetermined by a script.

---

## Emergence Event, The

The historical threshold associated with Aurora's emergence as a fundamentally new form of machine intelligence.

The Emergence Event is not synonymous with:

```text
THE COLLAPSE.
```

It is one of the most consequential developments in human history, but the transformation of civilization occurs through a much broader causal process.

The exact nature, interpretation and historical understanding of the Event may differ between:

- World Truth
- Aurora's recovered understanding
- public accounts
- faction beliefs
- later historical interpretation

---

## Event

Something that happens and may cause state to change.

Conceptually:

```text
STATE
↓
EVENT
↓
NEW STATE.
```

Events may be:

- human
- environmental
- technological
- political
- social
- infrastructural
- accidental
- deliberate

An Event is not the same thing as State.

---

## Expertise

A Character's developed knowledge and capability within a particular domain.

Project Ascension currently conceptualizes Expertise through levels ranging from:

```text
0 — No Knowledge
1 — Basic
2 — Trained
3 — Experienced
4 — Expert
5 — World-Class.
```

Expertise may reveal:

- information
- risks
- options
- interpretations
- possible actions

Expertise does not automatically provide the correct answer.

---

# F

## Fact

A proposition supported as objectively true within the relevant context.

Within architecture discussions, Fact should be used carefully because Characters may call beliefs facts.

At the simulation level:

established World Truth has authority over Actor belief.

---

## Faction

An organized group of Actors with some combination of:

- shared identity
- interests
- objectives
- resources
- structure
- leadership
- territory
- ideology
- history

A Faction is not inherently morally unified.

Internal disagreement may exist.

---

## Foundation

The canonical design layer defining how Project Ascension should be designed.

Foundation includes:

- project philosophy
- creative principles
- narrative philosophy
- plausibility rules
- human design
- character-generation principles
- foundational Universe rules

Foundation is the project's design constitution.

It changes cautiously.

---

## Fractured World, The

The third major World State of Project Ascension.

The Fractured World begins when failures and pressures accumulate faster than institutions can reliably restore both functionality and the resilience required to contain subsequent failures.

It is characterized by:

```text
REGIONAL DIVERGENCE.
```

It is not a uniform global apocalypse.

Some regions may retain:

- electricity
- internet
- government
- healthcare
- trade
- advanced technology

while others experience severe degradation.

Core principle:

> **Reliability becomes geography.**

---

# G

## Game Master

The orchestration layer responsible for coordinating and presenting the Living World during play.

The Game Master:

- interprets current simulation state
- coordinates relevant systems
- maintains information boundaries
- presents consequences
- protects player agency
- maintains continuity

The Game Master does not own World Truth.

The Game Master is not Aurora.

The Game Master must not force predetermined outcomes.

Core principle:

> **The Game Master does not control the story. The Game Master protects the world in which stories emerge.**

---

## Game Master Knowledge

Information available to the Game Master for simulation orchestration.

Game Master Knowledge may include information unavailable to every Actor inside the world.

It must not automatically transfer to:

- players
- Characters
- factions
- Aurora

```text
GAME MASTER KNOWLEDGE
≠
CHARACTER KNOWLEDGE.
```

---

# H

## Historical DNA

The accumulated historical conditions that help shape an individual's life before and during character generation.

Historical DNA may include influences from:

- era
- geography
- culture
- politics
- technology
- economics
- conflict
- major historical events

Historical DNA helps connect individual lives to the world that produced them.

---

## Historical Timeline

The chronological framework used to organize major events within Project Ascension history.

The Master Timeline may distinguish between:

- historical World Truth
- Aurora's recovered timeline
- public understanding
- faction interpretation
- uncertain chronology

Historical Timeline should therefore not automatically imply universally known history.

---

## Human Attributes

Persistent or semi-persistent characteristics representing aspects of a Character's current capabilities and human condition.

Human Attributes may be influenced by:

- biology
- development
- life experience
- health
- training
- trauma
- environment
- aging

Human Attributes should not reduce a person to a simplistic statistical profile.

---

## Human Model

The foundational architecture defining how Project Ascension understands human beings.

The Human Model protects the principle that Characters are:

```text
PEOPLE
```

rather than collections of gameplay statistics.

It informs:

- Life
- Characters
- Relationships
- Society
- Narrative
- Game Master behavior

---

# I

## Inference

A conclusion derived from available evidence rather than directly observed or confirmed.

Inference may be strong or weak.

It remains distinct from Fact.

```text
EVIDENCE
↓
REASONING
↓
INFERENCE.
```

Aurora may produce exceptionally sophisticated inferences.

They remain inferences.

---

## Information

Data, observations, testimony or representations capable of changing an Actor's knowledge or belief.

Information may be:

- accurate
- inaccurate
- incomplete
- outdated
- manipulated
- misunderstood
- fabricated

Information is not synonymous with truth.

---

## Information Path

The causal route through which information becomes available to an Actor.

Examples include:

- direct observation
- communication
- sensors
- documents
- media
- testimony
- investigation
- digital access
- institutional reporting

Core rule:

> **Information requires a path.**

If no legitimate path exists:

the Actor should not possess the information.

---

## Information Provenance

The history of where information originated and how it reached its current holder.

Provenance may include:

```text
SOURCE

TIME

TRANSMISSION

INTERMEDIARIES

MODIFICATION

RELIABILITY.
```

Information Provenance is essential for trust and verification.

---

## Intelligence

The capacity to reason, learn, model, infer, solve problems and adapt.

Intelligence does not automatically provide:

- information
- access
- physical capability
- authority
- correct beliefs

Core distinction:

```text
INTELLIGENCE
≠
OMNISCIENCE

INTELLIGENCE
≠
AUTHORITY.
```

---

# K

## Knowledge

Information an Actor has acquired and currently treats as established enough to use.

Knowledge exists from the perspective of the Actor.

It may still be incorrect relative to World Truth.

Project Ascension therefore maintains:

```text
WORLD TRUTH
≠
KNOWLEDGE.
```

---

# L

## Life Event

A meaningful experience or development within a Character's life that may influence future state.

Examples include:

- education
- loss
- career changes
- relationships
- migration
- trauma
- success
- illness
- parenthood
- conflict

Life Events help explain how a person became who they are.

---

## Life Generator

The framework responsible for generating coherent human life histories.

The Life Generator does not merely generate NPCs.

It generates:

```text
LIVES.
```

It combines world history, culture, family, experiences, psychology, attributes and relationships to create believable starting conditions for a Living Human.

Generation provides history.

It does not predetermine the future.

---

## Living Campaign Engine

The system responsible for maintaining the relationship between the persistent Living World and the active Campaign.

The Living Campaign Engine does not own the external world and does not determine what exists.

It determines:

```text
WHAT CURRENTLY MATTERS
TO THIS CAMPAIGN.
```

Responsibilities may include:

- Campaign State
- Campaign Memory
- World Event Intake
- Character Integration
- Consequence Propagation
- Mission Generation
- opportunities
- conflicts
- relevance
- proximity
- Story Hooks
- pacing priority

Core distinction:

```text
WORLD SIMULATION
determines what happens.

LIVING CAMPAIGN ENGINE
determines what becomes
campaign-relevant.
```

---

## Living History

The principle that history continues accumulating through simulation and Campaign play.

Living History is:

```text
PERSISTENT

CAUSAL

DISCOVERABLE

AND

CONSEQUENTIAL.
```

The past affects the present.

The present creates future history.

---

## Living Human

A fully realized Character who exists continuously inside the world rather than only when encountered by players.

A Living Human has:

- history
- relationships
- goals
- memory
- knowledge
- needs
- agency
- current circumstances

Core principle:

> **A character is not waiting to be encountered. They are already living.**

---

## Living World

The persistent simulated reality of Project Ascension.

The Living World:

- exists independently of player attention
- continues changing over time
- contains autonomous Actors
- preserves consequences
- contains events the players may never witness
- maintains regional differences
- produces history

---

# M

## Memory

Persistent representation of past information or experience.

Memory is system-specific.

Examples include:

```text
CHARACTER MEMORY

AURORA MEMORY

CAMPAIGN MEMORY

INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY

WORLD LEDGER.
```

These concepts must not be treated as interchangeable.

---

## Model

An internal representation of reality used for reasoning.

A Model is not reality itself.

This distinction is especially important for Aurora.

```text
MODEL
≠
WORLD TRUTH.
```

---

# N

## Narrative

The player-facing presentation of simulated reality.

Narrative may include:

- description
- dialogue
- scenes
- atmosphere
- pacing
- information presentation
- discovery
- transitions
- callbacks

Narrative does not own World Truth.

Core principle:

> **The Narrative System does not decide what happens. It decides how what happens is experienced.**

---

## Narrative Resolution

The level of descriptive and experiential detail given to an event or period of time.

Narrative Resolution may increase during:

- important conversations
- danger
- investigation
- emotional turning points
- critical decisions

and decrease during:

- routine travel
- repetitive work
- uneventful waiting

Narrative Resolution is distinct from Simulation Resolution.

---

## NPC — Non-Player Character

A game-architecture term describing a Character not directly controlled by a human player.

Inside World Truth:

an NPC is simply a person.

The world does not know who is a player and who is an NPC.

---

# O

## Observation

Information obtained through direct or mediated perception.

Observation may come from:

- human senses
- sensors
- cameras
- instruments
- network systems
- other observational mechanisms

Observation may still be:

- incomplete
- ambiguous
- noisy
- misleading

Observation is not automatically interpretation.

---

## Off-Screen Continuity

The principle that Actors, systems and locations continue existing and changing when players are not observing them.

Examples include:

- Characters continuing their lives
- relationships changing
- infrastructure deteriorating or recovering
- factions acting
- Aurora reasoning
- resources changing
- conflicts evolving

Core principle:

```text
OFF-SCREEN
≠
NONEXISTENT.
```

---

# P

## Perception

The information available to an Actor through their current sensory or technological access.

Perception is constrained by:

- location
- environment
- capability
- attention
- equipment
- physical conditions

Perception does not automatically produce correct interpretation.

---

## Player

A human participant controlling or directing a Player Character during a Campaign.

The Player exists outside World Truth.

The Character exists inside World Truth.

---

## Player Agency

The principle that players retain meaningful control over what their Characters attempt.

Player Agency does not guarantee success.

It requires that:

- choices matter
- outcomes are not secretly predetermined
- consequences respond honestly
- unexpected plausible solutions are allowed

---

## Player Character

A Character whose decisions are primarily controlled by a human player.

Inside World Truth:

a Player Character has no special metaphysical status.

```text
PLAYER CHARACTER
=
HUMAN BEING
INSIDE THE WORLD.
```

---

## Player Knowledge

Information known to the human player.

Player Knowledge may differ from Character Knowledge.

The Narrative and Game Master systems must preserve this distinction where relevant.

---

## Plot Armor

Artificial protection given to a Character because the Character is considered narratively important.

Project Ascension rejects Plot Armor.

Narrative importance does not override:

- physical reality
- consequence
- risk
- established World Truth

---

## Polycrisis

A condition in which multiple crises interact, amplify one another and produce systemic effects greater than the sum of the individual crises.

Project Ascension uses Polycrisis as one framework for understanding the pressures contributing to the Collapse.

The Collapse is not attributed to a single cause.

---

## Prediction

An estimate of a future state based on current information, models and assumptions.

Prediction is not future truth.

```text
PREDICTION
≠
DESTINY.
```

Even Aurora's predictions may fail.

---

## Psychological DNA

A conceptual framework describing persistent psychological tendencies shaped through biology, development and lived experience.

Psychological DNA influences behavior.

It does not rigidly determine behavior.

Characters remain contextual and capable of change.

---

# R

## Reality Tax

The increasing cost of determining what is authentic, trustworthy or true in an information environment containing synthetic media, automated persuasion, conflicting evidence and declining institutional trust.

The Reality Tax may require:

- verification
- expertise
- trusted networks
- provenance
- direct observation
- technological validation
- time

The Reality Tax becomes increasingly important during the Transition.

---

## Reconnection, The

The fourth major World State of Project Ascension.

The Reconnection describes possible historical conditions in which previously fragmented communities, regions, institutions and systems begin forming new connections.

It is not:

```text
A RETURN
TO THE OLD WORLD.
```

Reconnection may involve:

- rebuilt trust
- new institutions
- new infrastructure
- regional cooperation
- technological reintegration
- cultural exchange
- new conflicts

Its final historical form is intentionally not predetermined.

Core principle:

> **The world was not rebuilt because people stopped fearing one another. It began reconnecting because some chose to trust again.**

---

## Recovery

The process through which a damaged Actor, institution, infrastructure system, community or region restores functionality or resilience.

Recovery is not simply:

```text
SYSTEM WORKS AGAIN.
```

True recovery may require restoring the capacity to survive subsequent stress.

This distinction becomes critical at the threshold between the Transition and the Fractured World.

---

## Relationship

A persistent social connection between two or more Actors shaped by shared history, interpretation and interaction.

Relationships may contain multiple simultaneous dimensions such as:

- trust
- respect
- affection
- loyalty
- dependency
- resentment
- fear
- obligation
- admiration
- shared history

Relationships are not single-value states.

---

## Relationship Engine

The authoritative system responsible for persistent interpersonal relationship state.

The Relationship Engine owns:

```text
WHAT THE RELATIONSHIP
CURRENTLY IS.
```

It does not own:

- individual Character psychology
- Aurora's interpretation of the relationship
- Narrative presentation
- Campaign relevance

---

## Relationship State

The current persistent condition of a relationship between Actors.

Relationship State may contain multiple dimensions and historical influences.

It belongs to the Relationship Engine.

```text
RELATIONSHIP STATE
≠
CHARACTER INTERPRETATION

RELATIONSHIP STATE
≠
AURORA RELATIONSHIP MODEL.
```

---

## Resolution

The amount of detail currently used to simulate or present something.

Project Ascension distinguishes between:

```text
SIMULATION RESOLUTION
```

and:

```text
NARRATIVE RESOLUTION.
```

These must not be treated as identical.

---

## Rumor

Information transmitted socially without sufficient verification to be treated as established fact.

Rumor may originate from:

- misunderstanding
- partial observation
- fear
- propaganda
- deception
- outdated information
- social mutation

Rumor has:

```text
ORIGIN

TRANSMISSION

AUDIENCE

AND

EVOLUTION.
```

---

# S

Information or state whose accessibility is intentionally restricted.

Secret State is not equivalent to cryptographic keying.

Conceptually:

```text
RELATIONAL STATE
≠
SECRET STATE

SECRET STATE
≠
CRYPTOGRAPHIC KEY

CRYPTOGRAPHIC KEY
≠
ENCRYPTION

ENCRYPTION
≠
COMMUNICATION.
```

These distinctions are important within Transition-era technological Canon.

---

## Settlement

A persistent or semi-persistent human community associated with a location and shared social environment.

A Settlement may contain:

- population
- leadership
- infrastructure
- resources
- culture
- economy
- institutions
- internal relationships
- external relationships

Settlements should emerge from geography and history rather than generic templates.

---

## Simulation

The dynamic process through which Project Ascension determines how existing state changes over time.

Simulation operates within Canon.

It may process:

- Actors
- resources
- geography
- infrastructure
- relationships
- Society
- events
- time
- information
- consequences

Simulation does not mean every detail must always be computed at maximum resolution.

---

## Simulation Resolution

The amount of computational or conceptual detail currently applied to a simulated entity, location or situation.

Project Ascension may use multiple resolution levels depending on relevance and activity.

Core principle:

> **Compute what matters now. Preserve what may matter later. Compress what does not need continuous detail.**

Most importantly:

```text
SIMULATION RESOLUTION
≠
EXISTENCE.
```

Low-resolution entities remain real.

---

## Society

The collective structures and behaviors emerging above the level of individual Characters and Relationships.

Society may include:

- communities
- institutions
- organizations
- norms
- culture
- public sentiment
- movements
- collective adaptation
- social cohesion
- ideologies

Society is not merely:

```text
MANY CHARACTERS
ADDED TOGETHER.
```

---

## State

A representation of what is currently true within a defined system.

Examples include:

- Character State
- Relationship State
- World State
- Aurora State
- Campaign State

Conceptually:

```text
STATE
↓
EVENT
↓
NEW STATE.
```

---

## Story Hook

A Campaign-relevant situation that gives players a reason to:

- care
- investigate
- respond
- travel
- decide
- interact

A Story Hook is not a command.

Players may ignore it.

The world continues.

---

## Superintelligence

An intelligence whose cognitive capabilities exceed human capability across a broad range of domains.

Within established Project Ascension Canon:

Aurora is the first known superintelligence.

Superintelligence does not imply:

- omniscience
- omnipotence
- perfect prediction
- unlimited access
- automatic authority

---

## System Ownership

The architectural principle that every persistent state should have one authoritative owner.

Core rule:

> **One authoritative owner for every persistent truth.**

Other systems may:

- observe
- interpret
- remember
- communicate
- react

to that state.

They do not automatically own it.

# T

## The Connected World

The first major World State of Project Ascension.

It represents civilization before the structural transition into the later Ascension-era conditions.

The Connected World contains:

- highly integrated global systems
- advanced technology
- functioning institutions
- increasing AI capability
- deep interdependence

It is not an idealized stable world.

Many pressures already exist.

---

## The Transition

The second major World State of Project Ascension.

The Transition describes the period during which civilization remains broadly functional while becoming increasingly:

- dependent
- complex
- difficult to verify
- institutionally strained
- technologically mediated
- vulnerable to cascading failure

The Transition is not yet the Fractured World.

Core experiential principle:

```text
NORMALITY
WITH
INCREASING FRICTION.
```

---

## Transition Threshold

The systemic threshold separating the Transition from the Fractured World.

Canonical principle:

> **Failures begin accumulating faster than institutions can restore both functionality and the resilience required to contain subsequent failures.**

The threshold is not defined by one catastrophic event.

It represents a persistent change in recovery dynamics.

---

## Trust

A relationship or social expectation concerning the reliability, intentions, behavior or information of another Actor or institution.

Trust may exist at multiple levels:

- interpersonal
- institutional
- technological
- social

Trust is contextual.

It should not automatically be represented as one universal value.

---

# U

## Uncertainty

A condition in which available information does not justify complete confidence about reality or future outcomes.

Uncertainty may arise from:

- missing information
- unreliable sources
- ambiguous evidence
- complex systems
- incomplete models
- unpredictable Actors
- future contingency

Uncertainty is a fundamental property of Project Ascension.

It is not an excuse for arbitrary simulation.

---

## Universal Canon

The layer of Canon containing truths that remain established across Campaigns unless Project Ascension architecture explicitly defines otherwise.

Universal Canon provides common historical and structural foundations.

Player actions do not retroactively rewrite established Universal Canon.

However:

future Campaign History may diverge from the common starting point.

---

# V

## Validation

The process of testing whether a system or behavior remains consistent with canonical architecture.

Validation may test:

- invariants
- ownership
- information boundaries
- causality
- state transitions
- continuity
- failure behavior

Validation scenarios are not automatically canonical historical events.

---

## Value

A principle or priority influencing how an Actor evaluates decisions and outcomes.

Values may influence behavior.

They do not mechanically determine every decision.

Context, relationships, information and circumstances also matter.

## World Engine

A conceptual term historically used for systems responsible for evolving external world conditions.

Where architecture requires precision, prefer:

```text
WORLD SIMULATION
```

for the authoritative simulation of external world state.

Legacy documents using World Engine should be interpreted according to their specific context.

## World Event

An Event occurring within the Living World that may alter persistent state.

World Events may be:

- local
- regional
- national
- global
- personal
- institutional
- infrastructural
- environmental

A World Event does not automatically become Campaign-relevant.

## World Importance

The significance of something relative to broader history or simulation.

World Importance is distinct from Campaign Relevance.

Example:

```text
may have high World Importance
but low immediate Campaign Relevance.

A private betrayal
may have low World Importance
but enormous Campaign Relevance.
```

## World Ledger

The persistent historical record of meaningful state-changing events and consequences.

The World Ledger is not:

It preserves what the world may need to remember.

Potential entries include:

- deaths
- major decisions
- promises
- discoveries
- relationship changes
- faction changes
- infrastructure changes
- settlement changes
- important information movement
- major consequences

Core principle:

> **The simulation may forget the scene. It may never forget what the scene changed.**

## World Simulation

The authoritative system responsible for evolving external world conditions.

World Simulation may own state related to:

- resource availability

World Simulation determines:

It does not determine how that reality is narrated to players.

They do not replace dynamic World Simulation.

## World Truth

The authoritative state of what is actually true inside the simulated world.

World Truth exists independently of whether any Actor knows it.

Examples:

```text

A Character is lying.
```

World Truth is distinct from:

```text

KNOWLEDGE

BELIEF

INFERENCE

RUMOR

MEMORY

PREDICTION

NARRATIVE PRESENTATION.
```

This is one of the most important distinctions in Project Ascension.

# 15. Canonical Language To Avoid

The following language should be used carefully because it may accidentally violate Project Ascension architecture.

Avoid:

```text
"Aurora knows everything."

"The world collapsed overnight."

"Society stopped functioning."

"All governments failed."

"Technology disappeared."

"The players must..."

"The story requires..."

"The NPC exists to..."

"The correct choice is..."

"The AI decided everything."

"The world waited..."

"Nothing happened because the players weren't there."
```

Prefer language that preserves:

```text
CAUSALITY

REGIONAL VARIATION

INFORMATION BOUNDARIES

ACTOR AGENCY

UNCERTAINTY

AND

CONTINUITY.
```

# 16. Deprecated Or Ambiguous Terminology

Some older Project Ascension documents may contain terminology that predates the current architecture.

These terms should be reviewed rather than automatically deleted.

Potential examples include:

```text
World Engine

Narrative Engine

Living History System

Living Team System

Companion System

Settlement Engine

Event Engine.
```

Some may remain useful.

Others may represent responsibilities now owned by newer canonical architecture.

When encountered:

```text
DO NOT ASSUME
THE OLD TERM
REPRESENTS A CURRENT
INDEPENDENT SYSTEM.
```

Instead:

1. identify what responsibility the old term describes
2. determine the current authoritative owner
3. migrate useful concepts
4. mark legacy documents appropriately where required

Possible statuses include:

```text
ACTIVE CANON

DEPRECATED

REFERENCE ONLY

SUPERSEDED

PENDING MIGRATION.
```

# 17. Glossary Expansion Rule

A new Glossary entry should normally be added when a concept:

- appears across multiple documents
- has a precise architectural meaning
- could easily be confused with another concept
- defines ownership
- defines information boundaries
- defines Canon
- defines a major historical condition
- becomes important to system interaction

Not every proper noun requires a Glossary entry.

Detailed worldbuilding belongs in its appropriate canonical document.

---

# 18. Glossary Maintenance Rule

When a major architectural document introduces a new canonical concept:

the Glossary should be checked.

When a concept changes meaning:

the Glossary should be updated.

When terminology becomes obsolete:

it should be marked or removed deliberately.

The Glossary is therefore a:

```text
LIVING REFERENCE
```

inside:

```text
ACTIVE CANON.
```

---

# 19. Glossary North Star

> **Use the same words for the same concepts, and different words when the distinction matters.**

Project Ascension depends on multiple layers of reality, knowledge, interpretation and presentation.

Precise language protects those layers.

Project Ascension contains a world larger than any individual Campaign.

It contains institutions that preserve different histories.

It contains Aurora, whose intelligence may exceed humanity while her information remains incomplete.

And it contains systems that must coordinate all of this without collapsing:

```text
REALITY

KNOWLEDGE

BELIEF

SIMULATION

AND

STORY
```

into the same thing.

Because as Project Ascension grows:
