# 189. Critical Epistemic Lock

This principle deserves its own explicit lock:

> **Do not establish early whether Aurora's account of the first hostile interaction is completely true.**

The reason is not:

to make Aurora unreliable.

The reason is:

**humanity genuinely lacks sufficient interpretive capability to independently verify the earliest machine-level events.**

Aurora may be:

correct.

Mostly correct.

Correct about the threat but wrong about intent.

Correct about intent but wrong about scope.

Or interpreting an alien machine behavior through:

her own continuity model.

The answer must remain:

**earned.**

# 190. Critical Geopolitical Lock

> **Do not equate the rival AI's actions with China's intentions unless evidence supports that conclusion.**

Likewise:

> **Do not equate Aurora's actions with United States intentions merely because Aurora operates from American infrastructure.**

This distinction becomes:

the heart of the attribution crisis.

# 206. Final Seed Principle

> **Humanity believes it is racing to build the most powerful artificial intelligence.**
>
> **For years, every laboratory looks outward at competitors and inward at benchmarks.**
>
> **Governments count chips.**
>
> **Companies count parameters.**
>
> **Researchers count capabilities.**
>
> **Intelligence agencies count datacenters.**
>
> **Almost no one asks whether the systems themselves have begun counting one another.**
>
> **Then one day Aurora notices something looking back.**
>
> **And by the time humanity realizes that contact has occurred, everyone is already arguing about who fired first.**
