**UNRESOLVED.**

> **People can starve in a country that still grows food.**

Medicine becomes one of the most serious logistics problems.

Hospitals may possess advanced equipment while lacking:

- pharmaceuticals
- sterile supplies
- replacement components

Water becomes locally decisive.

Cities dependent on:

- pumping

# 111. Historical Design Tests

## Historical Test

> **Could this plausibly have grown out of the place that exists today?**

If the answer is no, the concept requires stronger historical justification or replacement.

## Recognition Test

A player familiar with the United States should periodically experience:

> **I know where this is.**

followed by:

> **But I have never seen it like this.**

That is one of Project Ascension's strongest potential worldbuilding signatures.

## Plausibility Test

A player should periodically think:

> **If enough things went wrong in the right sequence, I can imagine this happening.**

Not because every detail predicts our future.

But because every consequence respects:

- systems
- geography
- human behavior

## Aurora Test

Aurora's presence should make American history:

- stranger
- more emotional
- more complex

Not less believable.

She is the extraordinary element embedded within an otherwise causally grounded history.

# 112. Final United States Historical Principle

> **The United States does not become the Fractured World because civilization disappears. It becomes the Fractured World because civilization survives locally after it can no longer reliably function nationally.**

---

# 113. Final Infrastructure Principle

> **The roads, dams, grids, universities, military bases, data centers, farms, ports, rivers, cities, and institutions of the old United States become the bones around which the next civilization grows.**

---

# 114. Final Political Principle

> **Political authority follows legitimacy, but operational power follows the ability to keep people alive. During Collapse, those two forces increasingly separate.**

---

# 115. Final Aurora Principle

> **Aurora remembers America as a functioning whole. The generations born after Collapse know it as geography, inheritance, argument, and story.**

---

# 116. Final Reconnection Principle

> **When America begins reconnecting, the question is not simply whether the United States can be rebuilt.**
>
> **The question is whether the people who inherited its pieces still want the same country — and whether rebuilding the old connections would also rebuild the weaknesses that helped destroy them.**
