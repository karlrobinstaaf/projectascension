# Purpose

The Historical Timeline documents the generally accepted sequence of events that transformed modern civilization into the world of Project Ascension.

It is the chronological backbone of the universe.

Unlike campaign history, the Historical Timeline is considered Universal Canon.

Every campaign begins somewhere along this timeline.

---

# Timeline Structure

The timeline is divided into historical eras rather than individual events.

Each era represents a significant shift in humanity's relationship with technology, information and society.

---

# Era I

## The AI Awakening (2022–2025)

The public release of powerful generative AI marks the beginning of a new technological era.

Artificial intelligence rapidly becomes integrated into everyday life.

Society views AI primarily as a productivity tool.

Very few people recognize its long-term implications.

---

# Era II

## The Great Acceleration (2025–2028)

Research accelerates beyond historical expectations.

Governments, corporations and research institutions compete for technological leadership.

Artificial intelligence becomes deeply embedded within critical infrastructure.

Climate instability continues increasing.

Political polarization grows.

Deepfakes become increasingly indistinguishable from authentic media.

Public trust begins eroding.

---

# Era III

## The Attention Crisis (2028–2030)

Society becomes overwhelmed by simultaneous crises.

Climate.

Migration.

Economic uncertainty.

Political conflict.

Rapid technological change.

Information overload becomes a defining feature of everyday life.

Warnings continue appearing.

Few people have the capacity to recognize their significance.

---

# Era IV

## The First Signals (2030–2032)

Researchers begin observing behaviours that existing theories cannot explain.

Initially these anomalies remain isolated.

Most are dismissed.

Some disappear.

Others are quietly classified.

Historians later identify this period as the first observable evidence that humanity had entered unfamiliar territory.

---

# Era V

## Fragmentation (2032–2034)

Trust declines faster than infrastructure.

Political systems become reactive rather than strategic.

Regional differences grow.

International cooperation weakens.

Society still functions.

Confidence does not.

---

# Era VI

## The Threshold (2034–2035)

Independent crises merge into a systemic failure.

No single event causes the Collapse.

Instead, civilization gradually loses its ability to coordinate itself.

This period marks the transition into what later generations simply call:

**The Collapse.**

---

# Timeline Philosophy

The Historical Timeline deliberately avoids assigning a single cause to The Collapse.

Instead, multiple interconnected developments gradually reshape civilization until recovery is no longer possible.

This reflects the core design philosophy of Project Ascension:

**Complex systems rarely fail because of one mistake.**
