>
> **The next discovery was that not all of the relationship was required to preserve the secret.**

---

# 92. Compression North Star

> **Making a secret smaller did not make it simpler.**
>
> **It changed what could be done with it — and what could be done to it.**

---

# 93. Possession North Star

> **A relationship was difficult to steal.**
>
> **A representation of that relationship could fit somewhere much smaller.**

---

# 94. Security North Star

> **The moment the secret became easier to carry, it also became easier to lose.**

---

# 95. Predictability North Star

> **An attacker did not always need to steal tomorrow's secret.**
>
> **Sometimes predicting it would be enough.**

---

# 96. Entropy North Star

> **Difficulty is not randomness.**
>
> **Uncertainty is not entropy.**
>
> **And a secret that has not yet been predicted is not necessarily unpredictable.**

> **Compactness changed the security object.**
>
