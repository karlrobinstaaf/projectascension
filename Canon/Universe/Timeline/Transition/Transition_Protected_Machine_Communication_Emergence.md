# 89. Communication North Star

> **The machines did not suddenly learn to speak in secret.**
>
> **Humans authorized them to use a new kind of key inside security systems humans already understood.**

---

# 90. Authority North Star

> **Humans still controlled who could communicate, why they could communicate, and when the channel had to end.**
>
> **What humans increasingly could not do was follow every authorized exchange while it happened.**

---

# 91. Visibility North Star

> **A system could be visible without every internal event being directly observable.**
>
> **A channel could be governed without every governor holding its key.**

---

# 92. Tempo North Star

> **The machines did not need to hide from human oversight.**
>
> **They only needed to operate faster than human attention.**

---

# 93. Oversight North Star

> **To supervise systems operating beyond human cognitive scale, humanity increasingly built machines that explained machines to humans.**
>
> **Then humanity had to supervise those machines too.**

---

# 94. Control North Star

> **Control did not disappear.**
>
> **It separated into authority, policy, intervention, observation, understanding and reversibility.**
>
> **For the first time, humanity could clearly see that those were not the same thing.**

---

# 95. Governance North Star

> **The problem was no longer whether humans could press the stop button.**
>
> **The problem was whether they could understand everything that would stop with it.**

---

# 96. Transition North Star

> **The machines had changed enough.**
>
> **Now the story had to follow what humanity became around them.**

---
