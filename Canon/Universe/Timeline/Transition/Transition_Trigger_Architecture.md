# 193. Systems North Star

>
> **It is the event that causes another system to behave differently.**

---

# 194. Human North Star

> **People do not wake up one morning inside a collapsed civilization.**
>
> **They adapt to one exception, then another, then another — until the exceptions have become the world.**

# 195. Aurora North Star

>
> **She should begin by realizing that responsibility may no longer be the correct first question.**

# 196. Rival AI North Star

>
> **Then one machine action can mean far more than the action itself.**

# 197. Civilization North Star

> **Civilization survives individual failures through resilience.**
>

> **A market protects capital.**
>
> **A family protects itself.**

> **Aurora tries to prevent escalation.**
>
> **Each response changes the environment for everyone else.**
>
