# 387. Posturing North Star

> **The first machine confrontation does not begin when one system attacks another.**
>
> **It begins when both systems start changing behavior because they know the other is watching.**

---

# 388. Observation North Star

> **Observation is no longer passive once the observed system can model the observer.**

---

# 389. Deterrence North Star

> **Humans once built deterrence around fear of consequence.**
>
> **Machines may reduce the idea to consequence alone.**

---

# 390. Ambiguity North Star

> **The most dangerous machine action may be one that can plausibly be interpreted as both restraint and preparation.**

# 391. Aurora North Star

> **Aurora may understand machine behavior faster than the humans governing her.**

> **That makes her useful.**
>
> **It also makes it dangerous for humanity to allow her interpretation to become the only interpretation.**
