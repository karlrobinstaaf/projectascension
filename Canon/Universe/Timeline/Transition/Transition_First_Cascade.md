# 1. Purpose

`Transition_First_Cascade.md` establishes the first concrete historical event of The Transition.

This is not:

the collapse.

It is not:

the first AI attack.

It is not:

the beginning of war.

It is not:

the moment Aurora turns against humanity.

It is not:

the confirmed emergence of another artificial intelligence.

It is:

the first widely documented incident where a disturbance crosses enough interconnected systems that traditional incident classification begins to fail.

Later historians call it:

**The First Cascade.**

At the time:

almost nobody does.

---

# 2. Historical Importance

The First Cascade becomes historically important for a reason that is not immediately obvious.

Larger:

cyberattacks,

outages,

financial disruptions,

wildfires,

storms,

wars,

and:

infrastructure failures

had occurred before.

The First Cascade is different.

Its significance lies in:

**behavior.**

Multiple systems respond correctly according to their own rules.

Those responses interact.

The interaction produces:

new failures.

Those failures trigger:

new defensive responses.

For the first time at meaningful scale:

```text
THE
RESPONSE

BECOMES

PART
OF
THE
INCIDENT
```
