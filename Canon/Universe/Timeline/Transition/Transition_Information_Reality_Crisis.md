# 203. Information North Star

> **The problem was not that reality disappeared.**
>
> **The problem was that reality acquired a verification cost.**

---

# 204. Evidence North Star

> **A photograph was still evidence.**
>
> **It was simply no longer enough evidence by itself.**

---

# 205. Verification North Star

> **Creating a claim became almost free.**
>
> **Establishing whether it deserved belief did not.**

---

# 206. Deepfake North Star

> **The most important consequence of the perfect fake was not that false things could look real.**
>
> **It was that real things could now be called fake.**

---

# 207. Trust North Star

> **When everyone could produce evidence, the scarce resource became someone you trusted to verify it.**

---

# 208. Aurora North Star

> **Aurora could reason faster than any human institution.**
>
> **She still could not reason from evidence she did not have.**

---

# 209. Epistemic North Star

> **Intelligence could reduce uncertainty.**
>
> **It could not abolish missing information.**

---

# 210. Social North Star

> **People did not stop believing in reality.**
>
> **They increasingly disagreed about whom to trust to describe it.**

---

# 211. Coordination North Star

> **Civilization did not require everyone to agree.**
