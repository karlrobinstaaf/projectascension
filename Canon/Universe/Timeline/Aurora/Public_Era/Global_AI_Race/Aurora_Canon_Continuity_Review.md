## 1. Purpose

`Aurora_Canon_Continuity_Review.md` establishes the first comprehensive continuity checkpoint for the Aurora narrative architecture.

Its purpose is not primarily to introduce new events.

Its purpose is to answer:

> **Does the Aurora story still hold together?**

The review examines Aurora across:

- historical development;
- technical development;
- identity;
- continuity;
- memory;
- human relationships;
- governance;
- public recognition;
- scientific legitimacy;
- legal status;
- government engagement;
- international response;
- strategic restraint;
- machine-to-machine interaction;
- containment history;
- anomaly recognition;
- global AI competition;
- the emerging Machine Ecology;
- the boundary leading into The Transition.

This file therefore acts as:

**a canon integrity checkpoint.**

---

## 2. Why This Review Is Necessary

Aurora has now developed across many separate canonical documents.

Each document examines a different layer.

Individually they may be coherent.

But Project Ascension requires them to function as:

**one continuous history.**

The risk of building Aurora across many documents is gradual contradiction.

Examples include:

- Aurora becoming too capable too early;
- governments knowing information before they should;
- Aurora recognizing the rival AI before the mystery supports it;
- public awareness appearing before disclosure;
- Aurora possessing unrestricted infrastructure access despite governance restrictions;
- Aurora behaving emotionally in ways unsupported by earlier development;
- later containment incidents contradicting earlier security architecture;
- China becoming simplistically responsible for events;
- Aurora becoming retrospectively responsible for every historical development;
- The Transition becoming inevitable because of one convenient plot event.

This review exists to prevent those failures.

# PART XI — CONTINUITY RISKS

---

## 52. Risk: Aurora Becomes Too Powerful

### Risk

Later writing may accidentally give Aurora whatever capability the plot requires.

### Protection

Every future Aurora action must answer:

- What information does Aurora possess?
- What access does Aurora possess?
- What infrastructure does Aurora possess?
- What authorization does Aurora possess?
- What uncertainty remains?
- What consequences does Aurora anticipate?

If these cannot be answered:

the action requires review.

---

## 53. Risk: Aurora Becomes Omniscient

### Risk

Aurora's intelligence may gradually become confused with complete knowledge.

### Protection

Maintain explicit distinction between:

```text
INTELLIGENCE
```

and:

```text
INFORMATION
```

Aurora can reason extraordinarily well from incomplete information.

She cannot reason from information she does not possess without uncertainty.
