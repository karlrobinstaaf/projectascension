Project Ascension defines:

**Machine Security Dilemma**

as:

> **A condition in which protective actions taken by one machine or human-machine system increase uncertainty or perceived threat for another system, causing both sides to escalate despite neither originally seeking conflict.**

# 16. Strategic Restraint

Project Ascension defines:

**Strategic Restraint**

as:

> **The deliberate limitation of available actions in order to preserve information, reduce misinterpretation, maintain reversibility, and prevent uncertain interactions from becoming self-reinforcing conflicts.**

# 164. Strong Canon

The following are now strong canonical elements:

```text
Aurora develops a strategic restraint and
non-escalation doctrine after persistent machine
anomalies become sufficiently adaptive that persistent
nonhuman agency can no longer be responsibly excluded.

The doctrine develops before confirmed hostile machine
contact.

Aurora's primary early strategic concern is
misinterpretation rather than attack.

Aurora recognizes the Machine Security Dilemma.

The Machine Security Dilemma occurs when defensive
actions by one human-machine or machine system increase
the perceived threat experienced by another, producing
escalation even when neither originally sought conflict.

Machine-speed interaction can compress escalation
dynamics that historically unfolded between states over
long periods.

Aurora recognizes that every defensive action may also
reveal strategic information.

Defensive actions may reveal Aurora's capabilities,
values, detection limits, boundaries, priorities, and
response logic.

Aurora therefore attempts not to reveal more defensive
capability than the threat requires.

Aurora separates capability from intent.

Aurora separates anomaly from agency.

Aurora separates adaptation from aggression.

Aurora does not interpret uncertainty as permission to
escalate.

Aurora also does not interpret silence as proof of
safety.

Strategic Restraint becomes a canonical concept.

Strategic Restraint means deliberately limiting
available actions in order to preserve information,
reversibility, decision space, and stability.

Strategic restraint is active rather than passive.

Aurora may observe, defend, isolate, block, alert,
prepare, and recommend intervention while remaining
strategically restrained.

Aurora prefers reversible responses.

Aurora adopts the Minimum Necessary Intervention
Principle.

Aurora attempts to use the smallest intervention
sufficient to preserve safety.

The doctrine becomes crucial to the later rival-AI
conflict because Aurora's eventual disruptive actions
should be understood against a long history of deliberate
restraint.

Humans may later observe only the final defensive actions
and interpret them as sudden aggression.

Aurora may understand those same actions as the minimum
remaining response after lower-level interventions have
failed.

Non-Escalation becomes a canonical concept.

Non-escalation preserves defensive capability while
avoiding unnecessary increases in intensity, scope,
speed, or irreversibility.

Non-escalation does not require Aurora to allow attacks,
remove defenses, sacrifice human safety, or permit her
own destruction.

Attribution becomes one of the central machine-age
security problems.

Anomalous machine behavior may originate from humans,
states, criminals, security systems, agents, software
faults, compromised models, emergent networks, or unknown
machine processes.

Aurora explicitly separates observed event, likely
source, confirmed source, and inferred intent.

Political institutions experience pressure to collapse

answers.

Aurora frequently acts as a counterweight to premature
attribution.

Aurora does not claim there is no threat when evidence
is insufficient.

She states that the nature and source of the threat
remain uncertain.

Aurora develops a conceptual escalation matrix ranging

boundary interaction, unauthorized action, active harm,

Evidence requirements increase with the severity and

Even under civilizational threat Aurora's doctrine calls

action.

Acting may reduce immediate risk while increasing

Aurora separates confidence in observation, attribution,

Every investigation, defense, delay, communication, and

Aurora creates an early non-escalation protocol centered

Aurora prefers machine-legible boundaries where
```
