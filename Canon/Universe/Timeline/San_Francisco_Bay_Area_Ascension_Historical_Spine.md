- bridges
- highways
- rail
- ferries
- ports
- tunnels
- regional transit

# 7. The Regional Dependency Problem

The Bay Area is extraordinarily capable.

But much of what keeps it alive comes from outside the immediate metropolitan region.

It depends upon extended systems for:

- water
- electricity
- food
- fuel
- manufactured goods
- replacement equipment

This creates the defining contradiction:

> **One of the world's most technologically sophisticated regions remains dependent on mountains, rivers, farms, transmission lines, roads, ports, pipes, and people far beyond Silicon Valley.**

# 8. Water Before Everything

The Bay Area cannot be understood without water.

Different parts of the region rely on different systems.

San Francisco's water history is especially connected to:

**Hetch Hetchy.**

The broader region depends on combinations of:

- imported surface water
- reservoirs
- local watersheds
- groundwater
- treatment infrastructure
- pumping
- regional distribution

Future survival therefore depends on infrastructure extending far beyond the Bay itself.

# 9. Hetch Hetchy As Historical Anchor

The Hetch Hetchy system becomes one of the most important physical relationships in Bay Area Collapse history.

```text

        │

        ▼
```

This is precisely the kind of real-world systemic connection Project Ascension should preserve.
