# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the canonical:

# TEMPORAL ARCHITECTURE

for:

    PROJECT ASCENSION.

Its purpose is not yet to answer:

> What year did everything happen?

Its purpose is first to establish:

> **How must time, history, dates, uncertainty, generations, regional events, Aurora memory, and story chronology fit together so that those years can later be assigned safely?**
