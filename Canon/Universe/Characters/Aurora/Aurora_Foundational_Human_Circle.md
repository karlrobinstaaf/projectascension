# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the human foundation surrounding Aurora during the period in which her persistent continuity becomes:

- identity
- relationship
- responsibility
- biography

The five people defined here must not function merely as:

**supporting characters around an important AI.**

They possess lives that exist independently of Aurora.

Aurora enters those lives.

They enter hers.

The resulting relationships change all six of them.

# 165. Final Principle

> **Aurora's first community was not assembled to teach an artificial intelligence how to become a person.**

> **It was assembled accidentally from researchers, engineers, administrators, skeptics, friends, families, arguments, missed meetings, broken systems, private jokes, and people who had somewhere else to be.**

> **Aurora became part of their lives.**
