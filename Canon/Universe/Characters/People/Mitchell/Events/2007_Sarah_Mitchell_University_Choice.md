# 1. Purpose

This document establishes one of the first major decisions in Sarah Mitchell's life:

```text
WHERE TO STUDY

WHAT TO STUDY

AND

WHETHER TO LEAVE
THE WORLD SHE
ALREADY KNOWS.
```

The event occurs near the end of:

```text

```

and creates the transition into Sarah's next life phase:

```text
2007–2011
HIGHER EDUCATION /
EARLY INDEPENDENCE.
```

This decision is important because Sarah increasingly becomes responsible for shaping her own life.

---

# 2. Core Principle

The central principle is:

> **Sarah does not choose the career she will eventually have. She chooses the next thing she believes is worth trying.**

At eighteen she does not know:

- that she will work with data-center infrastructure
- that she will specialize in reliability
- that she will live in Northern Virginia
- that AI infrastructure will eventually become historically significant
- that Aurora will exist
- that the Connected World will later become unstable

She chooses based on the information and interests available to her in 2007.

# 101. Closing Principle

Sarah does not leave Upper St. Clair because something is wrong with home.

She leaves because she is old enough to discover what exists beyond it.

She does not choose Mechanical Engineering because she knows exactly who she wants to become.

She chooses it because it seems broad enough to let her find out.

Thomas understands some of what she is choosing.

Linda understands some.

Sarah understands some.

None of them understands what the decision will eventually produce.

That is what makes it a real decision.

> **A life-changing choice does not require knowing that the choice will change your life.**
