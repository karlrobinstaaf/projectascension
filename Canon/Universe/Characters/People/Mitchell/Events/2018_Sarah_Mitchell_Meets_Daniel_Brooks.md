# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the first meeting between:

```text
SARAH MITCHELL

AND

DANIEL BROOKS.
```

Until this Event:

```text
SARAH'S LIFE

AND

DANIEL'S LIFE

HAVE DEVELOPED
INDEPENDENTLY.
```

They have:

- different families
- different childhoods
- different education
- different professions
- different friends
- different homes
- different adult histories

They happen to live in the same broad region.

This Event is the moment those separate histories first intersect.

# 119. Closing Principle

The evening ends.

Daniel asks for Sarah's number.

Sarah gives it to him.

Sarah sees his name on her phone.

She can answer.

Or not.

She answers.
