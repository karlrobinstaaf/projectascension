# 2. Core Principle

The central principle for this period is:

> **Sarah begins making decisions about her future before she fully understands what that future will require.**

At fourteen, much of her life is still structured by:

- parents
- school
- family
- geography
- schedules
- available opportunities

By eighteen, Sarah has considerably greater influence over:

- friendships
- activities
- privacy
- academic priorities
- time
- relationships
- future education
- where she may eventually live

This is a transition in agency.

It is not complete independence.
