# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the developing relationship between:

```text
SARAH MITCHELL

AND

DANIEL BROOKS
```

following their first meeting in 2018.

The meeting established only:

```text
AWARENESS

+

POSITIVE INTEREST

+

DIRECT CONTACT

+

A DECISION
TO MEET AGAIN.
```

This document asks the next question:

> **What happens when two people who enjoyed meeting each other begin discovering what the other person is actually like?**

---

# 2. Core Principle

The central principle is:

> **A relationship is not created by compatibility. It is created by accumulated interaction and continued choice.**

Therefore:

```text
ATTRACTION

        ↓

REPEATED
INTERACTION

        ↓

GREATER
KNOWLEDGE

        ↓

EXPECTATIONS

        ↓

DIFFERENCES

        ↓

DISAGREEMENT

        ↓

REPAIR OR
WITHDRAWAL

        ↓

TRUST

        ↓

COMMITMENT
```

The process is not automatic.

At every stage:

```text
THE RELATIONSHIP
CAN CHANGE

OR

END.
```

# 3. Relationship Ownership

Sarah remains an independent Character.

Daniel remains an independent Character.

The relationship is a third state structure.

```text
SARAH

        │
        │
        ↓

SARAH ↔ DANIEL
RELATIONSHIP

        ↑
        │
```

The Relationship does not own:

- Sarah's personality
- Daniel's personality
- Sarah's beliefs
- Daniel's beliefs
- Sarah's goals
- Daniel's goals
- either Character's profession
- either Character's memories

It owns the evolving interpersonal state between them.
