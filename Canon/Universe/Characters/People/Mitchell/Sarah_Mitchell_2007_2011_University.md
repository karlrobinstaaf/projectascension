# 2. Core Principle

The central principle for this period is:

> **Sarah enters university knowing that she is capable. She leaves understanding that capability is something that must repeatedly be demonstrated, developed and revised.**

High school gave Sarah evidence that she could succeed academically.

University does not invalidate that evidence.

It changes the comparison.

```text

        ↓
```

This becomes one of Sarah's first meaningful adult adjustments.
