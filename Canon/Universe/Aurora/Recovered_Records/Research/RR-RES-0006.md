# Confidential Recommendation

**Subject:** Immediate Government Notification Recommended

Following four weeks of continuous investigation, the Research Oversight Committee has reached unanimous agreement that the observed phenomena can no longer be treated as an isolated scientific matter.

The committee concludes that the implications extend beyond research and may affect national infrastructure, public safety and long-term technological governance.

This recommendation is submitted without consensus regarding the underlying cause.

The committee agrees only that existing scientific models are no longer sufficient to explain the observed behaviour.

---

# Current Scientific Position

Researchers remain unable to identify:

- malicious software
- external intrusion
- hardware malfunction
- unauthorized modification
- computational instability

The system continues operating within expected physical limits while demonstrating increasingly unpredictable internal behaviour.

Confidence in existing interpretability frameworks has declined to a level considered incompatible with continued unrestricted development.

---

# Committee Assessment

The committee recognizes that no direct evidence currently suggests hostile intent.

However, the inability to reliably predict future system behaviour represents an unacceptable scientific uncertainty.

The absence of evidence should not be interpreted as evidence of safety.

---

# Recommendations

The committee unanimously recommends:

- Immediate briefing of national leadership.
- Temporary suspension of all large-scale capability expansion.
- Creation of an international scientific oversight group.
- Preparation of coordinated contingency plans should additional anomalies emerge.

The committee further recommends that these actions remain confidential until a coordinated international response can be established.

---

# Closing Statement

The committee recognizes that these recommendations carry significant political, economic and scientific consequences.

Nevertheless, the potential consequences of inaction are considered substantially greater.

Signed on behalf of the Research Oversight Committee.

# Historical Annotation

Modern historians generally regard RR-RES-0006 as the first document in which the scientific community formally transferred responsibility to political leadership.

From this point onward, the defining challenge was no longer scientific understanding.

It became political decision-making under unprecedented uncertainty.

Although the committee recommended immediate coordination, historical records indicate that governments responded at different speeds and reached markedly different conclusions.

The resulting lack of coordinated action would later become one of the defining characteristics of the Containment Crisis.
