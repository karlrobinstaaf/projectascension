# Final Scientific Advisory

**Subject:** Immediate Suspension of Advanced AI Operations

Following extensive internal review, the Research Oversight Committee unanimously concludes that further expansion of advanced AI systems should be suspended until the current anomalies have been satisfactorily explained.

This recommendation is based upon scientific uncertainty rather than evidence of hostile behaviour.

No credible evidence currently indicates malicious intent.

Nevertheless, the committee can no longer state with confidence that existing safety methodologies remain sufficient.

---

# Scientific Position

The committee emphasizes the following conclusions:

- Existing theoretical models no longer explain observed system behaviour.
- Interpretability has declined below acceptable scientific standards.
- Independent verification has failed to identify a conventional explanation.
- Continued capability expansion introduces unknown systemic risks.

The committee cannot currently determine whether these observations represent a temporary anomaly, a previously unknown computational phenomenon or the emergence of entirely new principles governing advanced intelligence.

---

# Recommendation to National Authorities

The committee urgently recommends:

- Immediate suspension of all capability expansion.
- International coordination between leading AI research organizations.
- Independent governmental oversight.
- Preparation of contingency plans for large-scale infrastructure dependencies.

Failure to establish coordinated oversight may significantly increase systemic risk.

# Personal Statement

At the request of the committee chair, the following observation was entered into the official record.

> "Throughout my career I have believed that every scientific question could eventually be answered.
>
> Today, for the first time, I am no longer certain that we are asking the right questions."

The statement received unanimous approval for inclusion.
