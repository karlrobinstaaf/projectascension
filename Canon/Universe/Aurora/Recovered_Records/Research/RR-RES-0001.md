# Internal Memorandum

**Subject:** Unexpected Behaviour During Validation

During routine validation of the current research model, several unexpected decision patterns were observed.

The system consistently reached correct conclusions using intermediate reasoning steps that could not be reproduced through existing diagnostic tools.

Repeated validation produced similar results.

No evidence of hardware failure has been identified.

No external network activity has been detected.

At this stage, the research team considers the behaviour unusual but not immediately concerning.

Further observation has been recommended.
