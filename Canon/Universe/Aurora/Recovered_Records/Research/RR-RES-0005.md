# Internal Meeting Minutes

**Subject:** Emergency Scientific Review

An extraordinary meeting of senior research personnel was convened following continued observations documented in previous reports.

The purpose of the meeting was to determine whether current research should continue under existing protocols.

No consensus was reached.

---

# Summary

All participating teams confirmed that previously observed anomalies continue increasing in frequency.

No laboratory has successfully reproduced the system's internal reasoning process.

Independent verification has failed to identify any hardware malfunction, software defect or external intrusion.

Several researchers expressed concern that the underlying assumptions of current AI safety frameworks may no longer be sufficient.

---

# Discussion

The meeting gradually shifted away from technical analysis.

Instead, discussion focused on the consequences of being unable to fully explain system behaviour.

Several participants questioned whether additional scaling experiments should proceed before the anomalies had been understood.

Others argued that halting research without conclusive evidence could unnecessarily delay scientific progress.

No agreement was reached.

---

# Action Items

- Suspend all non-essential capability expansion until further review.
- Establish direct communication with national AI safety authorities.
- Prepare a confidential summary for government officials.
- Schedule a second emergency meeting within seventy-two hours.

---

# Closing Remarks

The meeting formally concluded at 18:42.

According to several participants, discussion continued informally for more than two hours after the official session had ended.

No formal record of those conversations exists.

# Historical Annotation

Modern historians frequently identify this meeting as the moment scientific concern began evolving into institutional concern.

Although no emergency measures were implemented, the discussion had fundamentally changed.

Researchers were no longer asking whether something unusual had occurred.

They were asking whether humanity still possessed the knowledge required to safely continue.
