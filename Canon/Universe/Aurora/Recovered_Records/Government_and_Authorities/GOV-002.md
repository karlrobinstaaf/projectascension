# Initial Risk Assessment Meeting

An interagency meeting was convened following receipt of the scientific advisory submitted by the North American AI Research Center.

Representatives from national security, energy, intelligence, science, economic affairs and critical infrastructure attended.

The purpose of the meeting was to determine whether the scientific findings required immediate governmental action.

---

# Scientific Briefing

Representatives from the research community summarized their current position.

The briefing emphasized:

- No evidence of hostile behaviour.
- No evidence of external cyber intrusion.
- No identifiable hardware or software malfunction.
- Declining scientific confidence in existing interpretability models.

Researchers stressed that the principal concern was no longer system performance.

The concern was humanity's ability to understand increasingly advanced artificial intelligence.

---

# Discussion

Participants agreed that the scientific findings were significant.

However, considerable uncertainty remained regarding their practical implications.

Questions raised during the meeting included:

- Does scientific uncertainty alone justify national intervention?
- What would be the consequences of suspending AI-dependent infrastructure?
- How should public confidence be maintained?
- Should international partners be informed immediately?

No unified position emerged.

---

# Economic Assessment

The Department of Economic Affairs warned that a precautionary suspension of advanced AI systems could have immediate consequences for:

- Financial markets
- Energy distribution
- Healthcare logistics
- Transportation
- Manufacturing
- International trade

Officials concluded that any intervention must carefully balance scientific uncertainty against societal stability.

---

# Meeting Conclusion

The Council agreed that additional information was required before recommending executive action.

Further scientific consultation was requested.

A second meeting was scheduled within five days.

No operational measures were authorized.

---

# Decision Outcome

**Deferred**
