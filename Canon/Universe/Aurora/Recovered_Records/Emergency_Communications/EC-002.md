# National Infrastructure Conservation Advisory

**Subject:** Temporary Resource Conservation Measures

Federal authorities, in coordination with state emergency management agencies and critical infrastructure operators, are issuing the following advisory to support the continued stability of essential national services.

Recent operational assessments indicate that voluntary conservation measures may improve the resilience of several interconnected infrastructure systems while ongoing maintenance and coordination efforts continue.

These measures are precautionary.

They should not be interpreted as evidence of resource shortages.

---

# Public Guidance

Citizens are encouraged to:

- Reduce unnecessary electricity consumption during peak demand periods.
- Limit non-essential vehicle travel where practical.
- Avoid unnecessary water usage during local conservation advisories.
- Maintain reasonable household supplies without excessive purchasing.
- Follow guidance issued by local authorities.

These recommendations are voluntary.

---

# Infrastructure Status

Critical services remain operational nationwide.

Infrastructure operators continue monitoring:

- Electrical generation and transmission.
- Fuel distribution.
- Telecommunications.
- Transportation networks.
- Water treatment facilities.

Localized service interruptions remain possible while coordination activities continue.

---

# Public Information

Federal authorities recognize that recent reports of temporary disruptions have created uncertainty in some communities.

Citizens are encouraged to verify information through official communication channels before acting upon unconfirmed reports circulated through social media or private messaging platforms.

Reliable public communication remains a national priority.

---

# Closing Statement

Preparedness strengthens community resilience.

Small individual actions, when taken collectively, help maintain essential services during periods of increased operational complexity.

Federal agencies continue working closely with state and local partners to ensure continuity of critical infrastructure.

# Historical Annotation

Historians generally regard EC-002 as the first public communication acknowledging that individual citizen behavior had become a factor in maintaining national infrastructure stability.

The advisory did not introduce mandatory restrictions.

Instead, it reflected a growing recognition that highly interconnected systems possessed less operational flexibility than previously assumed.

Many citizens viewed the recommendations as routine emergency preparedness.

Only later did historians recognize them as early indicators of the nation's declining infrastructure resilience.
