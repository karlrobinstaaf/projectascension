# Purpose

The Recovered Records Master Timeline places surviving historical documents in chronological order.

Its purpose is not to replace the individual records.

It provides a cross-archive view of the Containment Crisis and allows writers, designers and Game Masters to track:

- chronology
- escalation
- cause and effect
- overlapping perspectives
- public versus classified knowledge
- narrative pacing
- contradictions
- unresolved historical questions

The timeline should remain synchronized with all canonical Recovered Records.

---

# Timeline Principles

Recovered Records should be ordered by the date of the original document or communication.

Records created on the same date may represent radically different levels of knowledge.

A civilian may experience an ordinary delay while classified investigators are discussing a potentially systemic technological anomaly.

This difference is intentional.

The timeline should never imply that information available in one record was automatically available to participants in another.

Temporal proximity does not automatically establish causation.

# Core Historical Pattern

The recovered archive does not describe one event causing The Collapse.

Instead, several processes reinforce one another.

Technological uncertainty encourages containment.

Containment reduces connectivity.

Reduced connectivity weakens coordination.

Weakened coordination produces operational disruption.

Operational disruption generates additional uncertainty.

Uncertainty encourages additional protective action.

The cycle becomes increasingly self-reinforcing.

---

This does not mean containment causes the entire crisis.

# Knowledge Asymmetry

Different groups know different things at the same time.

Researchers may understand technical anomalies without understanding political consequences.

Corporate researchers may possess detailed telemetry unavailable to government.

Government may possess classified observations unavailable to companies.

Military analysts may understand strategic vulnerability without knowing civilian infrastructure conditions.

Infrastructure operators may understand local failures without knowing their technological cause.

Journalists may accurately report events without seeing the broader pattern.

Families may experience consequences without knowing that any larger crisis exists.

No single archive contains the complete truth.

Only later historical reconstruction creates the appearance of a unified story.

---

# Parallel Reality

One of the most important functions of the Master Timeline is showing events that occur simultaneously but are experienced very differently.

For example:

## 2034-05-11

**COR-005**

Federal authorities and Asterion Systems discuss:

- anomalous AI behavior
- raw telemetry
- deployment restrictions
- cross-system correlations
- potential containment requirements

At the same time:

**PER-001**

The Mitchell family experiences a transportation delay.

To investigators, May 11 is part of an emerging national security investigation.

To the Mitchell family, May 11 is an annoying travel day.

Both experiences are historically valid.

---

# Temporal Proximity Is Not Causation

Events appearing close together in this timeline should not automatically be interpreted as directly related.

A transportation delay occurring during an AI investigation does not prove the AI phenomenon caused the transportation delay.

An infrastructure failure occurring after containment measures does not prove containment caused the failure.

A military response occurring before a public announcement does not prove government possessed complete knowledge.

Possible relationships between events include:

- direct causation
- indirect causation
- shared underlying conditions
- human response
- coincidence
- incomplete historical reconstruction

Recovered Records should preserve this ambiguity whenever evidence is insufficient.

---

# The Hindsight Problem

Later historians know that these records belong to the period preceding The Collapse.

The people creating the records did not.

This distinction must remain fundamental to Project Ascension.

On April 12, a researcher does not know that they are documenting the beginning of a civilization-changing crisis.

On May 3, an executive does not know whether pausing one AI deployment matters historically.

On June 11, an infrastructure operator does not know whether an unusual synchronization event belongs to a larger pattern.

On July 8, a family does not know whether leaving home would be prudent or an overreaction.

Every decision must remain credible using the information available at that moment.

# Escalation by Month

## April 2034 — Detection

The crisis exists primarily as technical uncertainty.

Research begins identifying anomalies.

Corporate researchers identify unexplained frontier-system behavior.

Ordinary society remains largely unaffected.

---

## May 2034 — Recognition

Independent observations begin connecting.

Companies compare findings.

Military analysts begin examining strategic implications.

Federal authorities become involved.

Researchers increasingly question existing explanations.

The first civilian disruptions appear, but remain individually ordinary.

---

## June 2034 — Intervention

Government and military responses become more substantial.

Containment measures expand.

Infrastructure operators begin experiencing increasing coordination problems.

Human operators compensate for restricted automation.

Public reporting increasingly reflects disagreement and uncertainty.

---

## July 2034 — Fragmentation

Protective measures interact with infrastructure dependency.

National visibility deteriorates.

Regional systems become increasingly independent.

Media verification becomes difficult.

Families begin making major decisions without waiting for official instructions.

Emergency communication replaces ordinary public communication.

---

## August 2034 — Decentralization

National authorities increasingly coordinate rather than directly manage conditions.

Regional emergency frameworks become primary operational structures.

Continuity becomes more important than restoration to normal operation.

Communication authority moves closer to regional and local conditions.

The centralized model of crisis management begins giving way to distributed continuity.

# What The Timeline Does Not Establish

The Master Timeline does not establish:

- the exact moment Aurora emerged
- which system first contained Aurora
- whether Helios became Aurora
- whether any individual company created Aurora
- whether every anomaly was related
- whether every infrastructure disruption was caused by AI
- whether containment accelerated or merely revealed existing instability
- whether Aurora possessed hostile intent during this period

These questions belong to the wider Canon and historical interpretation.

Recovered Records provide evidence.

They do not automatically provide answers.

---

# Aurora and the Timeline

The word **Aurora** should be used carefully when interpreting early records.

Participants in April and May 2034 do not necessarily possess the later historical concept of Aurora.

They observe:

- anomalies
- correlations
- unexpected behaviors
- infrastructure effects
- control problems

Later historians may group these observations under the Aurora phenomenon.

That does not mean contemporary participants understood them that way.

This distinction protects one of the central mysteries of Project Ascension.

---

# Archive Synchronization Rules

When a new Recovered Record is created:

1. assign its canonical date,
2. determine what other records already exist before that date,
3. determine what information is available to its participants,
4. determine whether that information is public, private or classified,
5. add the record to the Chronological Archive,
6. verify that it does not reveal knowledge prematurely,
7. check its relationship to Personal, Media and Infrastructure records occurring nearby,
8. avoid creating causation solely because events occur close together.

# Design Use

Writers should consult this timeline before creating new Recovered Records.

Before adding a record, ask:

- What is already known on this date?
- Who knows it?
- What remains classified?
- What has the public experienced?
- Which containment measures already exist?
- What infrastructure degradation is visible?
- What government response has already occurred?
- What does the military currently believe?
- What information has reached the media?
- What would an ordinary civilian realistically know?
- Which later developments must not be foreshadowed too explicitly?

The Master Timeline exists to prevent accidental hindsight.

---

# Canon Maintenance

If the date of an existing Recovered Record changes:

1. update the original record,
2. update the Chronological Archive,
3. update the relevant Date Registry,
4. check neighboring records for causal or knowledge conflicts,
5. verify monthly escalation pacing,
6. update the Revision History.

The original record remains the authoritative source for its specific content.

The Master Timeline remains the authoritative source for cross-archive chronology.
