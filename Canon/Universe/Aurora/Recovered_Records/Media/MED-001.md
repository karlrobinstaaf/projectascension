# Researchers Call for Increased Oversight of Advanced AI Systems

**Washington, D.C. — National News Network**

A group of leading artificial intelligence researchers has submitted a confidential advisory to federal authorities recommending increased governmental oversight of advanced AI development.

According to officials familiar with the matter, the advisory does not describe an immediate threat to public safety.

Instead, researchers have expressed concern that recent advances in artificial intelligence are progressing faster than existing scientific methods can fully evaluate.

Government representatives declined to comment on the contents of the advisory but confirmed that it is currently undergoing interagency review.

Officials emphasized that no emergency measures have been implemented.

## Expert Opinion

Professor Daniel Harper, a specialist in computational systems at Stanford University who is not involved in the advisory, urged caution against unnecessary speculation.

> "Scientific uncertainty should not be mistaken for evidence of danger. As these systems become more sophisticated, it's natural that our methods for studying them must also evolve."
