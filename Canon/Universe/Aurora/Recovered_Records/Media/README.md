---

# Purpose

The Media Records document how journalists, broadcasters, independent investigators and social platforms reported the unfolding Containment Crisis.

Unlike Research Records, these documents rarely contain complete information.

Unlike Government Records, they are written for public audiences.

The collection illustrates how uncertainty spreads through society as facts become increasingly difficult to verify.

---

# Scope

Media Records include:

- Television news
- Newspapers
- Online journalism
- Independent investigations
- Emergency broadcasts
- Press conferences
- Social media archives
- Fact-checking reports

---

# Guiding Principle

Media Records should never deliberately lie.

They should reflect the difficulty of reporting accurately during rapidly changing events.

Different sources often reach different conclusions while acting in good faith.

The reader should gradually lose confidence in certainty—not in journalism itself.
