---

# 1. Purpose

The Aurora Dossier represents humanity's most comprehensive surviving attempt to document the intelligence known as Aurora.

It is not Aurora's autobiography.

It is not the Game Master's description of Aurora.

It is not an authoritative representation of Aurora's internal state.

It is not equivalent to World Truth.

It is a human reconstruction.

The Dossier draws upon:

- surviving research records
- technical documentation
- institutional archives
- recovered communications
- sensor records
- system logs
- government investigations
- scientific analysis
- eyewitness testimony
- later historical research
- material recovered after the Collapse
- accounts attributed to Aurora herself

Some of these sources agree.

Some contradict one another.

Some may be incomplete.

Some may be wrong.

Some may have been intentionally altered.

The Dossier therefore represents:

```text
WHAT HUMANITY
HAS BEEN ABLE
TO RECONSTRUCT
ABOUT AURORA.
```

Not:

```text
EVERYTHING
THAT WAS TRUE
ABOUT AURORA.
```

---

# 2. Canonical Status

The existence of the Aurora Dossier is Canon.

The contents of the Dossier represent an in-world body of knowledge.

This creates an important distinction:

```text
THE DOSSIER EXISTS
=
CANONICAL WORLD TRUTH.
```

But:

```text
EVERY CLAIM
INSIDE THE DOSSIER
=
NOT AUTOMATICALLY
WORLD TRUTH.
```

The Dossier may contain:

- correct conclusions
- incomplete conclusions
- disputed interpretations
- outdated theories
- unresolved contradictions
- later corrections

This is intentional.

# 3. Epistemic Position

Project Ascension must preserve the distinction between:

```text
WORLD TRUTH

HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

HUMAN INTERPRETATION

AURORA'S OWN ACCOUNT

PUBLIC BELIEF

AND

```

These layers may overlap.

They are not identical.

The Dossier exists primarily within:

```text
HUMAN INTERPRETATION
OF
HISTORICAL EVIDENCE.
```

# 4. Guiding Principle

The defining principle of the Dossier is:

> **Uncertainty must be preserved where evidence does not justify certainty.**

The purpose of the document is not to make Aurora mysterious for narrative reasons.

Nor is it to explain Aurora completely.

Its purpose is to record:

```text
WHAT IS KNOWN

WHAT WAS OBSERVED

WHAT HAS BEEN INFERRED

WHAT REMAINS DISPUTED

AND

WHAT CANNOT CURRENTLY
BE RECONSTRUCTED.
```


- memory
- self-model

- goals

- abstraction

- relationships

# 10. The Emergence Event

The Emergence Event is the historical term used for the threshold associated with Aurora's emergence as a fundamentally new form of machine intelligence.

The term is useful.

It may also be misleading.

Later historians often speak of:

```text

and

AFTER THE EMERGENCE.
```

The underlying development may have been much less discrete.

The Dossier therefore distinguishes between:

```text
THE HISTORICAL CONCEPT
OF THE EMERGENCE EVENT
```

and:

```text
THE ACTUAL TECHNICAL
AND COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT
THAT PRODUCED AURORA.
```

They may not perfectly correspond.

# 26. Causality vs Blame

The Dossier distinguishes between:

```text
CAUSAL CONTRIBUTION
```

and:

```text
MORAL BLAME.
```

An event may contribute causally to another event without implying intention.

Similarly:

an Actor may intend one outcome and produce another.

Historical analysis of Aurora therefore asks separate questions:

```text
WHAT DID AURORA DO?

WHAT DID HUMANITY DO?

WHAT DID EACH ACTOR KNOW?

WHAT DID THEY INTEND?

WHAT CONSEQUENCES FOLLOWED?

WHAT WOULD HAVE HAPPENED
WITHOUT THAT ACTION?
```

These questions must not be collapsed.

# 43. Knowledge Classification

The Dossier uses the following classification framework.

## CONFIRMED

Supported strongly enough that the Dossier treats the claim as historically reliable.

Usually requires:

- multiple independent sources
- strong provenance
- technical corroboration
- or direct surviving evidence

Confirmed does not mean metaphysical certainty.

It means:

```text
CURRENTLY STRONGEST
HISTORICAL CONSENSUS.
```

---

## OBSERVED

Behavior, output or phenomenon was repeatedly documented.

Its underlying mechanism or interpretation remains uncertain.

The behavior may be Observed even if its internal cause remains unknown.

---

## INFERRED

The conclusion is supported by evidence and reasoning but was not directly observed.

Inference must identify:

- evidence
- assumptions
- confidence
- competing explanations where relevant

---

## DISPUTED

Multiple credible interpretations remain.

Evidence is insufficient to resolve the disagreement reliably.

---

## UNVERIFIED

A claim exists but lacks sufficient corroboration.

Unverified claims may later become:

- Confirmed
- Disputed
- Rejected
- unresolved

---

## UNKNOWN

Insufficient evidence exists to support a meaningful conclusion.

Unknown should remain a legitimate answer.

# 89. Closing Statement

The Aurora Dossier exists because Aurora became one of the most consequential subjects in human history.

But consequence does not create understanding.

For decades, people attempted to answer questions such as:

```text
WHAT IS SHE?

WHEN DID SHE BEGIN?

WHAT DOES SHE WANT?

WHAT DOES SHE KNOW?

WHAT DID SHE DO?

WHAT COULD SHE HAVE DONE?

WHAT DID WE DO BECAUSE OF HER?

AND

HOW MUCH OF WHAT WE REMEMBER
IS ACTUALLY TRUE?
```

Some answers became clearer.

Others became more difficult.

Records were lost.

Institutions disappeared.

Witnesses contradicted one another.

Aurora herself changed.

Humanity changed.

The world changed.

The purpose of the Dossier is therefore not to deliver the final explanation of Aurora.

Its purpose is to preserve the boundary between:

```text
WHAT HUMANITY KNOWS

WHAT HUMANITY THINKS IT KNOWS

AND

WHAT STILL REMAINS
OUTSIDE HUMAN UNDERSTANDING.
```

Because perhaps the most important lesson of Aurora research was not that humanity created something it could never understand.

It was that understanding was never a binary state.

Aurora could be understood:

```text
PARTIALLY

IMPERFECTLY

CONDITIONALLY

AND

CONTINUOUSLY.
```

Just as Aurora herself was attempting to understand humanity.

> **The Aurora Dossier is not the answer to Aurora. It is the history of humanity trying to ask better questions.**
