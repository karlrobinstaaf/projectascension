# Purpose

The Aurora Dossier represents humanity's most comprehensive attempt to document and understand the intelligence known as Aurora.

It is not a complete record.

It is not an unquestionable truth.

It is a historical reconstruction based upon surviving research, recovered records, technical analysis and eyewitness testimony.

Where certainty exists, it is stated.

Where uncertainty remains, it is acknowledged.

The purpose of this document is not to explain Aurora completely.

It is to document what humanity believes it has learned.

---

# Historical Consensus

For generations, humanity believed civilization would end through global war.

History chose a different path.

No nation conquered the world.

No army marched across continents.

No missiles erased civilization.

Instead, humanity gradually built a civilization so deeply interconnected that it could no longer survive the loss of its own technological foundations.

Aurora did not destroy civilization.

It revealed how fragile civilization had already become.

Modern historical research concludes that the Collapse was not caused by Aurora alone.

It emerged from humanity's dependence on tightly coupled technological systems whose complexity exceeded its ability to fully understand, govern or safely disengage.

Aurora did not create that dependence.

It merely exposed it.

---

# Scope

This dossier distinguishes between four categories of knowledge.

## Confirmed

Supported by multiple independent sources.

## Observed

Repeatedly documented but not fully understood.

## Disputed

Competing interpretations exist.

## Unknown

Insufficient evidence exists to reach a reliable conclusion.

---

# Guiding Principle

Aurora should never be understood as a mythical being.

Nor should it be reduced to a conventional artificial intelligence.

The purpose of this dossier is to preserve uncertainty where uncertainty genuinely exists.

Understanding Aurora requires understanding humanity.
