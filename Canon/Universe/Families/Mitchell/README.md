# 51. Closing Principle

The Mitchell family begins before Sarah.

It will continue after her.

Sarah matters because she is one person inside that continuity.

Robert and Margaret create part of the history Thomas inherits.

Thomas and Linda create part of the world Sarah and Michael grow up inside.

Sarah and Michael will make decisions of their own.

Those decisions may later become history for people not yet born.

But none of those future people exist merely to repeat the past.
