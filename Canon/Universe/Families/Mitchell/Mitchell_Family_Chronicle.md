# 1. Purpose

The Mitchell Family Chronicle records the historical spine of the Mitchell family.

It answers:

> **What happened to the Mitchell family, when did it happen, who was involved and what consequences followed?**

The Chronicle exists to preserve causal continuity across generations.

It is not:

- a Character biography
- a collection of memories
- a family mythology
- a Relationship ledger
- a psychological profile
- a list of future plot events
- a predetermined family destiny

The core principle is:

> **Record what has happened. Do not write what history has not yet produced.**

---

# 2. Chronicle Architecture

The Chronicle follows:

```text
WORLD CONDITIONS
        ↓
EVENT
        ↓
PEOPLE INVOLVED
        ↓
DIRECT CONSEQUENCES
        ↓
FAMILY CONSEQUENCES
        ↓
LATER HISTORY
```

Where relevant, an event may later produce:

```text
MEMORY

RETELLING

FAMILY STORY

REPUTATION

RESPONSIBILITY

PROPERTY CHANGE

RELATIONSHIP CHANGE

MIGRATION

FUTURE CONSEQUENCE
```

Those states remain owned by their authoritative systems.

The Chronicle records the historical connection between them.

---

# 3. Chronicle vs World Truth

The Chronicle should contain established family-relevant historical facts.

However:

```text
MITCHELL FAMILY CHRONICLE

≠

COMPLETE WORLD TRUTH.
```

World Truth may contain:

- causes unknown to the family
- events the family never learns about
- information misunderstood by participants
- consequences not recognized until much later

The Chronicle therefore records the Mitchell family's historical path through the world.

It does not claim omniscience.

---

# 4. Chronicle vs Memory

An event recorded here does not mean every Mitchell remembers it.

Example:

```text
EVENT
        ↓
ROBERT EXPERIENCES IT
        ↓
ROBERT REMEMBERS IT
        ↓
ROBERT TELLS THOMAS
        ↓
THOMAS REMEMBERS THE STORY
        ↓
THOMAS TELLS SARAH
        ↓
SARAH REMEMBERS
WHAT THOMAS TOLD HER.
```

Sarah does not possess Robert's Memory.

She possesses her own Memory of receiving information about Robert's past.

---

# 5. Chronicle vs Family Story

The Chronicle may record:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED
```

while the family may later tell:

```text
WHAT THEY BELIEVE
HAPPENED.
```

These may diverge.

Therefore:

```text
HISTORICAL EVENT
        ≠
PERSONAL MEMORY
        ≠
RETELLING
        ≠
FAMILY STORY
        ≠
DESCENDANT BELIEF.
```

This distinction must remain intact across generations.

---

# 6. Date Precision

Chronicle dates use different precision levels.

```text
1989
=
YEAR ESTABLISHED

1989-XX-XX
=
EXACT DATE NOT YET ESTABLISHED

~1985
=
APPROXIMATE YEAR

EARLY 1980s
=
ESTABLISHED PERIOD,
EXACT YEAR UNKNOWN
```

Do not invent exact dates merely to make the Chronicle appear complete.

Unknown precision is valid Canon.

# 7. Historical Status

Chronicle entries may conceptually exist at different levels of certainty.

## Established

The event is canonical.

## Approximate

The event is canonical, but exact timing remains unresolved.

## Established at Broad Level

The historical direction is canonical, but details remain unresolved.

## Development Pending

The period is known to require later development, but no event should yet be treated as established.

Development Pending sections are not future history.

They identify unresolved chronological space.
