The locations recorded here represent contemporary reference information.

```text

```

Its physical location remains historically relevant.

# 4. Republic's Useful Spatial Pattern

One particularly useful feature of Republic is that several important player resources are concentrated along or close to:

```text
CLARK AVENUE
```

while other services extend toward the highway approaches.

This means the town can remain physically small while still providing meaningful decisions about:

```text
WALKING

VEHICLE USE

RESOURCE ACCESS

AND

WHETHER TO
LEAVE TOWN.
```

They need medicine?
