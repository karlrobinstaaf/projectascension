# 1. Purpose

This document converts the broader Geographic Canon for Republic into a usable spatial model for gameplay.

It answers:

```text
WHERE ARE
THE PLAYERS?

WHAT ROADS
EXIST?

WHERE DO
THEY LEAD?

WHAT PARTS
OF TOWN
CAN THEY REACH?

WHAT PHYSICAL
OPTIONS EXIST?
```

It does not yet answer:

```text
WHO IS
DANGEROUS?

WHAT HAS
HAPPENED?

WHICH ROAD
IS SAFE?

WHAT SHOULD
THE PLAYERS DO?
```

Those questions belong to World State, Characters, Seeds and player decisions.

---

# 2. Baseline Rule

> **The map describes where things are. It does not describe what they mean.**

A road is geography.

Whether the road is open is World State.

Whether somebody claims the road is open is Information.

Whether the Characters believe them is Belief.

# 4. Regional Position

Republic is located in northeastern Washington within Ferry County.

The town exists within a mountainous and forested landscape where regional movement is strongly shaped by roads, terrain and distance.

Republic is not an isolated game level.

Real geography continues in every direction.

---

# 5. Primary Transportation Structure

Two real Washington State Routes form the most important regional transportation structure around Republic:

```text

SR-21

NORTH
  ↑

```

These routes form part of Geographic Canon.

Their current condition does not.

---

# 6. SR-20

State Route 20 provides Republic with an important east-west regional connection.

For gameplay, SR-20 represents access toward different parts of northeastern Washington and the wider regional road network.

The route's real alignment should remain authoritative.

---

# 7. SR-21

State Route 21 provides the major north-south axis through the Republic region.

This route becomes particularly important to VS-001 because players may independently decide that moving south offers their best chance of reaching:

- more populated areas
- functioning communications
- larger infrastructure
- reliable information
- family or institutional contact

However:

> **SR-21 South is an option, not an objective.**

---

# 8. Northern Movement

Travel north remains physically possible when World State permits it.

The northern road network leads toward increasingly remote communities and eventually toward the international border region.

Players choosing north are not choosing the "wrong" direction.

The simulation follows them.

---

# 9. Eastern Movement

SR-20 provides an eastern geographic option.

Players may choose east because of:

- information
- known contacts
- road reports
- resources
- personal reasons
- avoidance of another route

No direction receives narrative privilege.

---

# 11. Clark Avenue

Clark Avenue is one of the principal local spatial anchors for Republic.

It should become a major street-level reference for the playable town.

Important views of Republic should preserve its real relationship to:

- intersecting streets
- surrounding buildings
- terrain
- approaches to the state highway network

This gives Project Ascension a useful verified local anchor when aligning the real highway geometry with the local street network.

# 15. North Approach

The North Approach represents the transition between Republic and the road network extending north.

Gameplay-relevant physical characteristics may include:

- decreasing building density
- increasing vegetation
- highway access
- scattered properties
- longer sightlines along roads
- rapid transition toward rural terrain

Exact properties should be resolved from geographic data before use.

---

# 16. Central Republic

Central Republic represents the highest initial concentration of:

- streets
- commercial buildings
- civic activity
- public space
- information sources
- human interaction

For VS-001, this will likely become the highest-resolution part of the initial map.

---

# 17. Southern Republic

Southern Republic provides the transition between the central settlement and routes leading farther south.

This area is strategically important because players considering departure may pass through it.

The actual geography should determine:

- intersections
- properties
- road visibility
- available services
- possible stopping points

rather than narrative convenience.

---

# 18. South Approach

The South Approach represents the point where Republic increasingly gives way to regional road travel.

Psychologically, this may become an important threshold:

```text
BEHIND:

KNOWN TOWN


AHEAD:

UNKNOWN ROAD.
```

This emotional meaning emerges from gameplay.

It is not a geographic property.

# 28. Walking

Republic's small scale means walking may remain practical within parts of town.

Walking changes:

- what Characters can carry
- weather exposure
- travel time
- visibility
- social contact
- access to the vehicle

Leaving the vehicle behind is therefore a meaningful state change.

- vehicle condition
- access

# 46. Geographic Gameplay Principle

> **Players should be able to solve some problems simply by understanding the physical world.**

Examples:

- remembering another route
- estimating travel distance
- recognizing a bottleneck
- using terrain
- choosing where to leave a vehicle
- identifying a likely communication point

This rewards observation without requiring artificial game mechanics.

# 51. Closing Principle

Republic is not being designed around the players.

It already exists.

The roads were built before they arrived.

The mountains do not move for them.

The buildings stand where history placed them.

The distance south remains the distance south.

The forest does not care what objective the players have.

The Game Master does not construct convenient geography.

Instead, the players enter a place.

They learn it.

They misunderstand parts of it.

They remember others.

They leave possessions somewhere.

They discover routes.

They make decisions based on incomplete information.

And eventually they may know Republic not as a game map—

but as a place they have been.

> **The geography is real. What happens there belongs to Project Ascension.**
