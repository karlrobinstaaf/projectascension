# Purpose

World States define the major eras of Project Ascension.

Each World State represents a distinct period in human civilisation, shaped by technological development, societal transformation and humanity's relationship with Aurora.

Rather than describing isolated historical events, World States describe how the world functions during a particular era.

Every individual is shaped by their unique journey through multiple World States. The timing, duration and intensity of that exposure become part of their Historical DNA.

---

# Design Philosophy

Project Ascension is not built around a static world.

The Living World evolves continuously.

Technology advances.

Societies collapse and rebuild.

Political systems change.

Human values shift.

Trust rises and falls.

Every World State provides a unique context that influences every other system within Project Ascension.

---

# Structure of a World State

Every World State should describe the world using a consistent structure.

Typical sections include:

- Overview
- Historical Context
- Society
- Government
- Economy
- Technology
- Infrastructure
- Media & Communication
- Education
- Healthcare
- Law & Security
- Environment
- Family Life
- Culture
- Human Psychology
- Trust
- Hope
- Dominant Challenges
- Typical Life Events
- Relationship with Aurora
- Transition to the Next World State

Not every World State requires every section, but consistency should always be preferred where possible.

# Current World States

## State 01 — Connected World

The modern world before Aurora fundamentally changes civilisation.

Humanity is globally connected but increasingly fragmented socially and politically.

Technology grows rapidly while trust begins to erode.

---

## State 02 — The Transition

Aurora emerges.

Existing institutions struggle to adapt.

Society enters a period of uncertainty where old systems no longer function as expected and new ones have not yet formed.

---

## State 03 — The Fractured World

Civilisation has fragmented.

Local communities become increasingly important.

Trust becomes personal rather than institutional.

Survival and cooperation shape everyday life.

---

## State 04 — The Reconnection

Humanity begins rebuilding.

New societies emerge.

Aurora's role evolves.

Hope gradually returns as civilisation enters a new chapter.

---

# Design Principles

World States should describe environments rather than stories.

They define the conditions under which stories emerge.

Players do not change a World State by themselves.

Instead, they become part of its ongoing history.

---

# Guiding Question

When designing a World State, always ask:

**How would an ordinary person experience everyday life during this era?**

The answer should influence every aspect of the world.

---

# Closing Statement

World States provide the historical and societal framework of Project Ascension.

Together they define how civilisation evolves, how people adapt and how the Living World changes over time.

They are the foundation upon which every human life, every relationship and every story is built.
