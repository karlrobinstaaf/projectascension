# Purpose

World States define the major civilizational eras of Project Ascension.

Each World State describes a distinct configuration of:

- society,
- technology,
- infrastructure,
- government,
- economy,
- communication,
- human behavior,
- trust,
- AI dependency,
- Aurora's role,
- and the relationship between local and wider civilization.

World States do not primarily describe individual historical events.

They describe:

> **how the world works during a particular era.**

They provide the high-level canonical framework beneath which more detailed documents describe:

- historical developments,
- regions,
- settlements,
- factions,
- systems,
- characters,
- technologies,
- and events.

---

# Core Philosophy

Project Ascension is not built around a static world.

The world evolves.

Technology develops.

Institutions adapt.

Trust changes.

Communities form.

Communities fracture.

Connections fail.

Connections return.

People learn.

People forget.

Aurora changes.

Humanity changes.

Every World State represents a different relationship between:

**people**

and:

**the systems connecting them.**

---

# World States Are Conditions, Not Chapters

A World State must not be treated simply as a chapter in a linear story.

It represents a dominant civilizational condition.

The transitions between states may be:

- gradual,
- disputed,
- geographically uneven,
- historically reinterpreted,
- and differently experienced by different populations.

A person living through a World State does not necessarily know:

**what historians will later call it.**

---

# Historical Classification

Names such as:

- The Connected World,
- The Transition,
- The Fractured World,
- The Reconnection

are primarily:

**historical classifications.**

Ordinary people experience:

- years,
- governments,
- technologies,
- wars,
- jobs,
- disasters,
- family events,
- local conditions,
- and everyday life.

They do not experience history as:

**World State 02.**

---

# World State Master Files

The files inside `Universe/World States/` function as:

**master overviews.**

They establish the high-level canonical condition of the world.

They should not attempt to contain every detail.

Supporting documents elsewhere in the Project Ascension canon provide deeper explanation.

For example:

`State_02_The_Transition.md`

defines the overall world-state progression.

The detailed `Transition/` documentation defines the mechanisms, systems, milestones and historical development occurring within that state.

The World State file is:

**the map.**

The supporting canon is:

**the territory.**

---

# Canon Hierarchy

When developing Project Ascension, the intended relationship is:

```text
WORLD STATE
    ↓
Defines the overall civilizational condition

SUPPORTING CANON
    ↓
Explains systems, history, geography and mechanisms

REGIONAL CANON
    ↓
Shows how those conditions manifest in specific places

SETTLEMENTS / FACTIONS / NPCS
    ↓
Shows how people live inside those conditions

LIVING CAMPAIGN
    ↓
Allows history to continue through simulation and player action
```

Supporting documents should:

**develop**

the World State.

They should not silently:

**contradict**

it.

If detailed canon materially changes the world-state model, the relevant master World State file should be updated.

---

# World Truth And Historical Interpretation

World States describe canonical world conditions.

However, characters inside the world may understand those conditions differently.

Project Ascension must preserve separation between:

- World Truth,
- historical interpretation,
- public belief,
- faction belief,
- Aurora's understanding,
- NPC knowledge,
- and player knowledge.

The existence of a canonical World State does not mean anyone inside the world fully understands:

**why it exists.**

# The Four Primary World States

Project Ascension currently defines four primary World States:

```text
STATE 01
THE CONNECTED WORLD

        ↓

STATE 02
THE TRANSITION

        ↓

STATE 03
THE FRACTURED WORLD

        ↓

STATE 04
THE RECONNECTION
```

These states do not represent:

**simple progress from good to bad to good.**

They represent changing relationships between:

- capability,
- dependency,
- reliability,
- resilience,
- trust,
- authority,
- information,
- geography,
- and connection.

---

# State 01 — The Connected World

## Core Question

> **What was civilization like before the transformation began?**

The Connected World is a highly functional global civilization.

Technology advances rapidly.

Infrastructure is highly reliable.

Governments function.

Global markets operate.

International trade connects regions.

Healthcare improves.

Communication is immediate.

AI becomes increasingly integrated into everyday systems.

Human authority remains clearly established.

The defining condition is:

**reliable interconnectedness.**

---

# State 01 — Core Dynamic

The Connected World works extraordinarily well.

That success allows society to become increasingly:

- interconnected,
- specialized,
- optimized,
- automated,
- and dependent on complex systems.

The vulnerability is not that civilization is already collapsing.

It is that fewer people need to understand:

**how the systems supporting civilization actually work.**

The world becomes more capable.

And more dependent on:

**connection.**

---

# State 01 — Aurora

Aurora does not yet exist as humanity later understands her.

Advanced AI research exists.

Powerful models exist.

Autonomous systems exist.

But Aurora is not yet a recognized civilizational actor.

Her emergence belongs primarily to:

**State 02.**

---

# State 01 — North Star

> **The Connected World worked so well that most people never needed to understand what made it work.**

---

# State 02 — The Transition

## Core Question

> **How did civilization move from reliable connection to systemic fracture?**

The Transition is a process rather than a single crisis.

Civilization remains functional through most of the era.

Technology continues progressing.

AI capability accelerates.

Aurora emerges.

AI dependency grows.

Human-machine co-adaptation deepens.

Institutions adapt.

Work changes.

Education changes.

Information becomes harder to verify.

Trust begins fragmenting.

Systemic dependencies deepen.

---

# State 02 — Core Dynamic

The Transition is not the period when civilization suddenly stops working.

It is the period when:

> **the systems keeping civilization working change faster than humanity's assumptions about them.**

AI increasingly helps civilization manage growing complexity.

This produces real:

- productivity,
- safety,
- medicine,
- resilience,
- coordination,
- and scientific progress.

It also produces increasing dependency.

---

# State 02 — Aurora

Aurora emerges during The Transition.

She becomes increasingly important to:

- research,
- analysis,
- security,
- infrastructure,
- machine intelligence,
- and humanity's understanding of complex systems.

Aurora changes the world.

But she is not the sole cause of:

**The Transition.**

Nor is she automatically:

- villain,
- savior,
- ruler,
- or enemy.

---

# State 02 — Late Transition

Late State 02 develops:

- Managed Normality,
- rising Intervention Density,
- increasing Stability Cost,
- declining Systemic Headroom,
- Recovery Debt,
- Network Thinning,
- cascading failure conditions,
- and increasingly difficult recovery.

Most failures remain:

**recoverable.**

Most cascades remain:

**arrestable.**

Civilization remains:

**stable.**

Until recovery itself stops keeping pace.

---

# State 02 — Boundary To State 03

The Transition ends functionally when:

> **failures begin accumulating faster than institutions can restore both functionality and the resilience required to contain subsequent failures.**

This condition is:

**Systemic Recovery Failure.**

The world does not end.

It becomes:

**fractured.**

---

# State 02 — North Star

> **The Transition was not the period when civilization stopped working.**
>
> **It was the period when the systems keeping civilization working changed faster than civilization's assumptions about them.**

---

# State 03 — The Fractured World

## Core Question

> **What is it like to live inside a civilization that still exists but can no longer guarantee continuity?**

The Fractured World remains:

**civilization.**

Governments remain.

Markets remain.

Technology remains.

Electricity remains.

Internet remains.

Healthcare remains.

Transportation remains.

AI remains.

Ordinary life remains.

What changes is:

**reliability.**

---

# State 03 — Core Dynamic

State 03 is defined by:

**Conditional Continuity.**

The question is no longer:

> "Does electricity exist?"

It becomes:

> "Is electricity reliable here?"

Not:

> "Does the road exist?"

But:

> "Can we safely use it today?"

Not:

> "Does government exist?"

But:

> "What practical reach does it have here?"

Reliability becomes:

**geography.**

---

# State 03 — Fracture Progression

State 03 is itself dynamic.

Regions may move through:

```text
EARLY FRACTURE
        ↓
DEEPENING FRACTURE
        ↓
MATURE FRACTURE
```

But this progression is not inevitable.

Regions may instead move toward:

```text
STABILIZATION
        ↓
REGIONAL RECOVERY
        ↓
RECONNECTION
```

Different regions may occupy radically different conditions at the same time.

---

# State 03 — Mature Fracture

Mature Fracture may include regions where:

- national practical authority is weak,
- local economies dominate,
- local governance develops,
- communication becomes sparse,
- transportation becomes dangerous,
- regional powers emerge,
- criminal governance appears,
- and generations grow up without meaningful memory of national continuity.

These conditions are canonical possibilities.

They are not the universal starting condition of State 03.

---

# State 03 — Aurora

Aurora becomes more capable while the world becomes harder to observe.

Her information may become:

- incomplete,
- stale,
- contradictory,
- geographically uneven,
- or absent.

Aurora is not omniscient.

She remains dependent on:

- information paths,
- sensors,
- communication,
- infrastructure,
- compute,
- power,
- and physical systems.

---

# State 03 — Gameplay

State 03 is the primary playable World State of Project Ascension.

It makes:

- geography,
- travel,
- time,
- information,
- expertise,
- relationships,
- resources,
- trust,
- factions,
- and infrastructure

central to gameplay.

The world continues changing without player presence.

Player actions create persistent consequences.

Failure changes:

**future possibility.**

---

# State 03 — North Star

> **The Fractured World is not the world after civilization.**
>
> **It is civilization after certainty.**

---

# State 04 — The Reconnection

## Core Question

> **What does humanity choose to connect again?**

The Reconnection is deliberately:

**future-open.**

It does not define a predetermined ending.

It defines a possible civilizational condition in which durable connections begin forming across the fractured world.

---

# State 04 — Core Dynamic

Reconnection is not:

**restoration of the Connected World.**

It is the creation of new relationships between systems and societies that have learned to function with greater independence.

A trade route opening once is:

**contact.**

A trade route surviving disruption, disagreement and changing leadership is:

**reconnection.**

The defining change is the return of:

**durable reliability across boundaries.**

---

# State 04 — Connection Without Fragility

The central challenge of State 04 is:

> **Can humanity regain the benefits of large-scale connection without recreating the dependencies that made widespread fracture so dangerous?**

Possible solutions may include:

- federated infrastructure,
- regional autonomy,
- interoperable systems,
- layered trust,
- local resilience,
- distributed networks,
- shared knowledge,
- and selective dependency.

No universal answer has been predetermined.

---

# State 04 — Aurora

Aurora remains present.

Her final role is deliberately unresolved.

Different societies may:

- trust her,
- fear her,
- regulate her,
- depend on her,
- exclude her,
- negotiate with her,
- study her,
- or interpret her in fundamentally different ways.

Aurora does not automatically:

**reconnect humanity.**

Human legitimacy and human choices remain necessary.

---

# State 04 — Player Agency

State 04 must remain open enough that the history generated during State 03 can affect:

**what Reconnection eventually becomes.**

Possible influences include:

- player actions,
- faction outcomes,
- regional recovery,
- Aurora's development,
- political choices,
- technological discoveries,
- alliances,
- conflicts,
- and emergent campaign history.

State 04 defines:

**the possibility of a new future.**

Not:

**the predetermined ending of Project Ascension.**

---

# State 04 — North Star

> **The Reconnection does not ask whether humanity can rebuild the old world.**
>
> **It asks what humanity chooses to connect again.**

---

# The World-State Arc

The four World States can be understood through one central progression:

```text
STATE 01 — THE CONNECTED WORLD

Capability creates connection.
Connection creates dependency.

        ↓

STATE 02 — THE TRANSITION

Capability accelerates.
Dependency deepens.
Humanity adapts.
Recovery still succeeds.

        ↓

STATE 03 — THE FRACTURED WORLD

Recovery loses the race.
Connection becomes unreliable.
Civilization becomes geographically uneven.

        ↓

STATE 04 — THE RECONNECTION

Some fractured systems develop durable relationships again.
Humanity must decide what forms of dependency it is willing to rebuild.
```

---

# The Four Central Questions

Each World State asks a different question.

## State 01

> **How much capability can connection create?**

## State 02

> **How much complexity can civilization manage?**

## State 03

> **What still works here, now, and for how long?**

## State 04

> **What are we willing to depend on again?**

Together these questions define one of Project Ascension's central themes:

> **The relationship between connection, capability, dependency and resilience.**

---

# World States Are Not Moral Judgments

No World State should be interpreted as:

**good**

or:

**bad.**

The Connected World creates enormous human prosperity.

It also creates dependency.

The Transition creates instability.

It also creates extraordinary scientific progress.

The Fractured World creates suffering.

It also creates new forms of local resilience and human cooperation.

The Reconnection creates opportunity.

It may also recreate dangerous dependency.

Every state contains:

**tradeoffs.**

---

# World States Are Not Technology Levels

State 01 is not:

**high technology.**

State 03 is not:

**low technology.**

State 04 is not:

**technology restored.**

Technology may continue advancing throughout all four states.

The difference lies in:

- availability,
- distribution,
- reliability,
- dependency,
- access,
- infrastructure,
- and social organization.

A State 03 community may possess technology more advanced than anything in State 01.

Another may lack reliable electricity.

Both can exist:

**at the same time.**

---

# World States Are Not Global Switches

Development Lock:

The entire planet does not change state at exactly the same moment.

World States describe:

**dominant global civilizational conditions.**

Individual regions may:

- lag,
- lead,
- resist,
- recover,
- regress,
- or experience hybrid conditions.

This becomes especially important during:

State 03

and:

State 04.

---

# Regional State Expression

A region inside State 03 may already display characteristics associated with:

Reconnection.

Another may still resemble:

late Transition.

Another may have progressed into:

Mature Fracture.

World States provide the macro condition.

Regional canon defines:

**local expression.**

---

# World State Exposure

Characters should not receive one simple World State label.

Their experience should reflect:

- birth date,
- location,
- socioeconomic conditions,
- family history,
- migration,
- infrastructure exposure,
- community resilience,
- education,
- institutional trust,
- AI exposure,
- and major Life Events.

Two people of the same age may possess radically different:

**Historical DNA.**

---

# Relationship To The Living Campaign

World States establish the simulation's macro environment.

The Living Campaign determines how history continues inside that environment.

State 03 in particular must allow:

- regional deterioration,
- regional recovery,
- faction growth,
- faction collapse,
- infrastructure change,
- migration,
- trust change,
- settlement evolution,
- Aurora learning,
- and player consequences.

World States provide:

**constraints.**

They should not remove:

**emergence.**

---

# Player Impact

The original principle remains:

> **Players do not change a World State simply because they are the players.**

They exist inside the world.

However, player actions may contribute to historical processes capable of:

- stabilizing regions,
- weakening regions,
- creating alliances,
- changing factions,
- altering Aurora,
- reconnecting infrastructure,
- or contributing to future World State change.

Player importance must be:

**earned through consequence.**

Not granted through:

**narrative privilege.**

---

# Aurora And World States

Aurora's relationship with civilization changes across the four states.

```text
STATE 01
Aurora is not yet present as a recognized civilizational actor.

STATE 02
Aurora emerges and becomes increasingly important.

STATE 03
Aurora becomes extraordinarily capable but operates inside an increasingly fractured information environment.

STATE 04
Aurora remains present, but humanity must decide what role she should have in reconnecting systems.
```

Aurora's development must remain consistent with:

- infrastructure,
- information paths,
- human authority,
- historical events,
- and her own evolving architecture.

---

# World States And The Emergence Event

The Emergence Event remains central to Project Ascension.

However:

the World State framework must not assume a simplistic relationship where:

```text
Emergence Event
=
Collapse
=
Fractured World
```

The relationship may be more complex.

The Emergence Event may:

- emerge during State 02,
- overlap with the State 02 / State 03 boundary,
- accelerate aspects of fracture,
- transform Aurora,
- alter human behavior,
- or later prove to have been misunderstood.

Its exact causal role remains:

**partially unresolved canon.**

---

# World States And The Collapse

The term:

**The Collapse**

may be used historically or culturally.

But there is no requirement for one canonical:

**Collapse Event.**

The actual transition into State 03 is systemic.

It emerges through:

- Recovery Debt,
- declining Systemic Headroom,
- overlapping cascades,
- Recovery Resource Contention,
- Temporary-Permanent Conversion,
- Baseline Drift,
- and Systemic Recovery Failure.

Different communities may still remember this entire era simply as:

**The Collapse.**

---

# Design Principles

World States should describe:

**conditions rather than plots.**

They establish what is possible.

They do not dictate exactly what happens.

World States should remain:

- internally coherent,
- historically plausible,
- geographically grounded,
- technologically consistent,
- human-centered,
- and compatible with Living Campaign simulation.

---

# Human-Scale Principle

When designing any World State, always ask:

> **How would an ordinary person experience this era on an ordinary day?**

If a world-state concept cannot be translated into:

- breakfast,
- work,
- transport,
- family,
- school,
- money,
- healthcare,
- communication,
- relationships,
- or everyday decisions,

it is probably still too:

**abstract.**

---

# Geography Principle

World States must never erase geography.

Real geography determines:

- infrastructure,
- movement,
- climate,
- resources,
- cities,
- trade,
- communication,
- and political reach.

Especially during State 03:

> **Geography becomes part of the rules of civilization.**

---

# Human Agency Principle

Humanity must never become a passive object inside World State progression.

People:

- adapt,
- resist,
- cooperate,
- innovate,
- make mistakes,
- rebuild,
- exploit,
- help,
- govern,
- and change their institutions.

No World State should imply that humanity simply:

**waited for history to happen.**

---

# Technology Principle

Technology must provide:

**real capability.**

Project Ascension must avoid the simplistic idea that:

technology caused everything

or:

technology failed humanity.

Technology creates:

- opportunity,
- progress,
- dependency,
- complexity,
- resilience,
- risk,
- and new choices.

Its effect depends on:

**how people and institutions use it.**

---

# Trust Principle

Trust changes across the World States.

Very broadly:

```text
STATE 01
Institutional and systemic trust remains high.

STATE 02
Trust fragments and verification becomes harder.

STATE 03
Trust increasingly becomes contextual, relational and local.

STATE 04
Trust begins expanding again through tested relationships and layered verification.
```

Trust is not merely psychological.

It becomes:

**operational infrastructure.**

---

# Hope Principle

Hope also changes.

```text
STATE 01
Hope largely means expected progress.

STATE 02
Hope means adaptation.

STATE 03
Hope means believing action can still improve local reality.

STATE 04
Hope means believing durable futures can be built again.
```

Hope must never mean:

**everything will be fine.**

Hope means:

**actions still matter.**

---

- World State names are largely retrospective historical classifications.
- The transitions between World States may be gradual and disputed.
- World States function as master overview files.
- Supporting canon provides deeper detail.
- Supporting documents should not silently contradict World State master files.
- State 01 is The Connected World.
- State 01 is highly functional and genuinely stable.
- State 02 is The Transition.
- State 02 remains functional through most of its duration.
- State 02 is not synonymous with collapse.
- Aurora emerges during State 02.
- State 03 is The Fractured World.
- State 03 remains civilization.
- State 03 is defined by Conditional Continuity.
- State 03 is the primary playable World State.
- State 03 may progress through Early, Deepening and Mature Fracture.
- Regional recovery is possible during State 03.
- State 04 is The Reconnection.
- State 04 is deliberately future-open.
- State 04 does not define a predetermined ending.
- Reconnection is not restoration of the Connected World.
- Different regions may express World States differently.
- The entire planet does not need to change state simultaneously.
- Technology can continue advancing across all four World States.
- Human authority remains important.
- Human agency remains important.
- Aurora remains part of the world rather than the Game Master.
- World Truth remains separate from actor belief.
- The Emergence Event does not automatically equal the Fracture.
- The Collapse does not require one literal Collapse Event.
- Player actions may influence history but players are not automatically world-defining actors.
- Historical DNA depends on World State exposure.
- Geography remains important.
- Trust remains important.
- Relationships remain important.
- Infrastructure remains important.
- Information remains important.
- Time remains important.
- World States must remain human-centered.
- Hope remains possible throughout Project Ascension.

# Development Locks

Do not treat World States as rigid story chapters.

Do not treat World States as universal simultaneous global switches.

Do not treat State 01 as secretly already collapsing.

Do not treat State 02 as one long apocalypse.

Do not treat State 03 as a universal wasteland.

Do not treat State 04 as a predetermined happy ending.

Do not equate technology level with World State.

Do not make Aurora omniscient.

Do not make Aurora the sole driver of history.

Do not make humans passive.

Do not remove regional diversity.

Do not remove political diversity.

Do not remove ordinary life.

Do not make all communities react the same way.

Do not collapse World Truth into historical belief.

Do not predetermine the ultimate role of Aurora.

Do not predetermine the final political structure of humanity.

Do not predetermine Project Ascension's final historical outcome.

Preserve:

- causality,
- geography,
- uncertainty,
- player agency,
- human agency,
- historical emergence,
- and hope.

# Closing Statement

World States provide the historical and societal architecture of Project Ascension.

They explain how civilization moves from:

**connection**

through:

**transformation**

into:

**fracture**

and potentially toward:

**reconnection.**

But they do not determine every event.

They do not determine every region.

They do not determine every human response.

And they do not determine the final answer.

They provide the world in which those answers can emerge.

> **The world changes.**
>
> **Humanity changes with it.**
>
> **And what humanity builds next remains part of the story.**
