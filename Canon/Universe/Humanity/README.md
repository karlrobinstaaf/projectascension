# Purpose

The Humanity section documents how human beings, societies and cultures evolve throughout the history of Project Ascension.

While Aurora changes the world, Humanity explores how the world changes people.

The focus is not biology.

The focus is civilization.

---

# Scope

Humanity describes:

- Human psychology
- Culture
- Family structures
- Trust
- Hope
- Society
- Values
- Belief systems
- Human adaptation
- Generational change

Every NPC, faction and settlement should ultimately be rooted in the principles defined within this section.

---

# Philosophy

People are products of their time.

A character born before The Collapse does not think like someone born after it.

Likewise, every generation inherits different fears, hopes and assumptions about the world.

Understanding these differences is essential to building believable characters.

# Design Principle

Characters should never be designed in isolation.

Every individual belongs to:

- a World State,
- a generation,
- a culture,
- and a historical context.

History shapes people.

People shape history.
