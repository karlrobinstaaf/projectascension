# 1. Purpose

The Humanity directory defines the foundational human concepts and contextual models required to represent believable people across Project Ascension.

It exists to answer:

> **What does Project Ascension need to understand about human beings before Characters, Relationships, Society, Life and Narrative can simulate them meaningfully?**

Humanity is not a single simulation system.

It does not own all human state.

Instead, it establishes several foundational concepts that other systems rely upon.

These include:

- the philosophical understanding of humanity
- historical context associated with individual lives
- relatively stable human dispositions
- current and evolving psychological state
- individual human memory
- family as human social context
- culture as shared meaning and context
- trust as a cross-system human concept

Humanity defines foundations.

Other systems operationalize specific parts of human life.

---

# 2. Core Philosophy

The central Humanity principle is:

> **Humans exist inside history, are influenced by it, act within it and participate in creating what history becomes next.**

Humans are neither isolated from circumstance nor determined by it.

Conceptually:

```text
HISTORY
INFLUENCES

BUT

HISTORY
DOES NOT
DETERMINE.
```

Likewise:

```text
PSYCHOLOGY
INFLUENCES

BUT

PSYCHOLOGY
DOES NOT
DETERMINE.
```

And:

```text
CULTURE
INFLUENCES

BUT

CULTURE
DOES NOT
DETERMINE.
```

Human agency remains.

# 70. Development Locks

Future Humanity development must not introduce:

- deterministic culture models
- deterministic family models
- deterministic psychology
- deterministic Attributes
- personality archetype engines
- morality statistics
- universal trauma outcomes
- universal generational psychology
- perfect human Memory
- omniscient Characters
- omniscient Aurora
- NPCs without persistent human state
- one universal Trust score
- one universal cultural identity
- one universal family model
- human behavior controlled directly by narrative need

=
BEHAVIOR.

Prefer:

CONTEXT
+
PERSON
+
SITUATION
+
AGENCY

POSSIBLE ACTION.

# 75. Closing Principle

Project Ascension is a world of technological transformation, historical rupture, institutional change and artificial intelligence.

But none of those things matter without people.

People remember imperfectly.

They inherit history without being trapped by it.

They carry families and cultures without being reducible to them.

They trust.

They distrust.

They fear.

They hope.

They adapt.

They resist.

They misunderstand.

They love.

They make choices.

And those choices become part of what the world becomes next.

The central Humanity principle is:

> **Humans are shaped by the worlds they inherit, but they remain Actors in the creation of the worlds that follow.**
