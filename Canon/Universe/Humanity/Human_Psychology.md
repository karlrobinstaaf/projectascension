# Purpose

Human Psychology defines how individuals adapt to changing environments throughout the Project Ascension universe.

Rather than describing fixed personality traits, this document focuses on the psychological forces that shape human behaviour over time.

The purpose is not to predict every individual.

The purpose is to understand the conditions that influence them.

---

# Core Philosophy

Human beings constantly adapt.

When the world changes...

people change with it.

Fear becomes caution.

Hope becomes resilience.

Isolation becomes community.

Trust shifts from institutions to individuals.

Psychology is therefore dynamic rather than static.

---

# Design Principle

No psychological trait exists in isolation.

Every individual is influenced by:

- World State
- Generation
- Settlement
- Culture
- Personal experiences
- Relationships
- Trauma
- Hope

Human behaviour always emerges from the interaction between these factors.

---

# Psychological DNA

Every person possesses a Psychological DNA.

This is not genetic.

It is the collection of experiences that shape how a person interprets the world.

Psychological DNA evolves throughout life and differs between individuals, even when they share the same environment.

It influences:

- perception
- decision making
- trust
- fear
- leadership
- empathy
- cooperation
- conflict

---

# Adaptation

Adaptation is the single most important human survival mechanism.

People rarely remain unchanged.

Instead they continuously adjust to new realities.

Successful adaptation does not necessarily produce happier people.

It produces people capable of surviving their environment.

---

# Collective Psychology

Individuals influence society.

Society influences individuals.

Communities gradually develop shared psychological patterns based on common experiences.

Examples include:

- collective optimism
- collective anxiety
- collective resilience
- collective distrust
- collective purpose

These patterns may differ significantly between settlements.

---

# Generational Psychology

Every generation inherits a different world.

Those who remember life before The Collapse think differently from those born afterwards.

Historical context shapes expectations, values and emotional responses.

Generations therefore cannot be understood independently from history.

---

# Psychology Across World States

Human psychology evolves alongside civilization.

Each World State creates new social norms, fears, hopes and behavioural patterns.

Rather than defining absolute behaviour, World States establish the conditions under which human psychology develops.

Detailed descriptions are documented within each World State.

---

# Design Implications

Human Psychology should guide the creation of:

- Player Characters
- Companion Characters
- NPCs
- Settlements
- Factions
- Communities
- Storylines

Psychological realism should emerge naturally from the surrounding world rather than being artificially assigned.
