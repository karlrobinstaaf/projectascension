> *"History shapes the world. Psychology shapes the person."*

---

# Purpose

Psychological DNA describes how an individual internally interprets and adapts to life.

Where Historical DNA defines the world a person inherits, Psychological DNA defines how that world is experienced.

It is not personality.

It is the lens through which a person understands reality.

---

# Core Philosophy

No two people experience the same world in exactly the same way.

The same event may create courage in one person.

Fear in another.

Compassion in a third.

History creates circumstances.

Psychology creates interpretation.

---

# Design Principle

Historical DNA explains what happened.

Psychological DNA explains what it meant.

Together they explain why two people living through identical events can become completely different individuals.

---

# Formation

Psychological DNA develops continuously throughout life.

It is influenced by:

- childhood
- family relationships
- attachment
- education
- trauma
- success
- failure
- loss
- friendship
- responsibility
- love
- purpose

Every meaningful experience leaves a psychological imprint.

---

# Interpretation

People do not react to reality.

They react to their interpretation of reality.

Psychological DNA determines how events are understood, remembered and emotionally integrated.

Two individuals may witness the same event.

Neither experiences the same reality.

---

# Adaptation

Psychological DNA is never fixed.

It evolves through experience.

Growth, healing, betrayal and discovery all reshape the way a person interprets the world.

Psychology is therefore dynamic throughout life.

---

# Relationship to Human Attributes

Psychological DNA does not directly define behaviour.

Instead it influences the development of Human Attributes.

Examples:

A lifetime of betrayal may gradually reduce Trust.

Repeated acts of responsibility may strengthen Honor.

Meaningful relationships may increase Compassion.

Severe trauma may increase Caution while reducing Hope.

Attributes are expressions.

Psychological DNA is one of the forces shaping them.

---

# Relationship to Historical DNA

Historical DNA answers:

"What world shaped this person?"

Psychological DNA answers:

"How did this person understand that world?"

Historical DNA is largely shared.

Psychological DNA is deeply personal.

---

# Examples

Two siblings lose their parents during The Collapse.

They share the same Historical DNA.

One dedicates their life to protecting others.

The other believes that survival requires absolute self-interest.

The event is identical.

The meaning is not.

---

# Design Implications

Psychological DNA should influence:

- Character creation
- Life Generator
- Companion behaviour
- Relationships
- Dialogue
- Decision making
- Character development

It should never prescribe behaviour.

It should influence probability.

# Closing Statement

History explains where we come from.

Psychology explains who we become.

Every person carries an invisible interpretation of the world they have lived through.

That interpretation shapes every choice they make.
