# Purpose

The Human Condition explores how people change as civilization changes.

It is not a study of biology.

It is a study of humanity.

Every generation is shaped by the world it inherits.

Every world creates different people.

Understanding this relationship is essential to creating believable characters, believable societies and believable stories.

---

# Core Philosophy

Project Ascension is not ultimately about artificial intelligence.

It is about humanity's response to profound change.

Aurora may transform civilization.

But civilization transforms people.

The Human Condition serves as the philosophical foundation for every human character, settlement, faction and culture within Project Ascension.

---

# Fundamental Principle

No human being exists outside history.

Every individual is shaped by:

- the era they were born into,
- the events they experienced,
- the stories they inherited,
- the people they lost,
- and the future they believe is possible.

History shapes people.

People shape history.

---

# Human Evolution Through World States

Each World State represents more than changes in technology or infrastructure.

It represents a change in how people understand the world.

As civilization changes...

Humanity changes with it.

Every World State therefore creates a different generation.

---

## Areas of Change

Throughout the World States, humanity gradually changes its relationship with:

- Trust
- Family
- Community
- Knowledge
- Technology
- Authority
- Religion
- Violence
- Hope
- Identity
- Freedom
- Survival

Each of these themes is explored in dedicated Humanity documents.

---

# Historical DNA

Every character possesses a Historical DNA.

This is the invisible framework created by the time in which they grew up.

Historical DNA influences:

- worldview
- fears
- values
- ambitions
- morality
- communication
- leadership
- relationships

Characters are therefore never designed in isolation.

They are products of history.

---

# Design Philosophy

Characters should never feel like game mechanics.

They should feel like people who existed before the player met them.

Each individual carries memories, beliefs, hopes and traumas that extend far beyond the current campaign.

The player enters their story.

Not the other way around.

Project Ascension is not a story about the end of civilization.

It is the story of what humanity becomes after civilization changes.
