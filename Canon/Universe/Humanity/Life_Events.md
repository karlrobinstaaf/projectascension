# Purpose

Life Events represent the meaningful experiences that shape an individual's development throughout life.

They form the bridge between Historical DNA and Psychological DNA.

History creates the world.

Life Events create the personal journey within that world.

---

# Core Philosophy

Life is not a continuous line.

It is a collection of defining moments.

Some are joyful.

Some are tragic.

Some seem insignificant at the time but become life-changing years later.

Project Ascension simulates lives by combining many meaningful experiences rather than relying on a single defining event.

---

# Design Principle

No Life Event has a universal outcome.

The same event may strengthen one person, break another and inspire a third.

Life Events influence people.

They never determine who they become.

# Context Matters

Every Life Event is interpreted through context.

Its impact depends on factors such as:

- age
- Historical DNA
- Psychological DNA
- family
- culture
- relationships
- current responsibilities
- World State

No event exists in isolation.

---

# Timing

The same event occurring at different stages of life may produce very different outcomes.

Childhood experiences often shape identity.

Adult experiences often reshape identity.

Late-life experiences may redefine purpose.

---

# Chains of Events

Life Events rarely exist alone.

One event often creates another.

Examples:

Loss of employment

↓

Financial hardship

↓

Relocation

↓

New relationships

↓

Leadership opportunity

↓

Personal growth

A person's life is better understood as interconnected chains rather than isolated moments.

---

# Lasting Influence

Some Life Events leave only temporary effects.

Others remain influential throughout an individual's entire life.

The significance of an event is determined by its meaning, not by its scale.

Small moments may become defining memories.

Large events may gradually lose importance.

---

# Relationship to Psychological DNA

Life Events do not directly create personality.

They create experiences.

Psychological DNA determines how those experiences are interpreted and integrated over time.

---

# Relationship to Human Attributes

Repeated Life Events may gradually strengthen, weaken or rebalance Human Attributes.

Meaningful change should emerge over time rather than through isolated incidents.

Human growth is cumulative.

---
