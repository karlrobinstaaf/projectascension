# Purpose

Historical DNA defines how the world shapes an individual before personal choice begins.

It is the accumulated influence of history, geography, culture and society that forms the foundation of every human being.

Historical DNA is not personality.

It is the context from which personality emerges.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every person inherits more than genes.

They inherit a world.

They inherit wars they never fought.

Beliefs they never questioned.

Technologies they never invented.

Fears they never chose.

Hope they never created.

Historical DNA represents that inheritance.

---

# Design Principle

History is the first designer of every human life.

Before a character makes their first decision, history has already influenced:

- what they believe is normal
- who they trust
- what they fear
- what they hope for
- what opportunities exist
- what dangers are common

No individual begins with a blank slate.

---

# Components of Historical DNA

Historical DNA is influenced by multiple layers.

## Historical Timeline

The period in which a person is born.

---

## World State

The condition of civilization during their lifetime.

---

## Region

Climate, geography and local history.

---

## Settlement

The local community where the individual grows up.

---

## Culture

Shared traditions, values and customs.

---

## Family

Early relationships and inherited beliefs.

---

## Major Historical Events

Wars.

Pandemics.

Climate disasters.

Political collapse.

Aurora's emergence.

Any event capable of changing an entire generation.

---

# Historical DNA Is Shared

People from the same generation often share elements of Historical DNA.

They remember similar events.

They learn similar lessons.

However, identical Historical DNA does not create identical people.

Individual interpretation belongs to Psychological DNA.

---

# Relationship to Psychological DNA

Historical DNA answers:

"What happened?"

Psychological DNA answers:

"What did it mean to this individual?"

History creates the circumstances.

Psychology creates the response.

Together they form the foundation of every human life.

---

# Examples

Two children are born during The Threshold.

Both experience food shortages.

Both grow up without reliable national institutions.

Both witness increasing instability.

These experiences become part of their Historical DNA.

One becomes compassionate.

The other becomes ruthless.

The history is shared.

The interpretation is not.
