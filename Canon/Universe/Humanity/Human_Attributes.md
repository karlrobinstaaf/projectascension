# Purpose

Human Attributes represent the core dimensions that describe how an individual is likely to think, feel and act.

They are not skills.

They are not statistics.

They are long-term human characteristics that evolve throughout life.

Human Attributes provide the behavioural foundation for every living person in Project Ascension.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every human being is unique.

Not because they possess unique traits.

But because they possess unique combinations of traits shaped by their history and experiences.

The purpose of Human Attributes is not to simplify humanity.

The purpose is to simulate it.

---

# Design Principle

No Human Attribute should ever define a person on its own.

Every meaningful behaviour emerges through the interaction between multiple attributes.

Context always matters.

History always matters.

Relationships always matter.

---

# Relationship to Historical DNA

Historical DNA determines the world a person inherits.

It creates the circumstances that influence development.

Human Attributes are one expression of those influences.

---

# Relationship to Psychological DNA

Psychological DNA determines how experiences are interpreted.

Those interpretations gradually influence Human Attributes throughout life.

Historical DNA creates experiences.

Psychological DNA interprets them.

Human Attributes express them.

---

# Evolution

Human Attributes are dynamic.

They change slowly through meaningful experiences.

Examples include:

- repeated responsibility
- prolonged hardship
- betrayal
- success
- meaningful relationships
- leadership
- sacrifice
- personal growth

Major life events may strengthen, weaken or rebalance multiple attributes simultaneously.

---

# Categories

Human Attributes are expected to be organised into thematic groups.

Possible categories include:

- Identity
- Social
- Cognitive
- Survival

The categories exist only to improve understanding.

All attributes interact regardless of category.

---

# Balance

There are no "good" or "bad" attributes.

Every attribute represents both strengths and weaknesses depending on context.

Examples:

High Pride may create confidence.

It may also create arrogance.

High Compassion may create kindness.

It may also create vulnerability.

Every strength carries a potential cost.

Every weakness may become a strength under different circumstances.

---

# Human Diversity

No ideal attribute profile exists.

Project Ascension celebrates diversity of personality rather than optimisation.

Different people solve problems differently.

That diversity creates believable societies.

---

# Simulation

Human Attributes exist primarily for the simulation.

They allow the Life Generator and future systems to estimate:

- likely decisions
- reactions
- relationships
- leadership styles
- conflict resolution
- cooperation
- personal growth

Players should rarely interact directly with these numerical values.

They experience the consequences through the world.

---

# Design Goal

Human Attributes should make people feel consistent.

Not predictable.

The goal is believable behaviour.

Not perfect prediction.

---

# Future Attribute Library

A future revision of this document will define the project's canonical Human Attributes.

The design goal is approximately twenty-four carefully selected attributes capable of describing human behaviour through meaningful combinations rather than excessive complexity.
