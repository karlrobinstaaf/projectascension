# Purpose

This document captures the core messages, vision statements and marketing concepts that define Project Ascension.

It is not intended to be final marketing copy.

Instead, it serves as a living vision board where ideas, taglines and messaging can be collected, refined and revisited throughout development.

Every statement should reflect the soul of the project.

---

# Core Identity

**Project Ascension is a Living World RPG.**

This is currently the project's primary positioning statement.

It communicates that Project Ascension belongs to the RPG genre while highlighting that the world itself is alive, continuously evolving and independent of the player.

---

# What "Living World" Means

A Living World is a world where:

- People continue living whether the player is present or not.
- Relationships evolve naturally.
- Settlements grow, decline and rebuild.
- Stories emerge from people rather than scripted quests.
- History continues to shape the future.
- Every generation inherits the consequences of previous generations.

---

# Internal Design Principle

The phrase "Living World RPG" is not only a marketing message.

It is also a design philosophy.

Every new system should strengthen the feeling that the world exists independently of the player.
