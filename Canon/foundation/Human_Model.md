---

# Purpose

The Human Model defines how Project Ascension understands, designs and simulates human beings.

Rather than viewing characters as collections of statistics, the Human Model describes the chain of influences that transform history into behaviour.

Every player, companion and NPC follows the same fundamental model.

---

# Core Philosophy

People are not created from attributes.

People are created by life.

Attributes are only one visible expression of a much deeper process.

History creates experiences.

Experiences create interpretation.

Interpretation shapes behaviour.

---

# The Human Model

Every individual emerges through the following sequence.

```
Historical Timeline
        ↓
World State
        ↓
Region
        ↓
Settlement
        ↓
Culture
        ↓
Family
        ↓
Historical DNA
        ↓
Psychological DNA
        ↓
Human Attributes
        ↓
Current Situation
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Behaviour
        ↓
Relationships
        ↓
Story
```

This sequence represents the conceptual flow behind every living person within Project Ascension.

---

# Layer 1 – The World

The world exists before the individual.

The first layers define the environment into which a person is born.

These include:

- Historical Timeline
- World State
- Region
- Settlement
- Culture
- Family

These factors cannot be chosen by the individual.

---

# Layer 2 – Historical DNA

Historical DNA represents the inherited world.

It answers:

> *"What happened around this person?"*

Historical DNA establishes context.

---

# Layer 3 – Psychological DNA

Psychological DNA represents personal interpretation.

It answers:

> *"What did those experiences mean to this individual?"*

This layer explains why identical experiences produce different people.

---

# Layer 4 – Human Attributes

Human Attributes represent long-term behavioural tendencies.

They are shaped by both Historical DNA and Psychological DNA.

No attribute exists independently.

Each one is influenced by a lifetime of experiences.

---

# Layer 5 – Current Situation

Behaviour never depends solely on personality.

Context matters.

The same individual may behave differently depending on:

- physical safety
- available resources
- relationships
- emotional state
- responsibility
- uncertainty

The current situation acts as the final filter before a decision is made.

---

# Behaviour

Behaviour is never stored.

Behaviour emerges.

Every decision is the result of all previous layers interacting in the present moment.

---

# Relationships

Relationships are not static connections.

Every interaction changes both individuals.

Relationships continuously influence future behaviour and future Psychological DNA.

People change each other.

---

# Stories

Stories are not written.

Stories emerge.

When individuals interact with each other and with the world, narratives naturally arise.

The Human Model exists to support emergent storytelling rather than scripted storytelling.

---

# Design Principles

The Human Model follows several fundamental principles.

## History Before Personality

History always comes first.

---

## Context Before Behaviour

Behaviour cannot be understood without context.

---

## Change Is Expected

People evolve continuously.

---

## No Perfect Prediction

The model estimates probabilities.

It never attempts to predict exact behaviour.

---

## Simulation Supports Storytelling

Simulation exists to create believable stories.

Storytelling does not exist to justify the simulation.
