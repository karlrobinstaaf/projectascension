# Purpose

The `Canon/foundation/` directory contains the highest-level conceptual foundations of Project Ascension.

These documents establish the principles that guide:

- worldbuilding,
- narrative design,
- character creation,
- human behavior,
- plausibility,
- alternate history,
- gameplay philosophy,
- creative decision-making,
- and the interpretation of canon.

Foundation does not primarily describe:

**what happens in the world.**

It defines:

**how the world should be designed and understood.**

This distinction is critical.

---

# Canonical Role

Foundation sits near the top of the Project Ascension canon architecture.

Its documents establish constraints and principles that downstream canon should respect.

Conceptually:

```text
FOUNDATION
    ↓
Defines how Project Ascension should be designed

UNIVERSE
    ↓
Defines the world, history and civilizational conditions

SYSTEMS
    ↓
Defines how the world behaves and changes

REGIONS / FACTIONS / SETTLEMENTS
    ↓
Defines specific expressions of the world

CHARACTERS / NPCs
    ↓
Defines people living inside those systems

LIVING CAMPAIGN
    ↓
Allows the world to continue evolving through time and action
```

Foundation therefore acts as:

**design constitution**

rather than:

**historical encyclopedia.**

---

# Foundation Principle

A Foundation document should answer questions such as:

- What kind of experience are we creating?
- What makes Project Ascension different?
- What must remain believable?
- How should humans behave?
- How should characters be generated?
- How should alternate history be constructed?
- What narrative tone should be preserved?
- What principles should guide design decisions?
- Which creative compromises are acceptable?
- Which ideas must never be violated?

Foundation should rarely answer:

- Which faction controls Oakland?
- What happened on a specific date?
- Which highway is closed?
- What resources does a settlement possess?
- What exactly Aurora did during a particular event?

Those belong elsewhere.

# Group 01 — Project Identity

Primary documents:

- `Vision.md`
- `Project_Philosophy.md`

These documents answer:

> **What is Project Ascension trying to become?**

They should define the deepest creative identity of the project.

---

# Vision

`Vision.md` should describe the highest-level creative ambition.

It should answer:

- What experience should players ultimately have?
- What makes Project Ascension distinctive?
- What emotions should remain after a session?
- What kinds of questions should the project explore?
- What is the long-term creative ambition?

Vision should remain:

**aspirational.**

It should not become a detailed implementation document.

---

# Project Philosophy

`Project_Philosophy.md` should explain the deeper philosophy behind the experience.

This may include principles such as:

- understanding rather than simple victory,
- complexity rather than simplistic morality,
- humanity under pressure,
- consequence,
- uncertainty,
- agency,
- adaptation,
- and the relationship between humans and intelligence.

Project Philosophy answers:

> **Why are we building the experience this way?**

---

# Creative Pillars

`Creative_Pillars.md` should define the small number of principles against which major creative decisions are evaluated.

Examples may include:

- Plausibility
- Human Scale
- Living World
- Consequence
- Uncertainty
- Real Geography
- Aurora As Catalyst
- Human Agency

Creative Pillars should be:

**few enough to remember.**

If everything becomes a pillar:

nothing is.

---

# Group 02 — Creative And Design Governance

Primary documents:

- `Design_Principles.md`
- `Design_Decisions.md`
- `Design_Glossary.md`

These documents answer:

> **How do we make and preserve design decisions?**

---

# Design Principles

`Design_Principles.md` should contain reusable rules governing how Project Ascension is designed.

Examples:

- the world exists independently of the player,
- systems should create consequences,
- geography should matter,
- information should have provenance,
- failure should create new states,
- Aurora should not function as omniscient narrator.

Design Principles should be broadly applicable.

---

# Design Decisions

`Design_Decisions.md` should preserve important decisions that have already been made.

Its purpose is historical as well as architectural.

It should answer:

- What was decided?
- Why was it decided?
- What alternatives were rejected?
- Is the decision locked?
- What would justify revisiting it?

This helps prevent:

**creative regression.**

A previously resolved question should not repeatedly reappear simply because the original reasoning was forgotten.

---

# Design Glossary

`Design_Glossary.md` should define project-specific terminology.

It should provide canonical meanings for terms that could otherwise become ambiguous.

Examples may include:

- World Truth
- Historical DNA
- Psychological DNA
- Recovery Debt
- Systemic Headroom
- Conditional Continuity
- Emergence Event
- Fracture
- Reconnection
- Aurora
- Living Campaign
- World Ledger

The glossary should not replace detailed documents.

Its function is:

**terminological consistency.**

# Narrative Philosophy

`Narrative_Philosophy.md` should define how Project Ascension tells stories.

It should guide:

- tone,
- pacing,
- uncertainty,
- exposition,
- human scale,
- emotional realism,
- consequence,
- ambiguity,
- and player discovery.

The narrative should not exist merely to explain canon.

Players should:

**experience the world before being told how it works.**

---

# Plausibility Framework

`Plausibility_Framework.md` should provide the method for evaluating whether new ideas belong in Project Ascension.

Plausibility does not require:

**certainty.**

It requires:

**credible causal chains.**

A concept may be speculative.

But the project should be able to explain:

- what enables it,
- what constrains it,
- why people respond as they do,
- what resources it requires,
- what unintended consequences follow,
- and why it does not immediately solve every other problem.

---

# Plausibility Principle

Strong Canon:

> **Extraordinary outcomes require believable intermediate steps.**

Avoid:

```text
CURRENT WORLD
        ↓
HANDWAVE
        ↓
SCIENCE FICTION RESULT
```

Prefer:

```text
CURRENT WORLD
        ↓
TECHNOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT
        ↓
HUMAN ADAPTATION
        ↓
INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE
        ↓
SECOND-ORDER EFFECTS
        ↓
UNINTENDED CONSEQUENCES
        ↓
NEW EQUILIBRIUM
```

The Transition documentation is one major application of this principle.

---

# Real-World Anchoring

`Real_World_Anchoring_and_Alternate_History_Principles.md` should govern the relationship between:

**real history**

and:

**Project Ascension history.**

Project Ascension uses:

- real geography,
- real countries,
- real cities,
- real infrastructure principles,
- recognizable institutions,
- plausible technologies,
- and historically grounded human behavior.

The fictional future diverges from:

**a recognizable reality.**

---

# Alternate History Principle

Alternate history should diverge through:

**causal consequence.**

Not arbitrary rewriting.

When Project Ascension changes something historically important, downstream consequences should be considered.

The world should not preserve convenient parts of history while ignoring consequences created by major divergences.

---

# Geography Principle

Real geography is not background decoration.

It affects:

- transportation,
- infrastructure,
- water,
- energy,
- climate,
- trade,
- settlement development,
- military movement,
- communication,
- politics,
- and survival.

Especially in State 03:

> **Geography becomes part of gameplay.**

# Human Model

`Human_Model.md` should define the systemic representation of a human being inside Project Ascension.

It may interact with concepts such as:

- Historical DNA,
- Psychological DNA,
- Human Attributes,
- Expertise,
- Relationships,
- Memory,
- Trust,
- Life Events,
- Beliefs,
- and World State exposure.

The Human Model should avoid reducing characters to:

**stat blocks.**

---

# Human Model Principle

A person is not merely:

```text
Strength: 7
Intelligence: 8
Trust: 4
```

A person is the result of:

```text
BIOLOGY
    +
PERSONALITY
    +
HISTORY
    +
RELATIONSHIPS
    +
EXPERTISE
    +
CULTURE
    +
WORLD STATE EXPOSURE
    +
MEMORY
    +
CHOICES
    +
CURRENT CONDITIONS
```

Human behavior should emerge from:

**context.**

---

# Character Design Philosophy

`Character_Design_Philosophy.md` should establish what makes a Project Ascension character believable and interesting.

Characters should not exist merely to serve:

**plot functions.**

They should possess:

- motivations,
- fears,
- relationships,
- expertise,
- contradictions,
- histories,
- beliefs,
- limitations,
- and goals independent of the player.

NPCs are:

**people inside the simulation.**

---

# Character Generation Framework

`Character_Generation_Framework.md` should define the practical process for generating characters consistent with the Human Model.

It should translate philosophy into:

**creation methodology.**

Character generation may use:

- birthplace,
- age,
- family,
- education,
- profession,
- World State exposure,
- Historical DNA,
- Psychological DNA,
- expertise,
- relationships,
- Life Events,
- beliefs,
- and current conditions.

Generated characters should feel:

**causally produced by their lives.**

---

# Player And NPC Equality Principle

Strong Canon:

The fictional world does not know:

**who is a player.**

Aurora does not know:

**who is a player.**

NPCs should therefore be modeled as people rather than:

**supporting game objects.**

The distinction between player and NPC belongs to:

**the game architecture.**

Not:

**World Truth.**

**canonical orientation.**

# Universe Bible vs World States

The distinction should remain:

```text
UNIVERSE BIBLE
Defines the major truths of the entire setting.

WORLD STATES
Define how civilization functions during major historical eras.

TIMELINE
Defines how historical change unfolds.

REGIONAL CANON
Defines how global conditions manifest locally.

SYSTEM DOCUMENTS
Define how specific mechanisms operate.
```

These layers should support:

rather than duplicate:

one another.

# What Belongs In Foundation

A document belongs in `Canon/foundation/` when it primarily defines:

- project philosophy,
- design philosophy,
- creative principles,
- narrative philosophy,
- human representation,
- character creation philosophy,
- plausibility standards,
- alternate-history methodology,
- terminology,
- major design decisions,
- project vision,
- or universe-wide canonical foundations.

A good test is:

> **Would changing this document potentially require changes across many unrelated areas of Project Ascension?**

If yes:

it may belong in Foundation.

---

# What Does Not Belong In Foundation

Foundation should not become a dumping ground for important documents.

The following generally belong elsewhere.

## Historical Events

Examples:

- specific Transition events,
- Emergence Event chronology,
- wars,
- disasters,
- specific political events.

These belong in:

**Timeline / History.**

---

## Regional Worldbuilding

Examples:

- California,
- Bay Area,
- Texas,
- individual cities,
- specific infrastructure corridors.

These belong in:

**regional canon.**

---

## Factions

Faction-specific:

- ideology,
- leadership,
- resources,
- territory,
- and history

belong in:

**Factions.**

---

## Settlements

Settlement-specific:

- population,
- resources,
- governance,
- infrastructure,
- and local culture

belong in:

**Settlements / regional worldbuilding.**

---

## Aurora Technical Architecture

Detailed:

- runtime behavior,
- memory architecture,
- machine cognition,
- communication protocols,
- and technical AI systems

should live under:

**Aurora-specific canon.**

Foundation may define constraints on Aurora.

It should not contain every implementation detail.

---

## Gameplay Systems

Detailed mechanics for:

- travel,
- encounters,
- resources,
- factions,
- world simulation,
- time,
- and campaign persistence

belong under:

**Systems / Living Campaign.**

Foundation should establish principles those systems must follow.

---

# Foundation vs Canon Detail

Foundation should answer:

> **What rule should detailed canon follow?**

Detailed canon should answer:

> **What happens when that rule meets the world?**

For example:

Foundation:

> Geography must matter.

Regional canon:

> The Bay Bridge creates a strategic movement bottleneck.

Gameplay system:

> Crossing conditions affect travel time, resources and risk.

Encounter:

> Players must decide whether to wait for the bridge or take a longer route.

This is the intended relationship.

---

# Foundation Stability

Foundation documents should change:

**less frequently**

than downstream canon.

This does not mean they are immutable.

Project Ascension is still developing.

But a Foundation change should be treated as:

**architecturally significant.**

---

# Foundation Change Test

Before materially changing Foundation canon, ask:

1. Why is the existing principle insufficient?
2. Is the problem actually in downstream implementation?
3. Which documents depend on this principle?
4. Would the change invalidate existing canon?
5. Does it improve Project Ascension's core identity?
6. Does it introduce contradictions?
7. Is this an intentional evolution or accidental drift?

Major changes should be documented in:

`Design_Decisions.md`

when appropriate.

---

# Canon Drift Prevention

One of the Foundation directory's primary purposes is preventing:

**Canon Drift.**

Project Ascension contains many interconnected documents.

Without stable principles, gradual development can unintentionally change:

- Aurora,
- humanity,
- tone,
- plausibility,
- player agency,
- world behavior,
- or the meaning of earlier canon.

Foundation provides a reference point.

When uncertain:

**return here.**

---

# README Rule

Project Ascension should generally apply the following rule:

> **Every meaningful canonical, narrative, systemic or architectural directory should contain a README defining its role.**

A README should explain:

- Purpose
- Canonical Role
- Scope
- What Belongs Here
- What Does Not Belong Here
- Relationship To Other Canon
- Key Documents
- Development Rules
- Strong Canon
- Development Locks
- Navigation
- Revision History

README files are primarily:

**navigation + architecture + guardrails.**

They should not become duplicate lore documents.

---

# README Exception

Not every organizational subfolder requires a README.

A README may be omitted when:

- the directory contains only a few obvious files,
- its purpose is completely defined by its parent,
- it contains no independent canonical responsibility,
- and there is little risk of future ambiguity.

When in doubt:

prefer clarity.

# Foundation Strong Canon

The following principles apply across Foundation:

- Project Ascension is human-centered.
- The world exists independently of the players.
- Players exist inside the world rather than above it.
- NPCs should be treated as people.
- Aurora exists inside World Truth and is not the Game Master.
- Aurora must not receive narrative knowledge without a legitimate information path.
- Human behavior must remain varied and contextual.
- Human beings must not be reduced to simple archetypes or statistics.
- Real geography matters.
- Plausibility matters.
- Speculative outcomes require causal development.
- Technology must produce genuine capability.
- Technology must also create believable tradeoffs.
- Human agency remains important.
- Machine intelligence must not erase human agency.
- Failure should create consequences rather than arbitrary dead ends.
- The Living World must continue without player presence.
- World Truth remains separate from character belief.
- History may be interpreted differently by different actors.
- Complexity should not erase individual responsibility.
- No single ideology should automatically represent the correct future.
- No faction should automatically represent pure good or pure evil.
- The world must contain ordinary life.
- Humor, love, culture and human relationships remain important.
- Hope must remain possible.
- Project Ascension should ask questions rather than force simplistic answers.

# Foundation Development Locks

Do not convert Project Ascension into generic post-apocalyptic fiction.

Do not convert Project Ascension into simple AI rebellion fiction.

Do not make Aurora inherently evil.

Do not make Aurora inherently benevolent.

Do not make Aurora omniscient.

Do not make Aurora omnipotent.

Do not make humanity stupid in order to make the plot work.

Do not remove human agency.

Do not make players chosen ones by default.

Do not give players automatic narrative importance.

Do not treat NPCs as disposable content.

Do not ignore geography.

Do not ignore physical constraints.

Do not use technological handwaving to skip causal development.

Do not confuse plausibility with certainty.

Do not require every speculative development to already exist today.

Do require a believable chain connecting speculation to the world.

Do not collapse World Truth into belief.

Do not make every institution incompetent.

Do not make every corporation malicious.

Do not make every government corrupt.

Do not make every community altruistic.

Do not make every community violent.

Do not remove ambiguity merely to simplify storytelling.

Do not use ambiguity as an excuse for inconsistent canon.

Do not allow Foundation documents to become redundant copies of detailed canon.

Do not casually alter foundational principles without considering downstream consequences.

> **Foundation should tell us how to think about Project Ascension before it tells us what to add to Project Ascension.**

---

> **The question is not whether something is extraordinary.**
>
> **The question is whether the world provides enough believable steps to get there.**

> **Do not explain the world when the player can experience it.**

> **Technology should change what is possible without making consequence disappear.**

**which answers belong.**

> **Is this still Project Ascension?**

return here.
