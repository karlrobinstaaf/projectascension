# Purpose

This document defines the canonical terminology used throughout Project Ascension.

It exists to ensure that every designer, writer, developer and AI system uses the same vocabulary when discussing the project.

Whenever a new core concept receives an official name, it should be documented here together with the reasoning behind the decision.

The purpose is not only consistency.

The purpose is preserving design intent.

---

# Naming Principles

Every major concept should have one canonical name.

If multiple names exist, this document explains which one is official and why.

The chosen terminology should communicate the design philosophy behind the concept rather than simply describing its technical implementation.

---

# Terminology

---

## Life Generator

**Previous Term**

NPC Generator

**Canonical Term**

Life Generator

**Definition**

The procedural system responsible for creating living human beings within Project Ascension.

The system generates complete individuals with history, relationships, motivations, attributes, goals and personal stories.

These individuals continue living independently of the player's actions.

**Reason for the Name**

Traditional RPGs generate NPCs that primarily exist to support gameplay.

Project Ascension generates people who exist as part of a living world.

The name reflects that philosophical difference.

---

## Story Seed

**Previous Term**

Quest / Side Quest

**Canonical Term**

Story Seed

**Definition**

A narrative starting point rather than a predefined mission.

Story Seeds evolve naturally depending on the people, settlements and events involved.

The player discovers stories rather than accepting scripted quests.

**Reason for the Name**

The objective is to create emergent storytelling instead of predefined mission chains.

---

## World State

**Alternative Terms Considered**

Era

Age

Timeline Segment

**Canonical Term**

World State

**Definition**

A snapshot of civilization during a particular period.

A World State describes the conditions under which humanity, society and technology evolve.

It represents the state of the world rather than merely the passage of time.

**Reason for the Name**

The term emphasizes that civilization changes as a system, not simply through chronology.

---

## Living Campaign Engine

**Canonical Term**

Living Campaign Engine

**Definition**

The system responsible for generating dynamic campaigns that continue evolving regardless of player activity.

The campaign is never static.

It is continuously shaped by the simulated world.

**Reason for the Name**

The campaign behaves as a living process rather than a sequence of scripted events.

# Maintenance Rules

Whenever a new foundational concept is introduced:

1. Choose a canonical name.
2. Document any previous or alternative names.
3. Explain why the final name was selected.
4. Update related documentation to use the canonical terminology.
