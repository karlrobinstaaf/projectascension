> **Project Ascension does not begin in an invented world. It begins in ours.**

> **Use reality until history gives us a reason not to.**

---

# 1. Purpose

This document establishes a foundational worldbuilding principle for:

    PROJECT ASCENSION.

The world of Project Ascension should not feel like:

    an invented science-fiction map

with:

    fictional cities
    fictional universities
    fictional mountains
    fictional highways
    fictional technology centers
    fictional historical foundations

created merely because the story takes place in the future.

Instead:

    THE OLD WORLD
        IS
    OUR WORLD.

The geography is recognizable.

The history is recognizable.

The institutions are recognizable.

The infrastructure is recognizable.

The technological development begins from recognizable reality.

Then:

    HISTORY
    DIVERGES.

And the world changes.

---

# 2. Foundational Worldbuilding Principle

Canonical:

> **USE REALITY UNTIL HISTORY GIVES US A REASON NOT TO.**

If a real location can serve the story:

    use it.

If real infrastructure exists:

    understand it first.

If a real university exists:

    do not invent one unnecessarily.

If a real highway crosses the region:

    use that highway.

If a real river determines settlement geography:

    preserve that fact.

If a real technology ecosystem shaped the region:

    let that history matter.

Fiction begins where:

    PROJECT ASCENSION HISTORY

begins changing reality.

---

One of Project Ascension's most important worldbuilding tools should be:

    RECOGNITION.

The player should sometimes encounter:

    a city
    a bridge
    a highway
    a university
    a military installation
    a dam
    a coastline
    a mountain
    a technology campus
    a subway system
    an airport

and think:

> **I know this place.**

Then:

    ASCENSION

shows them:

> **what became of it.**

---

# 4. Plausibility Through Familiarity

Project Ascension should repeatedly create the feeling:

> **This didn't happen — but I can imagine how it could have.**

That feeling comes from connecting fiction to:

    recognizable reality
    credible technological trends
    actual geography
    existing infrastructure
    historical continuity
    human incentives
    institutional behavior
    environmental constraints.

The world should not require the player to accept that everything familiar disappeared merely because time passed.

---

# 5. Reality Is the Foundation, Not a Constraint

Using real-world foundations does not mean:

    documentary simulation.

Project Ascension remains:

    speculative fiction.

Creative freedom remains essential.

The principle is:

    understand
    reality

before:

    transforming
    reality.
