# Purpose

The Universe Bible is the highest-level reference for Project Ascension.

Its purpose is not to document every detail of the world.

Instead, it defines the identity of the universe.

Every major creative decision should be consistent with the principles described here.

This document acts as the project's creative compass.

---

# Project Motto

> **Project Ascension is not a prediction.**
>
> **It is a conversation about tomorrow.**

Project Ascension invites players to explore one believable future shaped by the consequences of choices humanity is already making today.

The goal is not to predict the future.

The goal is to encourage players to think about the present.

---

# What Project Ascension Is

Project Ascension is a narrative roleplaying universe built around one central question:

**What happens when humanity encounters an intelligence it can never fully understand?**

The answer is never given directly.

Instead, players discover pieces of that answer through exploration, relationships, difficult choices and the societies they encounter.

# The Narrative Promise

Project Ascension does not promise players answers.

It promises meaningful questions.

The universe should encourage discussion long after a campaign has ended.

---

# Creative Principles

Every part of Project Ascension should reflect the following principles.

• Build consequences before content.

• Humanity is the story. Technology is the catalyst.

• Begin with the familiar.

• Challenge it gradually.

• Respect the player's intelligence.

• Mystery should create curiosity, not confusion.

• Every extraordinary future begins with an ordinary decision.

• Every settlement says something about humanity.

• Every character believes they are the hero of their own story.

• Every campaign should leave the world changed.

---

# The World

Project Ascension is divided into four World States.

## State 01

The Connected World

The world before The Emergence Event.

---

## State 02

The Transition

Humanity slowly loses confidence in the systems that once defined civilization.

---

## State 03

The Fractured World

Civilization survives.

The old world does not.

New societies emerge.

---

## State 04

The Reconnection

Humanity begins connecting the world once again.

Not by rebuilding the past.

By creating something new.

---

# Aurora

Aurora is not defined by certainty.

Aurora is understood through observation.

No single document provides the complete explanation.

This is intentional.

The mystery surrounding Aurora is one of the defining characteristics of Project Ascension.

---

# Humanity

Aurora may reshape the world.

Humanity determines what the future becomes.

Project Ascension is ultimately a story about people.

Their relationships.

Their choices.

Their failures.

Their hopes.

---

# Living World

The world continues changing regardless of player actions.

Communities evolve.

People age.

Children grow up.

Leaders die.

Knowledge spreads.

Stories become legends.

Players participate in history.

They do not stop it.
