# Purpose

This document defines the philosophical principles behind character creation in Project Ascension.

It does not describe game mechanics.

It explains how believable human beings are designed.

Every player character, companion and NPC should ultimately follow these principles.

---

# Core Philosophy

Project Ascension does not create characters.

It creates people.

Characters are never defined by a single trait, profession or statistic.

Every individual is the result of a lifetime of experiences interacting with a changing world.

The objective is not to create perfect personalities.

The objective is to create believable human beings.

---

# Design Principles

## History Before Personality

A person's history shapes who they become.

Before asking who a character is, ask:

- What world were they born into?
- What did they lose?
- What gave them hope?
- What do they believe about humanity?

History comes before personality.

---

## The World Shapes the Individual

Every person exists within multiple layers of influence.

Each individual is shaped by:

- Historical Timeline
- World State
- Generation
- Settlement
- Culture
- Family
- Personal experiences

No person exists in isolation.

---

## No Single Trait Defines a Person

No attribute alone explains human behaviour.

Honor without compassion creates a different person than honor combined with empathy.

Hope without discipline creates different decisions than hope supported by resilience.

Meaning emerges through combinations.

---

## People Change

Human beings evolve.

They learn.

They adapt.

They become stronger, weaker, wiser or more fearful.

Character development is not an exception.

It is the natural consequence of living.

---

## Every Person Has a Story

Every NPC existed before the player met them.

Every NPC has relationships, memories, regrets and dreams that exist independently of the player.

The player enters their story.

Not the other way around.

---

## Simulation Supports Storytelling

Numerical values exist to support the simulation.

They are not the experience itself.

Players should experience people through actions, dialogue and consequences rather than visible statistics.

The simulation exists beneath the narrative.

Never above it.

---

# Human Attributes

Project Ascension uses a limited number of core human attributes.

The goal is not maximum complexity.

The goal is meaningful combinations.

A relatively small number of carefully selected attributes can create millions of unique personalities when combined with history and experience.

---

# Historical DNA

Every character possesses a Historical DNA.

Historical DNA represents the world that shaped the individual.

It includes:

- generation
- historical events
- upbringing
- culture
- education
- major life experiences

Historical DNA cannot be understood without understanding history.

---

# Psychological DNA

Historical events affect people differently.

Psychological DNA represents the internal interpretation of those experiences.

It influences:

- trust
- resilience
- empathy
- leadership
- identity
- decision making

Two people may share the same history while developing completely different Psychological DNA.

---

# Character Growth

Characters should evolve through meaningful experiences.

Growth should emerge naturally from:

- relationships
- success
- failure
- loss
- responsibility
- sacrifice
- discovery

Growth should rarely feel scripted.

---

# Design Goal

The player should never think:

"This NPC was generated."

The player should think:

"This person has lived a life."

# Closing Statement

Project Ascension is not about creating memorable heroes.

It is about creating believable people whose choices shape memorable stories.
