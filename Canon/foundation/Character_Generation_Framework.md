> *"Project Ascension does not generate characters. It generates lives."*

---

# Purpose

The Character Generation Framework defines how every human being is created within Project Ascension.

It provides the conceptual framework used by both players and the Life Generator.

Every person follows the same fundamental process.

The only difference is who makes the decisions.

---

# Core Philosophy

A human being is not the result of a random collection of attributes.

A human being emerges through the interaction of history, environment, relationships and personal experiences.

Character creation is therefore a simulation of a life rather than the construction of a game object.

---

# Universal Rule

Every human follows the same model.

This includes:

- Player Characters
- Companion Characters
- AI Generated People
- Story Characters
- Historical Characters

There are no separate systems.

Only different sources of decisions.

---

# Character Creation Modes

Project Ascension supports multiple creation modes.

Each mode follows the same Human Model.

---

## Random Life

The entire life is generated automatically.

The player discovers who they are through play.

---

## Guided Life

The player answers meaningful questions about their life.

The consequences remain largely hidden.

Choices shape the individual rather than assigning visible statistics.

---

## Custom Life

The player receives greater control over key decisions.

However, every choice is still governed by the Human Model.

Customisation expands expression.

It never removes consequence.

---

# Character Creation Flow

Every individual follows the same conceptual sequence.

```
Birth
    ↓
Historical Timeline
    ↓
World State
    ↓
Region
    ↓
Settlement
    ↓
Family
    ↓
Historical DNA
    ↓
Life Events
    ↓
Psychological DNA
    ↓
Human Attributes
    ↓
Current Purpose
    ↓
Relationships
    ↓
Current Situation
    ↓
Living Human
```

The order may vary internally within future implementations.

The philosophy does not.

---

# Hidden Simulation

Players should never optimise numbers.

Instead they experience the consequences of meaningful life choices.

Rather than assigning attributes directly, players make decisions.

The simulation determines how those decisions influence the individual.

The Human Model remains largely invisible.

The player experiences the person.

Not the mathematics.

---

# Character Balance

Project Ascension does not allow perfect characters.

Every individual possesses strengths.

Every individual possesses limitations.

Every meaningful strength creates vulnerability under different circumstances.

Likewise, every weakness may become a strength within the appropriate context.

The objective is balance through humanity rather than numerical equality.

---

# Human Diversity

No ideal character exists.

Different personalities solve problems differently.

Project Ascension values diversity of perspective over mechanical optimisation.

The strongest team is rarely composed of identical individuals.

---

# Life Before Story

Characters do not exist because the story needs them.

Stories emerge because believable people interact with each other.

Every important person should feel as though they existed before the player encountered them.

---

# Design Principles

## History Before Personality

People inherit a world before they develop an identity.

---

## Experiences Before Behaviour

Behaviour emerges from accumulated experiences.

---

## Consequences Before Rewards

Choices matter because they shape people.

Not because they increase statistics.

---

## Humanity Before Balance

Believability is always more important than optimisation.

---

## Simulation Before Narrative

Simulation creates believable conditions.

Narrative emerges naturally from those conditions.

# Closing Statement

Every person in Project Ascension carries a past.

Lives create people.

People create relationships.

Relationships create stories.

The framework exists to make those stories feel earned rather than scripted.
