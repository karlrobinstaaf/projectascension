# Purpose

The Life Generator is responsible for creating believable human beings within Project Ascension.

It does not generate NPCs.

It does not generate game objects.

It generates lives.

Every player character, companion and AI-controlled individual follows the same conceptual framework.

---

# Core Philosophy

Life Generator does not create personalities.

It creates the conditions from which personalities emerge.

Every generated individual should feel as though they have already lived a meaningful life before the player encounters them.

---

# Design Principle

The Life Generator orchestrates existing models.

It does not replace them.

Its purpose is to combine multiple systems into one coherent human life.

The intelligence of the Life Generator comes from the quality of the underlying models rather than from randomness.

---

# Human Generation Flow

Every individual is created through the following conceptual process.

```
Birth
    ↓
Historical Timeline
    ↓
World State
    ↓
Region
    ↓
Settlement
    ↓
Culture
    ↓
Family
    ↓
Historical DNA
    ↓
Life Events
    ↓
Psychological DNA
    ↓
Human Attributes
    ↓
Relationships
    ↓
Current Purpose
    ↓
Current Situation
    ↓
Living Human
```

The sequence represents the conceptual flow.

Future implementations may optimise or parallelise individual steps without changing the philosophy.

---

# Sources of Decisions

Not every decision is generated.

Different creation modes determine who provides input.

Possible sources include:

- Random generation
- Player choices
- AI reasoning
- Existing world history
- Previous Life Events
- Campaign state

The framework remains identical regardless of source.

---

# Consistency

Every generated life should be internally consistent.

Background, beliefs, relationships and behaviour should support one another.

The objective is not unpredictability.

The objective is believability.

---

# Living Evolution

Generation never truly ends.

Every significant experience creates new Life Events.

Those experiences may gradually influence:

- Psychological DNA
- Human Attributes
- Relationships
- Purpose
- Future decisions

Every person continues evolving throughout the campaign.

---

# Relationship with the Simulation

The Life Generator does not decide the future.

It provides the starting conditions from which future behaviour emerges.

Future decisions belong to the individual and the simulation.

---

# Relationship to AI

Artificial Intelligence is a reasoning tool.

Life Generator is a design framework.

AI may participate in generation.

It is never the definition of the system.

This separation allows Project Ascension to evolve independently of any specific AI technology.

---

# Design Goals

Every generated person should:

- feel believable
- possess internal consistency
- have meaningful strengths
- have meaningful limitations
- possess personal history
- have current goals
- maintain relationships
- continue evolving

No generated individual should feel disposable.

---

# Relationship to Other Documents

The Life Generator depends upon:

- Human Model
- Character Generation Framework
- Historical DNA
- Life Events
- Psychological DNA
- Human Attributes
- World States
- Historical Timeline

It also provides input for:

- Relationship System
- Narrative Engine
- Companion System
- Settlement Simulation
- Society Simulation

---

# Future Development

Future revisions may introduce:

- Life Templates
- Family Generation
- Occupation Generation
- Education Paths
- Relationship Networks
- Cultural Backgrounds
- Personality Validation
- Consistency Checks

These additions should extend the framework without altering its philosophical foundation.

---

# Closing Statement

The purpose of the Life Generator is not to populate a world.

Its purpose is to ensure that every person feels like they belonged to the world long before the player arrived.

Lives create people.

People create relationships.

Relationships create stories.

Stories create memories.
