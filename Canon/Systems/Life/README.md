# 1. Purpose

The Life System defines how human beings in Project Ascension acquire believable personal histories.

Its primary responsibility is to connect:

```text
HISTORY

PLACE

FAMILY

CULTURE

EXPERIENCE

OPPORTUNITY

TRAUMA

RELATIONSHIPS

EDUCATION

WORK

CHOICES

AND

TIME
```

into coherent human lives.

The Life System exists because a believable character should not be assembled from disconnected traits.

A person should instead feel like the result of:

```text
A LIFE.
```

The Life System therefore answers the fundamental question:

> **How did this person become the person they are now?**

# 4. Life vs Character

The distinction between Life and Characters is foundational.

The Life System answers:

```text
HOW DID THIS PERSON
BECOME WHO THEY ARE?
```

The Character System answers:

```text
WHO ARE THEY NOW?

WHAT DO THEY WANT?

WHAT DO THEY BELIEVE?

WHAT WILL THEY DO?
```

Conceptually:

```text
LIFE
↓
creates history and developmental context
↓
CHARACTER
↓
exists and acts in the present
```

Life influences Character.

Life does not permanently determine Character.

---

# 5. History Is Influence, Not Destiny

A person's past creates:

- experience
- knowledge
- habits
- fears
- expectations
- relationships
- capabilities
- opportunities
- limitations
- memories
- emotional associations
- cultural assumptions

But history does not mechanically dictate future behavior.

Therefore:

```text
PAST
≠
DESTINY.
```

Two people may experience similar events and become very different people.

Two people with very different histories may arrive at similar beliefs.

Human development must preserve:

```text
VARIATION.
```

# 7. Historical Anchoring

Every person exists at a specific point in history.

Their age determines which historical events they could realistically have experienced.

For example:

```text
BIRTH YEAR
+
LOCATION
+
HISTORICAL TIMELINE
=
POSSIBLE HISTORICAL EXPOSURE.
```

A person born before the acceleration of AI may remember:

```text
A WORLD BEFORE
MACHINE INTELLIGENCE
BECAME ORDINARY.
```

A younger person may not.

Someone born during the Transition may consider AI-mediated life completely normal.

Someone raised during the Fractured World may have no personal memory of reliable national infrastructure.

These differences matter.

```text

- stronger relationships

- purpose
- fear
- adaptation

INDIVIDUAL EXPERIENCE.
```

---

# 58. Life and World Simulation

World Simulation owns changing external conditions.

Life records how those conditions affect a particular person's history.

Conceptually:

```text
WORLD SIMULATION

Regional unemployment rises.
```

```text
LIFE

Parent loses employment.
Family relocates.
Education interrupted.
```

```text
CHARACTER

Current attitudes toward economic security
are influenced by that history.
```

The systems remain separate.

---

# 59. Life and Progression

Life provides the historical path through which progression becomes believable.

Progression may determine:

```text
HOW CAPABILITY CHANGES
OVER TIME.
```

Life records:

```text
WHERE THAT DEVELOPMENT
FITS INTO THE PERSON'S LIFE.
```

Neither should independently duplicate the other.

---

# 60. Life and Narrative

Narrative may reveal a person's history.

Narrative does not create that history merely when it becomes dramatically convenient.

Therefore:

```text
LIFE HISTORY
↓
EXISTS
↓
NARRATIVE MAY DISCOVER
AND PRESENT IT.
```

Never:

```text
NARRATIVE NEEDS TWIST
↓
NEW PAST INVENTED
WITHOUT CANONICAL SUPPORT.
```

---

# 61. Life and Living Campaign Engine

Most of a person's life may never become directly relevant to a campaign.

That is acceptable.

The Living Campaign Engine determines:

```text
WHICH PARTS OF HISTORY
MATTER NOW.
```

Life determines:

```text
WHAT THE HISTORY ACTUALLY IS.
```

This allows characters to possess depth beyond what the player currently knows.

---

# 62. Life Resolution

Not every character requires a fully simulated biography.

Life may operate at different levels of resolution.

Conceptually:

```text
LEVEL 0
DEMOGRAPHIC EXISTENCE

LEVEL 1
BASIC LIFE PROFILE

LEVEL 2
COHERENT BIOGRAPHY

LEVEL 3
DETAILED PERSONAL HISTORY

LEVEL 4
HIGH-RESOLUTION LIFE HISTORY

LEVEL 5
CRITICAL CHARACTER CONTINUITY
```

Resolution may increase when a character becomes more important.

---

# 63. Resolution Expansion

When increasing Life resolution:

```text
NEW DETAIL
MUST EXPLAIN
EXISTING STATE.
```

It must not contradict it.

For example:

Low resolution may establish:

```text
Age: 47
Profession: Civil Engineer
Origin: Sacramento
Current Location: Bay Area
```

Higher resolution may later establish:

```text
education

family

career history

relationships

specific projects

Transition experiences.
```

But it cannot suddenly establish:

```text
Age: 32

or

Born in New York
```

without a legitimate correction process.

---

# 64. Resolution Compression

When Life resolution decreases, the system may compress details.

It should preserve:

```text
IDENTITY

MAJOR LIFE EVENTS

RELATIONSHIPS

EXPERTISE ORIGINS

IMPORTANT MEMORIES

CURRENT OBLIGATIONS

HISTORICAL EXPOSURE

AND

CAUSALLY IMPORTANT HISTORY.
```

Minor details may be summarized.

---

# 65. Off-Screen Life

Characters continue having lives when not observed.

Possible off-screen developments include:

```text
work

travel

family events

relationship change

learning

aging

illness

economic change

relocation

new responsibilities

and

ordinary routines.
```

Off-screen simulation may operate at lower resolution.

It must preserve continuity.

---

# 66. Player Characters

Player characters should also possess believable life histories.

They are not exempt from:

```text
HISTORY

GEOGRAPHY

FAMILY

CULTURE

EDUCATION

EXPERTISE DEVELOPMENT

RELATIONSHIPS

AND

WORLD-STATE EXPOSURE.
```

Players may participate in creating their histories.

Those histories must remain compatible with Canon.

---

# 67. Player Freedom

Life history should create context without overdetermining player identity.

A player's past may establish:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED.
```

The player should retain meaningful authority over:

```text
WHAT IT MEANS TO THEM.
```

where this does not contradict established character state.

This preserves both:

```text
COHERENCE
```

and:

```text
PLAYER AGENCY.
```

---

# 68. NPC Equality Principle

NPC life generation should follow the same causal principles as player-character history.

NPCs should not be generated as:

```text
SHOPKEEPER

QUEST GIVER

ENEMY

VICTIM

INFORMANT.
```

Those may describe their current relationship to the player.

They do not define the person.

A shopkeeper may also be:

```text
parent

former engineer

widower

local organizer

amateur musician

diabetic

political skeptic

and

someone who remembers the Connected World.
```

The world should contain people.

Not functions wearing faces.

---

# 69. Historical Uncertainty

Not every detail of every life must be perfectly known.

Historical records may be:

```text
missing

contradictory

destroyed

misremembered

deliberately hidden

or

never recorded.
```

Therefore:

```text
LIFE TRUTH
```

may exist separately from:

```text
DOCUMENTED LIFE HISTORY.
```

This distinction becomes particularly important after Fracture.

---

# 70. Private History

A person's history may contain information unknown to:

```text
OTHER CHARACTERS

AURORA

INSTITUTIONS

AND

THE PLAYER.
```

Life history does not automatically become shared knowledge.

Information boundaries remain mandatory.

# 71. Aurora and Human Lives

Aurora may attempt to reconstruct human histories.

But:

```text
AURORA'S MODEL
OF A PERSON'S LIFE
≠
THE PERSON'S ACTUAL LIFE.
```

Aurora may possess:

- records
- observations
- reports
- communications
- inferred relationships
- public data
- private data she legitimately accessed
- uncertainty

Aurora may still misunderstand.

The epistemic rules remain intact.

# 75. Life State vs World Truth

Life history is part of World Truth.

But Life does not own all World Truth.

For example:

```text
WORLD TRUTH:

A major earthquake occurred.
```

World / historical canon owns the event.

Life owns:

```text
THIS PERSON
WAS IN SAN FRANCISCO

AND

EXPERIENCED THE EVENT
AT AGE 19.
```

This creates personal history without duplicating global history.

---

# 76. Causal Traceability

Important elements of a person's current state should ideally be traceable backward.

Example:

```text
CURRENT EXPERTISE
↓
PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
↓
EDUCATION
↓
EARLY INTEREST
↓
FORMATIVE EVENT.
```

Not every trait requires a dramatic origin.

But important capabilities should have plausible development paths.

---

# 77. Human Variation

The Life System must actively preserve variation.

People from the same:

```text
CITY

FAMILY

GENERATION

PROFESSION

OR

WORLD STATE
```

must not become psychologically identical.

Variation may emerge through:

- temperament
- opportunity
- relationships
- chance
- interpretation
- personal decisions
- timing
- different exposure
- different social networks

This prevents demographic stereotypes.

---

# 78. Life Complexity

Life generation should not attempt to simulate every day of every person's existence.

The goal is:

```text
SUFFICIENT CAUSAL DEPTH.
```

Not:

```text
MAXIMUM BIOGRAPHICAL DATA.
```

The system should preserve what is necessary for:

```text
BELIEVABILITY

CONTINUITY

CONSEQUENCE

AND

FUTURE SIMULATION.
```

Important Life Events should matter somewhere in the wider simulation.

# 80. Development Locks

Do not create characters without plausible histories when their role requires depth.

Do not generate random biographies without causal coherence.

Do not make every important character exceptional from childhood.

Do not make every expert a prodigy.

Do not make every survivor traumatized in the same way.

Do not make every Fractured World character violent or distrustful.

Do not make every Connected World character naive.

Do not make every Transition-era character anti-AI or pro-AI.

Do not use generation as a substitute for human variation.

Do not confuse profession with personality.

Do not confuse culture with personality.

Do not confuse trauma with personality.

Do not confuse World State with personality.

Do not assign expertise without developmental history.

Do not automatically expose characters to global events they had no plausible path to experience.

Do not give characters knowledge merely because the historical timeline contains it.

Do not allow Aurora automatic access to private life history.

Do not rewrite established biography when simulation resolution increases.

Do not create historical details solely to solve current narrative problems.

Do not turn Life into the Character System.

Do not turn Life into the Relationships System.

Do not turn Life into the World Simulation.

Do not turn Life into a procedural backstory generator disconnected from active simulation.

# 95. Closing Principle

Project Ascension is about civilization-scale change.

But civilization is never experienced at civilization scale.

It is experienced by individual people.

A supply-chain disruption becomes:

```text
a parent losing work.
```

A technological revolution becomes:

```text
a profession disappearing.
```

A political crisis becomes:

```text
an argument around a dinner table.
```

An infrastructure failure becomes:

```text
a family deciding whether to leave home.
```

The Fractured World becomes:

```text
the place where a child grows up.
```

The Reconnection becomes:

```text
the first time that child can safely visit
a city their parents once believed was unreachable.
```

The Life System exists to preserve that transformation:

```text
HISTORY
↓
HUMAN EXPERIENCE.
```

Because ultimately:

> **Project Ascension does not only simulate what happens to the world. It simulates what living through that world does to people.**
