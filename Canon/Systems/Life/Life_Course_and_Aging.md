# Purpose

The Life Course and Aging system defines how persistent human lives continue through simulation time.

Its central question is:

```text
WHAT HAPPENS
TO A HUMAN LIFE

WHEN

TIME
CONTINUES TO PASS?
```

Project Ascension must support people who:

```text
ARE BORN

GROW

LEARN

FORM RELATIONSHIPS

BUILD LIVES

CHANGE ROLES

MOVE

AGE

ADAPT

EXPERIENCE EVENTS

LOSE PEOPLE

CHANGE DIRECTION

GROW OLD

DIE

AND

LEAVE CONSEQUENCES BEHIND.
```

Characters must not exist only while:

```text
THE PLAYER
IS LOOKING AT THEM.
```

They possess:

```text
LIFE COURSES.
```

# Core Definition

A Life Course is:

> **The continuous chronological and biographical path through which a human life develops within World History.**

Conceptually:

```text
BIRTH
↓
LIVED TIME
↓
EXPERIENCE
↓
CHOICE
↓
CONSEQUENCE
↓
CHANGE
↓
CONTINUED LIFE
↓
DEATH
↓
PERSISTING CONSEQUENCES.
```

A Life Course is not:

```text
A PREWRITTEN STORY

A CHARACTER CLASS

A STANDARD LIFE PATH

A PROGRESSION TREE

A RANDOM EVENT TABLE

OR

A NARRATIVE ARC.
```

It is:

```text
THE HISTORY
OF THE LIFE
THAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED.
```

```text

```

Conceptually:

```text
CURRENT WORLD DATE
-

=
CHRONOLOGICAL AGE.
```

Chronological age is objective World Truth.

---

# Age Is Not Identity

```text
AGE
≠
```

```text
physical conditions

life histories

families

relationships

expertise

responsibilities

resources

psychological states

goals

social positions

worldviews.
```

# Life Stage

Life Stage represents:

> **A broad developmental and social context associated with a period of human life.**

Conceptual stages may include:

```text
INFANCY

CHILDHOOD

ADOLESCENCE

EARLY ADULTHOOD

ADULTHOOD

MIDDLE AGE

LATER ADULTHOOD

OLD AGE.
```

These categories exist for simulation reasoning.

They are not rigid human classes.

---

# Life Stage Is Gradual

A person does not suddenly become fundamentally different because:

```text
A BIRTHDAY OCCURRED.
```

Life-stage transitions represent:

```text
GRADUAL CHANGE.
```

Legal or institutional thresholds may occur on specific dates.

Human development does not.

---

# Legal Age Versus Human Development

Age thresholds may determine:

```text
LEGAL ELIGIBILITY

EDUCATION ACCESS

EMPLOYMENT RIGHTS

VOTING RIGHTS

DRIVING RIGHTS

RETIREMENT SYSTEMS

MILITARY ELIGIBILITY

OTHER INSTITUTIONAL RULES.
```

```text
WORLD / SOCIETY RULES.
```

They must not be confused with:

```text
INSTANT HUMAN DEVELOPMENT.
```

---

# Chronological Age Versus Functional Condition

Project Ascension must distinguish:

```text
CHRONOLOGICAL AGE
```

from:

```text
FUNCTIONAL CONDITION.
```

Two people aged seventy may have very different:

```text
mobility

strength

endurance

health

cognitive performance

professional capability

independence.
```

Age alone is insufficient to infer capability.

# Aging

Aging represents:

> **Long-term change associated with a human life continuing through time.**

Aging may interact with:

```text
physical condition

recovery

energy

experience

expertise

professional role

family role

social role

responsibilities

priorities

resources.
```

But aging does not mechanically determine any of them.

---

# Aging Principle

```text
AGING
IS NOT

AGING IS
PASSAGE THROUGH

```

---

# Aging Is Gradual

Normal aging should usually occur through:

```text
SLOW CHANGE
```

rather than:

```text
ANNUAL STAT PENALTIES.
```

Where abrupt change occurs, it should have a cause such as:

```text

illness

major event

environmental exposure

other valid condition.
```

That cause belongs to the appropriate authoritative system.

# Life Course and Memory

```text
LIFE COURSE
≠
MEMORY.
```

Life Course records:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED
IN THE PERSON'S LIFE.
```

Memory represents:

```text
WHAT THE PERSON
REMEMBERS.
```

Therefore:

```text
LIFE HISTORY
→ WORLD / BIOGRAPHICAL TRUTH

MEMORY
→ INTERNAL HUMAN RETENTION
AND RECONSTRUCTION.
```

A person may:

```text
forget an event

misremember an event

remember only part of an event

remember something differently
from another participant.
```

The Life Course itself does not change because the memory changed.
