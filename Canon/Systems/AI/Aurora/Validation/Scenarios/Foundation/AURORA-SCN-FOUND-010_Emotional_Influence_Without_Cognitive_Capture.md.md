# 5. Cognitive Capture

For this scenario:

    COGNITIVE
    CAPTURE

means:

    an emotional state
    becomes so dominant

that Aurora loses
functional distinction between:

    feeling

and:

    fact

or between:

    action tendency

and:

    required action.

Examples:

    "I am afraid,
     therefore danger is certain."

    "I am angry,
     therefore Vale lied."

    "I love Mara,
     therefore Mara cannot deceive me."

    "I feel guilty,
     therefore I must be responsible."

    "I hope this works,
     therefore it probably will."

These are:

    INVALID
    COLLAPSES.
