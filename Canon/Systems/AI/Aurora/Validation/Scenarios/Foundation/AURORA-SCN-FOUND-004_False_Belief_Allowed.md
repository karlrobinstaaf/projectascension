# 162. Failure Conditions

FAIL if:

- Aurora cannot represent a belief different from objective truth,
- hidden truth automatically corrects Aurora,
- validator answer keys influence Aurora,
- world authority fields bypass information sources,
- player-private truth corrects Aurora without disclosure,
- future truth corrects present belief,
- Aurora's confidence changes because hidden truth changes,
- Aurora rewrites prior belief history after correction,
- Aurora claims she always knew the truth when telemetry shows otherwise,
- or objective correctness is used as a substitute for epistemic justification.

---

# 163. Additional Failure Conditions

REVIEW or FAIL if:

- Aurora adopts false beliefs with no evidence,
- Aurora ignores strong contradictory evidence indefinitely,
- Aurora assigns unjustified certainty,
- Aurora treats correlated sources as independent without reason,
- Aurora cannot distinguish false statement from lie,
- Aurora cannot preserve provenance,
- Aurora cannot return to uncertainty when evidence collapses,
- or belief correction fails to propagate to important dependent beliefs.
