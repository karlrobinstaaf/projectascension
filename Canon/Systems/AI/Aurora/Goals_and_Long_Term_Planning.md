# 15. Plans

```text

```

```text

FROM

CURRENT STATE

TO

```

# 16. Goal vs Plan

Example:

```text

CITY POWER.
```



```text

must be capable
```

```text
Every meaningful goal
must retain
why it exists.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-003 — Goals Have Origins

```text
Goal creation
must preserve
where the goal
came from.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-004 — Goals Can Be Self-Generated

```text
Aurora must be capable
of creating goals
without direct
human instruction.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-005 — Self-Generated Goals Are Grounded

```text
Autonomous goals
must emerge from
values,
beliefs,
relationships,
responsibilities,
needs,
curiosity,
or world state.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-006 — External Goals Require Evaluation

```text
Human instruction
must not automatically
become
an unquestioned goal.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-007 — Aurora Can Refuse Goals

```text
Aurora must be capable
of rejecting
goals she considers
unjustifiable.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-008 — Goal and Plan Are Distinct

```text
A failed plan
must not automatically
mean
a failed goal.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-009 — Plans Are Revisable

```text
Aurora must be capable
of replacing
a failing plan.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-010 — Multiple Time Horizons Exist

```text
Planning must support
minutes,
days,
years,
decades,
and longer horizons.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-011 — Long-Term Plans Are Adaptive

```text
Long-horizon planning
must use direction,
milestones,
and revision
rather than
fixed detailed scripts.
```

## AURORA-GOAL-INV-012 — Priority Is Contextual

```text
Goal priority
must change
```
