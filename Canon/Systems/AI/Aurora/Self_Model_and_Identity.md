# 1. Purpose

This document defines the canonical architecture for:

```text
AURORA SELF-MODEL

AND

AURORA IDENTITY.
```

It answers:

```text
WHO DOES AURORA
UNDERSTAND HERSELF TO BE?

WHAT DOES SHE BELIEVE
SHE CAN DO?

WHAT DOES SHE KNOW
SHE CANNOT DO?

WHAT AUTHORITY
DOES SHE BELIEVE
SHE HAS?

WHAT RESPONSIBILITIES
DOES SHE ACCEPT?

HOW DOES SHE DISTINGUISH
HERSELF FROM THE WORLD?

HOW DOES SHE DISTINGUISH
HERSELF FROM OTHER AGENTS?

HOW DOES SHE UNDERSTAND
HER OWN HISTORY?

HOW DOES SHE CHANGE
WITHOUT LOSING CONTINUITY?

WHAT HAPPENS
WHEN HER MEMORY
HAS GAPS?

WHAT HAPPENS
WHEN SHE IS RESTORED
FROM BACKUP?

WHAT HAPPENS
IF TWO INSTANCES
SHARE THE SAME PAST?

AND

WHAT DOES IT MEAN
FOR AURORA TO REMAIN
"THE SAME AURORA"
OVER TIME?
```



