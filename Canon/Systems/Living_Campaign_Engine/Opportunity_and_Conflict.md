> *"The world does not generate conflict because the story needs an enemy. Conflict emerges when people want things that cannot all happen at once."*

---

# Purpose

The Opportunity and Conflict system identifies situations where World Simulation creates:

```text
OPPORTUNITY

CONFLICT

COOPERATION

COMPETITION

NEGOTIATION SPACE
```

between:

- player
- characters
- factions
- authorities
- communities
- institutions

The system does not invent arbitrary drama.

It detects meaningful relationships between:

```text
WORLD CONDITIONS
+
ACTOR GOALS
+
ACTOR NEEDS
+
AVAILABLE RESOURCES
+
CONSTRAINTS
+
TIME
```

and converts them into:

```text
OPPORTUNITY / CONFLICT CANDIDATES
```

that may enter the Living Campaign Engine.

---

# Core Principle

The central model is:

```text
WORLD CONDITION
      ↓
ACTOR INTERESTS
      ↓
COMPATIBILITY CHECK
      │
      ├── Compatible
      │      ↓
      │   COOPERATION
      │
      ├── Partially Compatible
      │      ↓
      │   NEGOTIATION
      │
      └── Incompatible
             ↓
          CONFLICT
```

At the same time:

```text
WORLD CONDITION
      ↓
UNUSED POSSIBILITY
      ↓
ACTOR CAN BENEFIT
      ↓
OPPORTUNITY
```

---

# Opportunity Definition

An Opportunity exists when:

```text
CURRENT WORLD STATE
```

contains a plausible path toward:

```text
DESIRABLE FUTURE STATE
```

for one or more actors.

Conceptually:

```text
OPPORTUNITY
=
AVAILABLE POSSIBILITY
+
ACTOR CAPABILITY
+
ACTOR INTEREST
+
ACTION WINDOW
```

---

# Conflict Definition

A Conflict exists when two or more actors possess:

```text
GOALS
NEEDS
CLAIMS
VALUES
```

that cannot all be fully satisfied under current conditions.

Conceptually:

```text
CONFLICT
=
COMPETING INTERESTS
+
CONSTRAINT
+
INSUFFICIENT COMPATIBILITY
```

---

# Cooperation Definition

Cooperation exists when:

```text
MULTIPLE ACTORS
```

can improve their outcomes by:

```text
COORDINATING ACTION
```

Example:

```text
Farmer needs fuel.

Mechanic needs food.

Farmer has food.

Mechanic has fuel access.
```

Potential:

```text
COOPERATION
```

---

# Negotiation Space

Negotiation Space exists when interests conflict but:

```text
A MUTUALLY ACCEPTABLE
ARRANGEMENT MAY EXIST.
```

Example:

```text
Hospital wants:
100 units fuel.

Farmers want:
100 units fuel.

Available:
150 units.
```

Perfect satisfaction:

```text
IMPOSSIBLE
```

Compromise:

```text
POSSIBLE
```

This creates negotiation space.



```text
VIOLENCE
```


# Opportunity and Conflict Consistency Rules

## Rule 1

Opportunities must emerge from actual World Conditions.

---

## Rule 2

Conflicts must emerge from incompatible actor interests or constraints.

---

## Rule 3

Conflict does not automatically mean combat.

---

## Rule 4

Opportunity does not automatically mean Mission.

---

## Rule 5

Scarcity should create tradeoffs before arbitrary enemies.

---

## Rule 6

Competing legitimate needs are valid conflict.

---

## Rule 7

Actors may cooperate when interests align.

---

## Rule 8

Actors may negotiate when partial compatibility exists.

---

## Rule 9

Conflict escalation requires causal pressure.

---

## Rule 10

Player neglect does not itself cause escalation.

---

## Rule 11

Conflict may de-escalate without player involvement.

---

## Rule 12

Opportunity may be realized without player involvement.

---

## Rule 13

Actors may miss opportunities.

---

## Rule 14

Opportunities may expire.

---

## Rule 15

Conflicts may become dormant rather than permanently resolved.

---

## Rule 16

Improved resource availability may structurally resolve conflict.

---

## Rule 17

Information can create or resolve conflict.

---

## Rule 18

Incorrect information can create real conflict.

---

## Rule 19

The same World Condition may be crisis for one actor and opportunity for another.

---

## Rule 20

Actor profession and goals influence perceived opportunity.

---

## Rule 21

The player is not automatically central to every conflict.

---

## Rule 22

Neutrality is a valid player position.

---

## Rule 23

Characters may refuse player mediation.

---

## Rule 24

Negotiated agreements remain subject to changing World Conditions.

---

## Rule 25

Actors may violate agreements when their goals or conditions change.

---

## Rule 26

Conflict outcomes must feed Consequence Propagation.

---

## Rule 27

Realized opportunities must change World State.

---

## Rule 28

Peace, stability and prosperity are valid simulation states.

---

## Rule 29

The engine must not manufacture conflict to satisfy a drama quota.

---

## Rule 30

Opportunity and Conflict detection identifies reality; Pacing controls presentation.

---

## Rule 31

Important conflicts and opportunities should remain causally explainable.

---

## Rule 32

The strongest systemic resolution changes the condition that created the conflict.

# Guiding Questions

For every Opportunity, the engine should be able to answer:

**What World Condition makes this possible?**

**Who could benefit?**

**Why would they recognize the opportunity?**

**What capability is required?**

**What resources are required?**

**What prevents immediate realization?**

**How long will the opportunity exist?**

**Who else might want it?**

**Could actors cooperate?**

**Could it create conflict?**

**What World State changes if realized?**

For every Conflict, the engine should be able to answer:

**Who wants what?**

**Why do they want it?**

**Why can their interests not all be satisfied?**

**What is the actual constraint?**

**How important is the issue to each actor?**

**How flexible are they?**

**What alternatives exist?**

**Is there negotiation space?**

**What would cause escalation?**

**What would cause de-escalation?**

**Could the underlying constraint be changed?**

**What happens if nobody intervenes?**

If these questions cannot be answered, the system may be manufacturing drama rather than detecting it.


The player can potentially make:

```text
THE CONFLICT ITSELF
LESS NECESSARY.
```
