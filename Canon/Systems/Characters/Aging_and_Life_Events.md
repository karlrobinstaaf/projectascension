---

# Purpose

The Aging and Life Events system defines how characters in Project Ascension:

```text
are born

grow

learn

form relationships

enter adulthood

build households

develop careers

become parents

move

age

experience major life events

lose people

change roles

retire

die

leave consequences behind.
```

The purpose is to allow:

```text
CHARACTER LIFE
```

to continue across:

```text
SIMULATION TIME.
```

Characters should not exist only while:

```text
THE PLAYER
IS LOOKING AT THEM.
```

They should possess:

```text
LIFE COURSES.
```

---

# Core Principle

Aging should not be modeled as:

```text
TIME PASSES
↓
STATS DECREASE.
```

Instead:

```text
TIME
+
BIOGRAPHY
+
RELATIONSHIPS
+
WORLD CONDITIONS
+
OPPORTUNITIES
+
LIFE EVENTS
+
CHARACTER DECISIONS
      ↓
LIFE COURSE.
```
