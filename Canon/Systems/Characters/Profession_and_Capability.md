---

# Purpose

The Profession and Capability system defines what characters in Project Ascension are actually capable of doing.

It connects:

```text
Profession

Education

Training

Skills

Experience

Knowledge

Physical Ability

Cognitive Ability

Social Ability

Tools

Equipment

Resources

Networks

Environment
```

into:

```text
PRACTICAL CAPABILITY.
```

The purpose is to prevent:

```text
INTENTION
```

from automatically becoming:

```text
SUCCESS.
```

---

# Core Principle

A character may:

```text
WANT TO DO SOMETHING.
```

They may:

```text
DECIDE TO DO IT.
```

But the simulation must still ask:

```text
CAN THEY ACTUALLY DO IT?
```

---

Conceptually:

```text

+

+
```
