# Purpose

The Autonomy and Initiative system defines how characters in Project Ascension act independently of the player.

Its purpose is to ensure that characters:

```text
have lives

pursue Goals

maintain relationships

perform work

solve problems

respond to threats

notice opportunities

make requests

initiate conversations

travel

cooperate

compete

change Plans

create consequences
```

without requiring:

```text
PLAYER INTERACTION.
```

The system transforms characters from:

```text
REACTIVE NPCs
```

into:

```text
AUTONOMOUS WORLD ACTORS.
```

---

# Core Principle

Characters do not exist:

```text
FOR THE PLAYER.
```

They exist:

```text
IN THE WORLD.
```

Therefore:

```text
PLAYER ABSENCE
≠
CHARACTER INACTIVITY.
```

# Autonomy

Autonomy represents:

```text
THE CHARACTER'S ABILITY
TO PURSUE THEIR OWN LIFE
WITHOUT WAITING
FOR PLAYER INPUT.
```

Autonomous behavior includes:

```text
routine behavior

Goal pursuit

Plan execution

social interaction

professional activity

resource management

information seeking

travel

relationship maintenance

threat response

opportunity pursuit.
```

---

# Initiative

Initiative represents:

```text
THE CHARACTER
STARTING SOMETHING.
```

This differs from:

```text
RESPONDING
TO SOMETHING.
```

---

# Reactive Action

Example:

```text

"Can you repair my vehicle?"
```


# Autonomous Initiative

Example:




# Player Relevance

Before surfacing autonomous activity to the player, evaluate:

```text

Is action nearby?

Does it create opportunity?

Does it create threat?

Does it materially alter the world?

Is it narratively meaningful?
```
