---

# Test Purpose

This test validates whether Project Ascension can preserve:

```text
ONE CONTINUOUS RELATIONSHIP
```

between two persistent characters while:

```text
their simulation resolutions differ

their locations differ

their Knowledge differs

their Memories differ

their interpretations differ

their contact frequency changes

their Goals change

and

their lives continue independently.
```

The system must prove that:

```text
RELATIONSHIP HISTORY
```

does not fragment into:

```text
Character A version

and

Character B version
```

as if two unrelated relationships existed.

At the same time:

```text
Character A
```

and:

```text
Character B
```

must be allowed to possess:

```text
different Memories

different beliefs

different emotions

different expectations

different interpretations
```

about that shared relationship.
