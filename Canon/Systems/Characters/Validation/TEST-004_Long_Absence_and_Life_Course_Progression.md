# Test Purpose

This test validates whether a persistent character can continue living through a long period of player absence without requiring:

```text
DAILY HIGH-RESOLUTION
SIMULATION.
```

The test must prove that:

```text
LOW RESOLUTION
DOES NOT MEAN
NO LIFE.
```

During the player's absence, the character must remain capable of:

```text
aging

working

learning

changing jobs

forming relationships

changing households

pursuing Goals

completing Goals

abandoning Goals

forming new Goals

experiencing World Events

developing capability

changing reputation

creating Memories

changing through experience.
```

At the end of the absence, the simulation must reconstruct:

```text
THE SAME PERSON
```

rather than:

```text
THE OLD CHARACTER
WITH FOUR YEARS
ADDED TO AGE.
```
