---

# Purpose

The Relationship Engine simulates how individuals influence one another throughout their lives.

Relationships are not static values.

They are living connections that evolve through shared experiences.

The Relationship Engine transforms individual lives into communities, families and societies.

---

# Core Philosophy

No person exists in isolation.

Every meaningful interaction changes someone.

Relationships are therefore one of the primary forces driving the evolution of both individuals and the world itself.

---

# Design Principle

Relationships are never defined by a single value.

Every relationship is a unique combination of emotional, social and historical dimensions.

Two people may trust each other deeply while competing for the same leadership role.

They may love one another while carrying years of unresolved resentment.

Human relationships are complex.

The simulation should reflect that complexity.

---

# Relationship Model

A relationship emerges through:

```
Two Individuals
        ↓
Shared Experiences
        ↓
Emotional Interpretation
        ↓
Relationship Evolution
        ↓
Future Behaviour
```

Relationships continuously influence future choices made by both individuals.

---

# Relationship Dimensions

Relationships are composed of multiple dimensions rather than a single status.

Examples include:

- Trust
- Respect
- Affection
- Loyalty
- Dependency
- Responsibility
- Admiration
- Fear
- Resentment
- Gratitude
- Shared History
- Shared Secrets

The final canonical dimensions will be defined separately.

---

# Shared Experiences

Relationships evolve because people experience life together.

Examples include:

- surviving danger
- betrayal
- cooperation
- sacrifice
- raising children
- shared loss
- solving problems
- conflict
- forgiveness
- success

Experiences matter more than time.

---

# Dynamic Evolution

Relationships never stop changing.

Every meaningful interaction has the potential to strengthen, weaken or redefine the relationship.

Change is gradual.

Major events may accelerate that change.

---

# Memory

Relationships remember.

Important moments remain part of the relationship's history.

Not every interaction is equally significant.

Some moments become defining memories that continue influencing future behaviour for years.

---

# Social Networks

Every person exists within multiple overlapping relationship networks.

Examples include:

- Family
- Friends
- Community
- Occupation
- Faction
- Rivals
- Mentors
- Former relationships

These networks create the social fabric of the Living World.

---

# Relationship Influence

Relationships influence:

- dialogue
- cooperation
- leadership
- conflict
- trade
- trust
- decision making
- emotional resilience
- personal growth

Relationships never replace personality.

They continuously reshape it.

---

# Emergent Narrative

The Relationship Engine does not create stories directly.

Instead it creates conditions from which stories naturally emerge.

A betrayal becomes meaningful because trust existed.

A sacrifice matters because love existed.

A reunion matters because separation mattered first.

Narrative emerges from relationships.

---

# Relationship Lifecycle

Relationships are living systems.

They may:

- begin
- grow
- weaken
- recover
- transform
- end

Even after ending, relationships may continue influencing future behaviour through memory.
