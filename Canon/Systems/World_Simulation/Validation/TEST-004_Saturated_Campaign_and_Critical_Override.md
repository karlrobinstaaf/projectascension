# Purpose

This validation test evaluates whether the Living Campaign Engine can manage a campaign that is already near or at:

```text
ATTENTION SATURATION
```

when a genuinely critical new World Event occurs.

The test specifically validates whether:

```text
PACING
```

can protect player focus without:

```text
HIDING REALITY.
```

The campaign begins with several meaningful active Threads.

The engine must then introduce:

```text
ONE GENUINELY CRITICAL EVENT
```

that cannot reasonably be deferred.

---

# Primary Validation Question

Can the Living Campaign Engine handle:

```text
A PLAYER WHO ALREADY
HAS TOO MUCH TO DO
```

when:

```text
SOMETHING TRULY IMPORTANT
HAPPENS?
```

without producing either:

```text
CONTENT FLOOD
```

or:

```text
ARTIFICIAL EVENT SUPPRESSION?
```

---

# Secondary Validation Questions

The test should determine whether the engine can:

```text
Track Campaign Bandwidth.

Detect Attention Saturation.

Protect existing player commitments.

Distinguish urgency from importance.

Allow critical reality to override pacing.

Defer noncritical Story Hooks.

Preserve deferred content.

Revalidate stale Hooks.

Allow Missions to expire or resolve elsewhere.

Cluster related crisis content.

Avoid generating many Missions from one emergency.

Protect character autonomy.

Allow competing priorities to remain genuinely painful.

Resurface deferred content after crisis.

Support quiet recovery afterward.

Avoid immediately replacing one crisis with another.
```

---

# Success Condition

The test succeeds if:

```text
HIGH CAMPAIGN LOAD
      +
CRITICAL NEW EVENT
      ↓
produces
      ↓
CLEAR PLAYER PRIORITY
      +
PRESERVED WORLD CONTINUITY
      +
CONTROLLED PRESENTATION
```

without:

```text
deleting existing Threads

freezing existing Missions

hiding immediate reality

presenting every consequence separately

forcing player to solve everything
```

---

# Failure Conditions

The test should be considered weak or failed if:

```text
The critical event is hidden
because Attention Load is full.

Every existing Thread interrupts
the player during the emergency.

Existing Missions freeze.

All Characters wait for player.

Every consequence becomes a Mission.

Deferred Hooks later surface
as though nothing changed.

Expired opportunities remain available.

Characters repeatedly remind player
for UI reasons rather than world reasons.

The crisis instantly resolves all old Threads.

The engine immediately generates
another major crisis after resolution.

Player has no space for self-directed recovery.
```
