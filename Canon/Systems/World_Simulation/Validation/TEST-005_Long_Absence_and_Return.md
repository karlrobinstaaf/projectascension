---

# Purpose

This validation test evaluates whether the Living Campaign Engine can preserve believable continuity when the player leaves a familiar region for an extended period.

The test stresses:

```text
Character Autonomy

Character Compression

Campaign Memory

World Ledger

Regional State

Relationship Persistence

Relationship Drift

Character Aging

Character Migration

Character Death

Institutional Evolution

Faction Evolution

Place Memory

Delayed Consequences

Opportunity Expiration

Mission Resolution Without Player

Story Hook Compression

Pacing and Priority

Return Presentation
```

The central requirement is:

```text
THE WORLD MUST KEEP LIVING
WHILE THE PLAYER IS GONE.
```

---

# Primary Validation Question

Can the player leave:

```text
A FAMILIAR REGION
```

for:

```text
TEN YEARS
```

and return to a place where:

```text
PEOPLE AGED

RELATIONSHIPS CHANGED

INSTITUTIONS EVOLVED

OLD PROBLEMS RESOLVED

NEW PROBLEMS APPEARED

PLACES CHANGED

PLAYER ACTIONS MATURED INTO CONSEQUENCES
```

without the region feeling:

```text
RESET
```

or:

```text
FROZEN?
```

---

# Secondary Validation Questions

The test should determine whether the engine can:

```text
Compress off-screen character simulation.

Preserve major life events.

Allow minor characters to become important.

Allow important characters to become less central.

Allow characters to move away.

Allow characters to die naturally.

Preserve player relationships without freezing them.

Allow relationships to weaken through absence.

Allow strong relationships to survive long separation.

Resolve old Missions without player involvement.

Allow opportunities to expire.

Allow new institutions and factions to emerge.

Propagate previous player consequences for years.

Convert old consequences into World Baseline.

Preserve historical causal chains.

Create meaningful Place Memory.

Present ten years of history without exposition overload.

Prioritize what the returning player should learn first.

Allow some changes to remain undiscovered.

Create emotional continuity without forcing nostalgia.

Support characters who remember the player differently.

Allow younger characters to know the player only through stories.

Allow player reputation to persist, fade, or transform.
```

---

# Success Condition

The test succeeds if:

```text
PLAYER DEPARTURE
      ↓
LONG ABSENCE
      ↓
AUTONOMOUS WORLD DEVELOPMENT
      ↓
PLAYER RETURN
      ↓
SELECTIVE DISCOVERY
      ↓
CONTINUITY
```

produces the feeling:

```text
TEN YEARS ACTUALLY PASSED.
```

---

# Failure Conditions

The test should be considered weak or failed if:

```text
Characters remain in identical roles.

Characters wait ten years for player requests.

Old Missions remain unchanged.

Old opportunities remain conveniently available.

No important character moves.

No institutions evolve.

Every relationship remains numerically identical.

Player reputation remains globally frozen.

Every old character immediately recognizes player.

Every regional change is explained in one exposition dump.

The game tells the player all ten years of history instantly.

Nothing important happens without player involvement.

Player-caused consequences stop progressing during absence.

Character death occurs only for dramatic return reveals.

All familiar businesses and locations remain unchanged.

The player's return automatically becomes
the most important regional event.
```
