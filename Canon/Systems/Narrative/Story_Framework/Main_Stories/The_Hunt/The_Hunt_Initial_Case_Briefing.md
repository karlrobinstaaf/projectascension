# 1. Operational Briefing

You have been assigned to a federal investigative team responding to two recent homicides in Ferry County, Washington.

Both victims were recovered near the Republic area.

At first glance, the cases appeared to be isolated local homicides.

They are no longer being treated that way.

Specific characteristics of the recovered victims show significant similarities to an unsolved Florida serial murder investigation from the 1990s.

That offender became known in the press as:

# THE SUNDAY MAN

No offender was ever identified.

No arrest was made.

The murders appeared to stop more than three decades ago.

Until now.

# 4. Historical Overview

## Florida — 1990s

[CONFIRMED]

During the 1990s, multiple law-enforcement agencies in Florida investigated a series of disappearances and homicides involving adults and children.

The cases were not initially believed to be connected.

Victims differed by:

- sex
- age
- location
- occupation
- family background
- socioeconomic status
- ethnicity
- circumstance of disappearance

No conventional victim profile was immediately apparent.

---

# 5. Identified Victims

[CONFIRMED]

Fifteen victims were eventually associated with the series.

They appeared to form three repeating groups of five.

The sequence was not recognized during the earliest cases.

It became apparent only after investigators reconstructed the cases chronologically.

# 10. Clothing
