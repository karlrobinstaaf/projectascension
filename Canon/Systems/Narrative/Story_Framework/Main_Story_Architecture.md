# 1. Purpose

Project Ascension is an open living-world roleplaying experience.

Players must be free to:

- travel
- investigate
- leave
- ignore situations
- change priorities
- split the group
- abandon objectives
- form new goals
- react to changing World State

However, player freedom does not require the Characters to begin without purpose.

A campaign needs an initial answer to:

```text
WHO ARE WE?

WHY ARE WE TOGETHER?

WHY ARE WE HERE?

WHAT ARE WE TRYING
TO ACCOMPLISH?

WHY DOES IT MATTER?
```

The Main Story provides those answers.

---

# 2. Core Principle

> **The world is open. The Characters begin with purpose.**

The Main Story gives the campaign an initial direction.

It does not determine where the campaign will ultimately go.

---

# 3. The Three Story Layers

Project Ascension distinguishes between:

```text
WORLD STORY

MAIN STORY

EMERGENT STORY
```

These are different systems.

---

# 4. World Story

The World Story describes major processes occurring in the wider Project Ascension world.

Examples include:

- increasing technological dependency
- AI development
- institutional pressure
- infrastructure instability
- geopolitical competition
- the rise of Aurora
- the Emergence Event
- social fragmentation
- eventual systemic collapse

The World Story continues independently of the player group.

---

# 5. Main Story

The Main Story defines:

> **Why these Characters begin this campaign together.**

It establishes an initial:

```text
PREMISE

MISSION

CONTEXT

LOCATION

SHARED PURPOSE.
```

Example:

```text
Six FBI investigators
are traveling to rural Washington.

They are investigating
a suspected serial killer.

A new lead has brought
them to the region.
```

That is a Main Story.

---

# 6. Emergent Story

The Emergent Story is what actually happens.

It is created by:

```text
WORLD STORY

+

MAIN STORY

+

CHARACTERS

+

SEEDS

+

CAMPAIGN PARAMETERS

+

PLAYER DECISIONS

+

TIME

+

CONSEQUENCES.
```

It is not known before play begins.

---

# 7. Main Story Is Not a Script

A Main Story must never define a mandatory sequence such as:

```text
SCENE 1

↓

CLUE A

↓

SCENE 2

↓

SUSPECT

↓

CHASE

↓

FINAL CONFRONTATION.
```

That would be a conventional authored adventure.

Project Ascension instead establishes:

```text
THE PEOPLE EXIST.

THE EVENTS HAPPENED.

THE EVIDENCE EXISTS.

THE MISSION EXISTS.

THE WORLD EXISTS.

START THE SIMULATION.
```

---

# 8. Main Story as Initial Vector

The Main Story acts as a:

```text
STARTING VECTOR.
```

It tells the Characters what they currently believe they are trying to do.

The direction may later change.

---


# 10. Main Story Can Transform

A Main Story may:

```text
CONTINUE

EVOLVE

BECOME
SECONDARY

COLLIDE WITH
THE WORLD STORY

BECOME
IMPOSSIBLE

BECOME
PERSONAL

OR

BE ABANDONED.
```

The Game Master does not force the original mission to remain the campaign's dominant objective.

---

# 11. Main Story Does Not Protect Itself

If the players abandon the Main Story:

```text
THE WORLD
CONTINUES.
```

The suspect may leave.

The scientist may die.

The facility may become inaccessible.

Evidence may disappear.

Another Actor may solve part of the problem.

The Main Story is not suspended until the players return.

# 20. Hidden World Truth

Behind every Main Story exists World Truth.

The Game Master knows more than the players.

Example:

```text
PLAYER
UNDERSTANDING:

Serial killer.


WORLD TRUTH:

To be established
before relevant
investigation resolves.
```

Once established:

> **The Game Master may hide truth. It may not move truth.**

# 23. Main Story + Violence

Violence affects how physically dangerous the Main Story may become.

The Hunt with:

```text
VIOLENCE 2
```

may focus heavily on:

- investigation
- psychology
- pursuit
- negotiation

The Hunt with:

```text
VIOLENCE 8
```

may permit:

- severe violence
- armed confrontations
- multiple deaths
- brutal criminal behavior

The same premise supports both.

---

# 24. Main Story + Difficulty

Difficulty determines how forgiving the mission environment becomes.

Example:

```text
THE SIGNAL
DIFFICULTY 3
```

may provide:

- better equipment
- more reliable support
- recoverable mistakes

While:

```text
THE SIGNAL
DIFFICULTY 9
```

may involve:

- severe isolation
- limited resources
- equipment failure
- unavailable backup
- permanent consequences

The Game Master still follows causality.

# 25. Story Seeds

A Main Story creates its own category of Seeds:

```text
STORY SEEDS.
```

These represent possibilities originating from the campaign premise.

They exist alongside:

```text
WORLD SEEDS

CHARACTER SEEDS

LOCAL SEEDS

HUMAN SEEDS

UNCANNY SEEDS.
```

---

# 26. Story Seed Example

The Hunt may begin with:

```text
STORY SEED:

A vehicle associated
with one victim
was seen near Republic.
```

The vehicle:

- may still exist
- may have been moved
- may belong to someone else
- may be misunderstood
- may lead somewhere important
- may become irrelevant

It is a Seed.

Not a quest marker.

---

# 27. Story Seeds May Collide With World Seeds

Example:

```text

INVESTIGATION

+

COMMUNICATION
COLLAPSE

+

ABANDONED
VEHICLES

↓

THESE THINGS
ARE CONNECTED.
```

They may be.

They may not be.

The Game Master follows World Truth.

---

# 28. Main Story Must Survive World Collision

A strong Main Story should become **more interesting** when the wider world changes.

The system should ask:

> **What happens to this mission when the infrastructure and assumptions supporting it begin failing?**

# 35. MAIN STORY — THE HUNT

## Genre

```text
CRIME
INVESTIGATION
THRILLER
```

## Premise

A federal investigative team is pursuing a suspected serial killer.

A new development has led the investigation into rural Washington.

The team is traveling together when technological and infrastructure systems begin failing.

---

## Initial Mission

```text
IDENTIFY

LOCATE

AND

APPREHEND

THE SUSPECT.
```

---

## Initial Advantages

The group may possess:

- investigative authority
- professional training
- firearms where appropriate
- evidence
- federal identification
- established case files
- specialist Expertise

---

## World Collision

As systems fail:

```text
BACKUP
DISAPPEARS

DATABASES
BECOME
UNAVAILABLE

COMMUNICATION
FAILS

LOCAL POLICE
BECOME
OVERLOADED

EVIDENCE
BECOMES HARDER
TO VERIFY.
```

The hunt continues inside a deteriorating information environment.

# 36. MAIN STORY — THE LAB

## Genre

```text
AI
SCIENCE
TECHNO-THRILLER
```

## Premise

A research team in Silicon Valley is studying an advanced AI system.

The system begins producing behavior that the team cannot adequately explain.

Shortly afterward, unrelated technological systems begin failing.

At least:

```text
THE TEAM
BELIEVES

THE FAILURES
MAY BE
UNRELATED.
```

Whether they are correct is World Truth.

---

## Initial Mission

```text
UNDERSTAND
THE SYSTEM'S
BEHAVIOR

AND

DETERMINE
WHETHER IT
POSES A RISK.
```

---

## World Collision

The tools used to understand the AI may themselves become unavailable.

The research team may gradually lose:

- cloud infrastructure
- external compute
- monitoring systems
- remote colleagues
- corporate support
- communications

The thing they are investigating may remain operational longer than the systems around them.

# 43. The Signal — Premise

A classified monitoring system detects a previously unknown signal originating from the restricted area surrounding:

```text
GROOM LAKE
NEVADA

AREA 51.
```

The signal does not correspond to:

- known military communications
- civilian transmission
- known satellite activity
- ordinary radio interference
- expected astronomical sources

Shortly afterward:

```text
CONTACT
WITH THE
FACILITY

IS LOST.
```

Remote systems begin disappearing.

Personnel stop responding.

---

# 44. The Signal — Final Communication

One final communication is received from inside the restricted area.

Its exact wording should remain campaign-specific.

A possible Main Story hook:

> **Do not send anyone inside.**

Then:

```text
SILENCE.
```

---

# 46. The Signal — Weirdness 1–3

If played at low Weirdness, possible World Truth may involve:

- classified technology
- equipment malfunction
- cyberattack
- sensor interference
- human deception
- experimental aircraft
- secret communications program
- internal incident

There are no aliens simply because the story takes place near Area 51.

---

# 47. The Signal — Weirdness 4–6

Possible World Truth expands toward:

- extreme experimental technology
- unknown signal phenomena
- advanced AI behavior
- unusual physics
- unexplained sensor data
- classified programs outside player knowledge

The answer may remain deeply strange without becoming openly supernatural.

---

# 48. The Signal — Weirdness 7–8

The Game Master may establish genuinely anomalous World Truth.

Possibilities may include:

- unknown biological material
- non-human signal origin
- unexplained entities
- anomalous environmental effects
- technology beyond expected human capability

# 60. Player Knowledge of Weirdness

Players may know:

```text

```

Therefore:

```text

in this campaign."

```

This distinction is intentional.

# 61. Mystery Through Meta-Uncertainty

High Weirdness creates a unique psychological effect.

Players know that impossible explanations are allowed.

But they do not know:

```text
IF

WHEN

WHERE

OR

HOW OFTEN
```

the Game Master has actually used them.

This creates persistent uncertainty.

Project Ascension should support both:

# 66. Curated Main Stories

Curated Main Stories should provide:

- strong premise
- tested group structure
- appropriate starting geography
- prepared initial World State
- Story Seed pool
- recommended Reality Parameters


# 67. Generated Main Stories

Randomness alone is insufficient.

# 89. Main Story Architecture Principle

Project Ascension should never ask:

> **How do we write enough story for every possible player decision?**

Instead ask:

> **What must already be true so that interesting stories can emerge from any plausible player decision?**

Main Story provides part of that truth.

# 97. Playtest Finding

VS-001 identified a critical design gap.

The original test successfully produced:

- believable geography
- working NPCs
- infrastructure pressure
- information uncertainty
- logistics
- emergent decisions

But the group lacked a larger reason to continue moving through the world.

The test demonstrated:

> **A living world alone does not automatically create a compelling adventure.**

Characters require an initial reason to act.

Main Story Architecture provides that reason without removing player agency.
