# Purpose

This document describes how the Project Ascension repository is organized.

Its purpose is to make the project easy to understand, maintain and expand over many years.

The structure should support both human creators and future AI-assisted development.

---

# Design Philosophy

Project Ascension is built in layers.

Each layer depends upon the previous one.

New content should always be created on top of an existing foundation rather than introducing isolated ideas.

The project grows from principles to worldbuilding, from worldbuilding to civilizations, from civilizations to people, and finally to stories.

---

# Repository Structure

## Foundation

The creative identity of Project Ascension.

Contains the project's vision, philosophy, design principles and creative frameworks.

Examples:

- Universe Bible
- Vision
- Narrative Philosophy
- Creative Pillars
- Plausibility Framework
- Design Decisions

---

## World

Describes how the world functions.

Contains:

- World States
- The Collapse
- The Emergence Event
- Aurora
- Settlements
- Geography
- Factions

The World folder explains how civilization changes over time.

---

## Humanity

Explores humanity itself.

Not through biology.

Through psychology, society, relationships, trust, hope and culture.

This layer explains why people behave as they do.

---

## Canon

The permanent truth of the universe.

Anything inside Canon is considered established unless officially revised.

Canon changes rarely.

---

## Living Campaign Engine

Defines how the world continues evolving during gameplay.

Contains systems rather than stories.

Examples include:

- Dynamic settlements
- Reputation
- Economy
- Politics
- AI Game Master logic
- World evolution

---

## Characters

Everything related to people.

NPCs.

Companions.

Major historical figures.

Character templates.

Behaviour.

Relationships.

---

## Locations

Cities.

Settlements.

Regions.

Landmarks.

Infrastructure.

Every location should connect back to both World and Humanity.

---

## Story

Narrative content.

Campaigns.

Quests.

Encounters.

Dialogue.

Story content should always emerge from the world rather than exist independently.

---

## Academy

The learning area.

Documents explaining how Project Ascension is built.

Examples:

- Writing Guidelines
- AI Collaboration
- Worldbuilding Lessons
- Design Tutorials
- Programming Notes

The Academy teaches the process behind the project.

---

# Dependency Order

Creative work should generally follow this order:

Foundation

↓

Humanity

↓

World

↓

Settlements

↓

Characters

↓

Stories

↓

Gameplay

---

# Living Documentation

Project Ascension is designed as a living project.

Every document should evolve as the universe grows.

Documentation should never become static.

Every revision should make the universe clearer rather than larger.

---

# Guiding Question

Before creating a new document, ask:

**Does this belong in an existing document, or does it introduce an entirely new concept?**

The project should remain organized through clarity rather than quantity.

---

# Final Statement

A well-organized project allows creativity to grow without creating chaos.

Project Ascension is designed to evolve for many years.

Its structure should make future expansion feel natural rather than overwhelming.
